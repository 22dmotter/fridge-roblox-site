How to Win EVERY Race in Steal an Egg: Easy Race Tips

By Fridge · Published October 13, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

The Steal an Egg race event turned the egg-stealing game into a kart racer, and winning first place is the only way to unlock the best racer pets. This guide covers how to win races in Steal an Egg: when the race portal opens, how to drive clean laps, what every item does, and how to use them like a pro. These are the tips Fridge uses in his "win every race" video.

Event status: The racing event launched with the October 10 mount update and was a limited-time event that ran into the Halloween update. Races also came back as a quest during the Roblox Hollow event ("win three races"). If races return in a future update, every tip here still applies. Check the in-game event menu for the current schedule.

When and Where Races Happen

  • Every 30 minutes, the game announces that a race has appeared, and a portal opens in the Abyss Ocean biome.
  • Get in fast. The portal stays open for about a minute. You don't need a mount or any Speed to enter.
  • Six racers per race. Empty slots fill with bots, and the bots use items aggressively.
  • Three laps, usually two to three minutes total.

Server hopping doesn't get you more races, because they run on the same schedule everywhere.

Does Your Speed Stat Matter in Races?

No. Everyone drives at the same base speed, no matter how much Speed you've trained. Races are decided by boost pads, items and clean driving lines. That's great news for new players: a fresh account can beat a leaderboard account. Your racer pet doesn't change top speed either, but they handle differently. Slim racers like the Seahorse and Bird are easier to steer, while the wide Lizard bounces off other racers.

How to Drive a Perfect Lap

  1. Hit every green boost pad. There are three boost arrows per lap. Missing one is the most common way to lose the lead, and the first one right after the start sets up your whole race.
  2. Stay in the middle of the track. Corners and walls slow you down, especially on mobile.
  3. Jump over hazards. You can hop over dropped eggs and the blasts on the loops.
  4. Watch for red markings. Anything marked red on the ground will knock you out of your stride.
  5. Practice after the finish. You can keep driving after you cross the line. Use that time to learn where the boost pads are.

Every Race Item and How to Use It

Item boxes are scattered around each lap. You hold one item at a time and press E to use it.

Item Strategy: Leading vs. Chasing

When you're in first

Hold a shield instead of firing items the second you get them. A Spike does little when nobody's ahead of you, so drop it behind you instead. Place Egg Splatters right around boost pads where the racers behind you have to drive.

When you're behind

Use everything immediately and grab the next box. Items are your catch-up mechanic, and holding them while you're in fourth wastes them.

Race Rewards: Trophies and Racer Pets

Every finisher earns trophies, with more for better places (roughly 2,500 for first). You only get them if you cross the line. Top finishers can also win an Ostrich pet. Trophies are spent in the race shop on things like the Nitro mutation, the Racing Bat, 1.25x speed and 2x treadmill boosters.

First-place wins unlock new racers, each of which also comes as a pet egg:

Keep Improving Outside the Races

Races don't care about your Speed stat, but the rest of the game does. Use our guide on how to get speed in Steal an Egg between races, check the latest on Steal an Egg codes, and see how racing tied into the Roblox Hollow event.

Steal an Egg FAQ

How often do races happen in Steal an Egg?

During the racing event, a race portal opened every 30 minutes in the Abyss Ocean biome and stayed open for about a minute.

Does speed matter in Steal an Egg races?

No. Everyone drives at the same base speed. Races are won with boost pads, items and clean lines, so new players can win too.

What is the best item in Steal an Egg races?

The Royal Sky Whale is the rarest and strongest, giving a speed burst of about three and a half seconds. The Scrambled Shield is the best defensive item when leading.

How do you unlock the Engine Snail in Steal an Egg?

Win 30 races in first place. Only first-place finishes count toward racer unlocks.
