How to Redeem Steal an Egg Codes (When They Arrive)

Most Roblox games put codes in the same places, so here's the routine to follow the day a code system goes live:

  1. Launch Steal an Egg from Roblox and wait for your base to load.
  2. Look for a Codes, Gift, or Settings button on the left or top of the screen. Most games hide the code box in one of these.
  3. Type the code exactly as written. Some Roblox games treat codes as case-sensitive, so copy capital letters carefully.
  4. Press Redeem and check the notification in the corner for your reward.
  5. If it says invalid, the code has expired or was mistyped. Try once more, then move on.

Free Steal an Egg Rewards You Can Claim Without Codes

Codes might be missing, but free stuff isn't. These rewards give you more than a starter code usually would:

1. The free rewards panel (big Speed boost)

Steal an Egg has a free rewards section that pays out a large Speed gift for liking the game, favoriting it, and joining the creator group. Code trackers list the payout at around 10,000 Speed in total. That's a huge jump at the very start, when every biome gate feels impossible. Claim it on your first session in case a future update removes the offer.

2. Pet Index rewards

Every new pet you discover unlocks a claimable reward in the Index. During your first hour these stack up quickly, so open the Index after each hatch and collect everything waiting there.

3. Limited-time events

The developers run regular events instead of codes. The Halloween Dimension brought Candy Corn farming and the Phantom Express Ticket quest, and the racing event gave away pets and trophies. Admin Abuse events also hand out massive temporary Speed boosts. Join the official Discord linked on the game page so you get pinged when one starts.

The Real "Code": Getting Speed Fast

Most players searching for codes really want one thing: more Speed. Speed gates every biome, from Lake at 900 Speed all the way to the billions for late-game areas. Here's the short version of our full Steal an Egg speed guide:

  • Unlock and upgrade the treadmill. It opens after a $1,000 garden upgrade and is your only unlimited Speed source.
  • Buy the best trail you can afford before training. Trails multiply what the treadmill gives you, so a better trail makes every minute of training worth more.
  • Train during night cycles. You can't steal eggs at night, so that downtime is perfect treadmill time.
  • Steal from the deepest biome you can escape. Better eggs hatch better pets, which earn more cash for more treadmill upgrades.

Avoid Fake Codes and "Free Speed" Scams

Because there are no real codes, scams fill the gap. Watch out for:

  • Sites asking for your Roblox password to "unlock" a code. No legit code ever needs your login.
  • The Wi-Fi cut trick. The rumor that disconnecting mid-purchase gives you a free Speed gamepass doesn't work. The Robux still leaves your account.
  • Scripts and exploits. They can get your account banned and often contain malware.

Stick to the free rewards panel, the treadmill, and events. You'll progress faster and keep your account safe. And if you play other Roblox games, our Anime Dice codes and Huzz Valley codes pages do have real, working codes right now.
