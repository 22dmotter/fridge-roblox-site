Catalog Avatar Creator Codes (November 2026): None Confirmed + Free Items

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Looking for Catalog Avatar Creator codes? As of last checked November 11, 2026, no code is confirmed working. The only recent code, DAPPER, has mixed reports, and the official game page and community don't mention any code. Codes in CAC are typed into chat with /redeem, not into a menu. Catalog Avatar Creator by Muneeb was the #19 most-played game on Roblox on November 11, with about 54,000 players online.

How to Redeem Catalog Avatar Creator Codes

  1. Open Catalog Avatar Creator on Roblox.
  2. Tap the chat bubble in the top-left corner.
  3. Type /redeem, a space and the code, for example /redeem DAPPER.
  4. Press Enter. If nothing happens, the code is expired or mistyped.
Chat turned off? Because codes go through chat, you can't redeem them if chat is disabled on your account. Parents can review chat settings in our Roblox parental controls guide.

What's Free in CAC

According to the game page and official community, nearly everything in Catalog Avatar Creator is free to use. You can try on hats, hair, limiteds, bundles and animation packs, browse more than 50 million community outfits and save avatars you make. You only spend Robux when you buy an item to keep, and it goes to your normal Roblox inventory. The game also sells Sponsor Tokens and one-session items like the Big Avatar Potion (29 Robux), but you don't need any of them.

Catalog Avatar Creator FAQ

Are there any working Catalog Avatar Creator promo codes?

None are confirmed as of November 11, 2026. DAPPER is disputed: some trackers list it as active, others as expired.

How do you redeem codes in Catalog Avatar Creator?

Open chat in the game, type /redeem followed by a space and the code, then press Enter.

Does Catalog Avatar Creator give free Robux?

No. No code in the game has ever been reported to give Robux.

How do I get free items in Catalog Avatar Creator?

Try items on for free, then claim 0-Robux Roblox items like Pal Hair or the Roblox Visor from the Marketplace.

Is the Catalog Avatar Creator Backpack still free?

No. All free copies are gone; it can only be bought from resellers now.

What does CAC stand for?

CAC is short for Catalog Avatar Creator, the avatar try-on game by Muneeb on Roblox.

Who made Catalog Avatar Creator?

The verified developer Muneeb, according to the Roblox game page.
