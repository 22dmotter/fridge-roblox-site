Roblox Fisch

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Fisch is a Roblox fishing adventure published by the Fisching group. The official Roblox game page describes it as a fishing adventure focused on exploration and progression, with many possible fish variations. The Official Fisch Wiki likewise calls it an open-world adventure and exploration fishing game.

Play the official experience on Roblox: Fisch. This page is the Fisch hub on Roblox Guides.
