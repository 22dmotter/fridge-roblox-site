How to Get the Prismatic Pickaxe in The Forge: Iceberg Location and Stats

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

The Prismatic Pickaxe was the best pickaxe in The Forge when the Winter Expansion launched on December 25, 2025, and Fridge posted three guides to it that day. Here's how to get the Prismatic Pickaxe: where it is, what you need, and what it costs now.

Watch Fridge's Prismatic Pickaxe Videos

Fridge covered this topic in 1 full video and 2 Shorts uploaded on December 25, 2025. They are all embedded here, so you can watch and follow the written steps side by side.

The Shorts play in a vertical player. Tap one to watch it here, or open it on YouTube.

Video date check: The Forge gets updates often. The newest is version 1.09, Halloween & Guilds, from October 20, 2026. Fridge's videos show the game as it was when he filmed them. The written steps on this page match the game as of November 11, 2026, and were checked against the Forge Wiki.

Where the Prismatic Pickaxe Is

The Forge Wiki says it's in The Iceberg on the Frostspire Expanse, "under the frozen lake" behind the giant iceberg that holds the Mosasaursit. Pro Game Guides gives the route:

  1. Travel to the Frostspire Expanse (level 70+).
  2. Go up to The Peak cave entrance, but don't go inside.
  3. Keep going left to the edge of the mountain until you see a hidden frozen lake with a hole in the ice.
  4. Jump through the hole and break the barrier. Pro Game Guides says you need at least a Frost Pickaxe.
  5. Mine the Mosasaursit inside, then claim the pickaxe.

The Mosasaursit Requirement

To stop players skipping the iceberg, you must have at least 1 Mosasaursit in your inventory to get the pickaxe, according to the Forge Wiki. Mosasaursit is an Exotic ore, a fossilized sea creature. It gives armor +35% max Health but -25% Movement Speed, so keep one even if you don't plan to use it.

The Forge FAQ

Where is the Prismatic Pickaxe in The Forge?

In The Iceberg under a frozen lake to the left of The Peak's cave entrance on the Frostspire Expanse.

How much is the Prismatic Pickaxe?

1,500,000 Gold, or 1,320,000 with the Goblin race, on the Forge Wiki as of November 11, 2026.

Why can't I get the Prismatic Pickaxe?

You need at least 1 Mosasaursit in your inventory, and a Frost Pickaxe or better to break the barrier.
