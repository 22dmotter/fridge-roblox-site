Anime Adventures Codes (November 2026): Free Gems and Star Remnants

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Here are all the working Anime Adventures codes for November 2026, last checked November 11, 2026. There are 5 working codes, including the new 2MILLIONFAVES and the two Update 21 launch codes. Most of them need you to be level 10 or 20, so read the requirements before you type them in. Anime Adventures by Gomu had about 99,000 players online on the morning of November 11 according to the official Roblox games API.

Level Requirements Explained

If a code says it can't be redeemed, check your level first. Four of the five codes are locked behind a level: AACOMEBACK needs level 10, and 2MILLIONFAVES, SUMMERCOMEBACK and APRILFOOLS need level 20. New players should play story stages until level 20 and then come back to claim the full set. CELESTIALCOMPASSU21 has no level gate but only works once per account.

How to Redeem Anime Adventures Codes

  1. Launch Anime Adventures on Roblox and wait in the lobby.
  2. Walk to the Codes circle near the spawn area.
  3. Stand inside the circle so the code box opens.
  4. Type or paste a code into the Enter Code box. Fossbytes says codes are case-sensitive, so copy them exactly.
  5. Press Redeem. Rewards go straight to your inventory.
Tip: redeem codes one at a time and wait for the confirmation message before trying the next one.

What to Spend Your Free Rewards On

  • Gems: summon new units from the banner. Save up rather than rolling one at a time.
  • Star Remnants: Gaming Route suggests using them on trait rerolls for your best Mythic or Secret unit. Each reroll costs 5.
  • Celestial Compass: an Update 21 item, so check the new Update 21 content before you use it.

Codes That May Not Work

Roblox Den still marks CLOVER2 and MERRYCHRISTMAS as "check", but Destructoid and Radio Times list both as expired, so we don't count them. CLOUD (250 Gems) is in the same situation. Try them if you like, but expect an error.

Expired Anime Adventures Codes

These no longer work according to at least two sources: UNBREAKABLE, UPDATESOON5189, DUNGEONS, MILLIONFAVES, 2BILLIONAA, UPDATE20RELEASE, UPD20INCOMING, ASSASSIN, UPDSOON, HOLIDAYS2024, MERRYCHRISTMAS2, SHUTDOWNCODE1230 and RELEASE. Destructoid's archive has dozens more older codes.

Anime Adventures FAQ

What are the working Anime Adventures codes?

As of November 11, 2026: 2MILLIONFAVES, SUMMERCOMEBACK, CELESTIALCOMPASSU21, AACOMEBACK and APRILFOOLS.

Why does my Anime Adventures code not work?

You may be below the level requirement (level 10 or 20), you may have already used it, or it may be mistyped. Codes are case-sensitive.

Where do you redeem codes in Anime Adventures?

Walk into the Codes circle near the spawn area in the lobby, type the code and press Redeem.

What is the newest Anime Adventures code?

2MILLIONFAVES, added November 8, 2026. It gives a Star Remnant and 2,000 Gems at level 20+.

Are there Update 21 codes?

Yes. SUMMERCOMEBACK and CELESTIALCOMPASSU21 launched with Update 21 on November 1, 2026.

Does CELESTIALCOMPASSU21 have extra requirements?

Roblox Den and RBLXSTAT say you must like and favorite the game and join the Gomu Roblox community before it redeems. It works once per account.
