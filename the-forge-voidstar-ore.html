Voidstar in The Forge: Stats and Trait

Last checked: November 15, 2026

Voidstar is a Legendary ore on the official The Peak list. Sources agree it is obtained from Medium Ice Crystal, Large Ice Crystal, Floating Crystal, with a $187.50 sell price. They do not agree on the drop chance and multiplier, so those fields are left blank on this page. Its confirmed trait is Increases Critical Chance by 33.3%; Increases Critical Damage by 15%; Decreases max Health by 15% (Weapons).

How to Get Voidstar

On the official Forge Wiki list, Voidstar is a The Peak ore, obtained from Medium Ice Crystal, Large Ice Crystal, Floating Crystal. the official Forge Wiki, Game8, BloxInformer, Destructoid, and Pocket Tactics all mention this ore. The official wiki item page also lists raven cave. This page follows the official wiki list for the location label (The Peak). Sources do not agree on the drop chance (see below). The official item page also lists Raven Cave (Heart of the Island). This page follows the official wiki list for the location label (The Peak).

What Voidstar Is Good For

Voidstar's confirmed trait is Increases Critical Chance by 33.3%; Increases Critical Damage by 15%; Decreases max Health by 15% (Weapons). It has a $187.50 sell price. The multiplier is left blank because sources do not agree. See the Island 3 / Frostspire Expanse guide and the forging guide for how later World 3 ores fit the crafting loop.
