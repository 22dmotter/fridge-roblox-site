How to Redeem a Roblox Gift Card in 2026 (Web, Mobile, Console)

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

This guide shows how to redeem a Roblox gift card the official way in 2026. Most cards are redeemed in a web browser at roblox.com/redeem, and the value usually lands as Roblox Credit that you then turn into Robux or a Roblox Plus (formerly Premium) subscription. The steps below follow Roblox Support.

How to Redeem a Roblox Gift Card (Step by Step)

  1. Open roblox.com/redeem in a web browser on a computer or phone.
  2. Log in to the account that should get the value. Double-check the username.
  3. Scratch off the back of a physical card, or copy the PIN from your digital card email.
  4. Type the PIN into the box and click Redeem.
  5. A success message shows the amount added. Redeem cards one at a time.

Turn Roblox Credit into Robux

  1. Stay on the website (Credit only works in a browser).
  2. Click Get Robux and choose a package. You'll see Roblox Credit as a payment option, or use Convert Credit to Robux on the Redeem page.
  3. Or use Credit to subscribe to Roblox Plus (formerly Roblox Premium) on the website.
  4. Check your Credit balance on the Redeem page or in Billing Settings.
Heads up: Roblox Support says you can't split one purchase between Credit and another payment method. If your balance is short, pick a smaller package.

How to Redeem a Gift Card on Mobile, Xbox and PS5

  • iPhone/iPad (US): Roblox Support says US iOS users can redeem in the app.
  • Samsung Galaxy app: has a Redeem option in the app.
  • Other phones: open roblox.com/redeem in your phone's browser instead of the app.
  • Xbox and PlayStation: consoles can't redeem Roblox cards. Redeem in any browser on the same account; Robux you buy then shows up on console.

Gift Card Not Working? Fixes

  1. Check for look-alike characters. Roblox suggests swapping 0↔O, 1↔I, 2↔Z, 5↔S, 6↔G or Q, and 8↔B.
  2. Remove spaces and make sure you typed the PIN, not the card's serial number.
  3. Confirm the account. The value goes to whoever is logged in and can't be moved to another account.
  4. Card not activated: if the store didn't activate it, contact the retailer with your receipt.
  5. Still failing: contact Roblox Support with a photo of the card and receipt.
Scam warning: never share a gift card PIN with anyone, "free Robux" sites or people claiming to be Roblox staff. Gift cards aren't refundable. If someone took over your account, use our Roblox account recovery guide, and check for violations with our Roblox account status guide.

For Parents

A gift card is a safe way to give Robux without a stored card. You can still set a monthly spending limit; see the Roblox parental controls guide. Kids can then spend Robux on avatar items. Here's how to equip multiple accessories on Roblox.

Quick Checklist Before You Redeem

  1. Sign in to the right account (check the username at the top of the page).
  2. Use a web browser at roblox.com/redeem, not a console.
  3. Type the PIN carefully, swapping look-alike characters if it fails.
  4. Buying a card? Our buyer's guide covers where to buy, value and scams.
  5. Decide whether you want Robux or Roblox Plus before you spend the Credit.

Roblox FAQ

How do you redeem a Gift card?

Go to roblox.com/redeem in a web browser, log in, enter the PIN from the card and click Redeem. The value is added as Roblox Credit or Robux.

Can you redeem a Gift card on Xbox or PS5?

No. Redeem it in a web browser on the same account; any Robux you buy then appears on console.

Why is my Gift card PIN invalid?

Check for look-alike characters such as 0 and O or 1 and I, remove spaces, and make sure the store activated the card.

How do I turn Roblox Credit into Robux?

On the Roblox website, click Get Robux and pay with Roblox Credit, or use Convert Credit to Robux on the Redeem page.

Can I use a Gift card from another country?

Yes. Roblox says gift cards bought in any country can be redeemed on any account.
