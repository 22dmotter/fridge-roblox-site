How to Block and Parry in The Forge, and How to Beat the Bosses

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Most deaths in The Forge come from taking hits you could have stopped. Fridge's Shorts show how to block and parry in The Forge and how to handle bosses. Here are the controls and timing tips, checked against two combat guides and the Forge Wiki.

Watch Fridge's Combat Shorts

Fridge covered this topic in 2 Shorts uploaded between December 19, 2025 and December 29, 2025. They are all embedded here, so you can watch and follow the written steps side by side.

The Shorts play in a vertical player. Tap one to watch it here, or open it on YouTube.

Video date check: The Forge gets updates often. The newest is version 1.09, Halloween & Guilds, from October 20, 2026. Fridge's videos show the game as it was when he filmed them. The written steps on this page match the game as of November 11, 2026, and were checked against the Forge Wiki.

How to Block

  1. Equip a weapon, not your pickaxe. Blocking and parrying only work with a weapon out.
  2. On PC, hold F as an enemy attacks. On mobile, press the shield button.
  3. Your character takes a blocking stance and takes less damage.

The Spike's controls table notes that block needs Shift-Lock (Ctrl) turned on, and that holding block too long drains Stamina.

How to Parry

A parry is a perfectly timed block. Press F (or the shield) at the exact moment the enemy's attack connects. If you time it right, the hit does no damage and the enemy is briefly stunned, which gives you a free counterattack. Both esports.gg and The Spike describe the same timing.

  • Practice on heavy hitters. Esports.gg recommends slow, heavy-weapon enemies like the Elite Zombie, Deathaxe Skeleton, Elite Deathaxe Skeleton and Reaper, because their wind-ups are easy to read.
  • Watch for red particles. Slimes and Blazing Slimes glow red just before they attack, which is your parry cue.
  • Felynx players get a passive 20% chance to parry the next attack.

Once you can block and parry in The Forge reliably, dash through the attacks you can't block. Fast enemies and big slams are usually better dodged than parried.

How to Fight Bosses

The Golem was the first boss added. It's an Ice Titan at The Peak on Island 3, reached through the Colossal Gate, which opens from XX:00 to XX:15 and XX:30 to XX:45. Its 25,000 HP scales with party size, and it has four attacks, according to the Forge Wiki. Other boss-style fights include the Yeti at The Peak, the Prismarine Spider in the Frost Nest, and the Demonic Queen Spider for Raven 3.

  • Learn one attack pattern at a time and parry only the moves you can read.
  • Bring Health Potions. Health Potion III restores 500 HP over 5 seconds.
  • Tanky races help. Golem gives +30% Health, and Dragonborn gives +20% Health and +12% damage. See our races guide.
  • Upgrade your armor first with our armor guide.

The Forge FAQ

How do you parry in The Forge?

With a weapon equipped, press F on PC, or the shield button on mobile, right as the enemy's attack lands.

Why can't I block in The Forge?

You're probably holding your pickaxe. Equip a weapon, and on PC turn on Shift-Lock.

When does the Golem boss spawn?

The Colossal Gate at The Peak opens from XX:00 to XX:15 and from XX:30 to XX:45 each hour.
