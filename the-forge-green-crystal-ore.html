Green Crystal Ore in The Forge: Drop Chance and Price

Last checked: November 15, 2026

Green Crystal Ore is an Epic ore on the official Goblin Cave list. Sources agree it is obtained from Light Crystal, Earth Crystal, Violet Crystal, Crimson Crystal, Cyan Crystal, with a 1-in-255 drop chance, a 3.2x multiplier, and a $48 sell price. It has no confirmed forge trait.

How to Get Green Crystal Ore

On the official Forge Wiki list, Green Crystal Ore is a Goblin Cave ore, obtained from Light Crystal, Earth Crystal, Violet Crystal, Crimson Crystal, Cyan Crystal. the official Forge Wiki, Game8, BloxInformer, Destructoid, and Pocket Tactics all mention this ore. Bloxinformer places it under how to craft. This page follows the official wiki list for the location label (Goblin Cave). The confirmed drop chance on this page is 1-in-255.

What Green Crystal Ore Is Good For

Green Crystal Ore has no confirmed forge trait. It has a 3.2x multiplier and a $48 sell price. See the Goblin Cave guide and the forging guide for how these ores fit the crafting loop.
