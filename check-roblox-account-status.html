How to Check Your Roblox Account Status and Violations (2026)

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

This guide shows how to check your Roblox account status in 2026. Roblox has been rolling out an Account Status page (part of its Safety Dashboard) that shows your moderation standing and recent violations. If your account doesn't have it yet, the older Violations & Appeals page does a similar job. Checking is private, free and never affects your record.

What Roblox Account Status Shows

Parts of the Account Status page
SectionWhat it tells you
Status meterA broad view of your standing, from "All good" toward worse levels
Recent violationsRecent moderation actions, with dates and appeal results
Current timeoutsAny active restriction and how long is left
Community tipsGeneral reminders about the rules (not personal accusations)

Roblox doesn't publish an exact formula for the meter. Treat it as a summary, not a strike counter.

How to Check Account Status on Roblox

  1. Sign in to the account you want to check in a web browser.
  2. Click the gear icon at the top right.
  3. Choose Help and Safety, then Account Status, if it appears. You can also go to roblox.com/safety-dashboard directly.
  4. If it asks you to log in again, sign in in the same browser and reopen the page.

Step 2: Read Your Standing and Violations

  • "All good" means no recent problems are showing.
  • Recent violations lists what Roblox actioned and when. "Appeal accepted" means Roblox reversed that action.
  • An empty violations list means nothing recent, not a lifetime clean record.
  • Roblox says standing can improve over time when you follow the rules.

No Account Status? Use Violations & Appeals

The new page is rolling out gradually. If you don't see it, open roblox.com/support/violations/appeals while signed in. Roblox Support says eligible violations appear there with an Appeal option, and your moderation notification will link to it when your case qualifies.

How to Appeal a Roblox Ban or Warning

  1. Read the moderation notice. It tells you whether to appeal through Violations & Appeals or the Support form.
  2. Violations & Appeals: select the action, choose Appeal and explain briefly why it should be reviewed.
  3. Support form: if the action isn't listed, use the Roblox Support form. Include your username and a short reason (plus the asset ID and link for content appeals).
  4. Meet the deadline: appeals must be filed within 30 days of the action (EU users get 6 months).
  5. Appeal once. Roblox won't review duplicate appeals for the same action, and only the account owner can appeal.
Banned from just one game? That's the game creator's ban, not a Roblox account action, so it won't show on Account Status. Contact that game's developer.

What Doesn't Change Your Standing

  • Opening or refreshing Account Status.
  • Reinstalling Roblox, clearing cache or switching devices.
  • Paid "ban checkers" or "unban services." These are scams; never give anyone your password.
Avoid new violations: exploits, modified clients and bypassing Roblox systems can lead to bans. Our running multiple Roblox accounts guide explains which tools carry ban risk.

If someone else got into your account and broke the rules, start with our Roblox account recovery guide. Leaving for good instead? See how to delete a Roblox account. Lost voice chat after a violation? Our voice chat guide explains the rules.

Tips to Keep Your Account in Good Standing

  • Read the Roblox Community Standards once. Most violations come from chat, usernames and avatar items.
  • Turn on 2-step verification so nobody else can break rules on your account.
  • Check your account status after any warning so you know exactly what was actioned.

Roblox FAQ

How do I check my account status on Roblox?

Sign in on a browser, click the gear icon, choose Help and Safety and then Account Status, or go to roblox.com/safety-dashboard. If it isn't available, use roblox.com/support/violations/appeals.

Does checking Roblox Account Status get me in trouble?

No. Opening or refreshing the page is private and doesn't change your record.

How long do I have to appeal a Roblox ban?

30 days from the moderation action, or 6 months for users in the European Union.

Can someone else check my Roblox account status?

No. There's no official public lookup; the page requires you to be signed in to the account.

Where is Account Status in Roblox settings?

On the website, click the gear icon, choose Help and Safety and then Account Status. It's rolling out gradually, so some accounts only see Violations & Appeals.
