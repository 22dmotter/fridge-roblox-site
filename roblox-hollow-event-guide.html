Roblox Hollow Event 2026: Full Guide, Confirmed Games & Leaks

By Fridge · Published October 21, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

The Roblox Hollow event was Roblox's platform-wide Halloween survival event for 2026. Players entered a shared hub called the Hollow, survived a new maze and monster every night, and completed quests in dozens of partner games for badges, tickets and free UGC. This guide covers everything: dates, all nine monsters, the maze rewards, partner game quests, how tickets worked, and which of the pre-event leaks turned out to be real.

Event status: The Hollow ran from October 22, 2026 (2 p.m. PT) and rewards could be collected until noon PT on November 2, 2026. The event is now over. This page stays up as a full recap and will be updated if Roblox brings the Hollow back next Halloween.

What Was the Roblox Hollow Event?

Roblox announced the Hollow as a "shared survival event" celebrating horror games, one of the most popular genres on the platform. Each day for nine days, a new maze opened in the Hollow hub and a new monster from a famous horror game joined. Creators like KreekCraft, LimaMosca, Bundun and SirDapper livestreamed from inside the Hollow, and a live finale started on the morning of October 31.

How the Hollow Worked (Step by Step)

  1. Enter the Hollow hub from the event page on Roblox.
  2. Clear that night's maze, finding clues and dodging the day's monster, for a guaranteed UGC reward.
  3. Use "Visit Games" to jump into partner games and complete their Hollow objectives.
  4. Earn a badge in each partner game. Partner games didn't give tickets directly.
  5. Return to the Hollow and open the REDEEM BADGES panel. Press Confirm to turn each badge into tickets (usually 20, with a few paying more).
  6. Spend tickets at the Concession Stand on cosmetics like the Haunted Harvest Crown (20 tickets) and Gourdlord: The Entangled (100 tickets).

Hollow Event Leaks: What Was Real?

Before launch, leakers shared item names, game lists and teaser images. Here's how the main rumors held up:

  • Nine horror games with daily monsters: confirmed. Roblox's official announcement named all nine.
  • Free UGC from mazes: confirmed. Every night's maze had a guaranteed reward.
  • Big partner game list: confirmed, and it was even bigger than early leaks suggested.
  • Specific leaked items like the Bloodsteel Bathelm and the Wing of Corrupted Jerry: Fridge covered these in Shorts (Bloodsteel Bathelm, Wing of Corrupted Jerry). Leaked item names often change or get cut before release, so treat any item that didn't show up in the official shop or maze rewards as unconfirmed.
Leak safety: "Early access" offers that ask for your password or a download are scams. Real early access only ever comes through Roblox itself or official creator events.

Tips for the Next Roblox Event

  • Do the maze every day. Daily rewards can't be made up later.
  • Pick quick partner quests first. Some, like joining a game or a 15-minute playtime quest, are almost free tickets.
  • Redeem badges before the deadline. Badges earned in partner games only became tickets once you confirmed them in the Hollow.
  • Follow Fridge for leaks, confirmed games and badge guides when the next event is announced.

The Hollow FAQ

When was the Roblox Hollow event?

The Hollow started October 22, 2026 at 2 p.m. PT, with a live finale on October 31. Rewards could be collected until noon PT on November 2, 2026.

Is the Roblox Hollow event still going?

No. The Hollow 2026 ended on November 2, 2026. Roblox hasn't announced whether it will return.

How did you get tickets in the Roblox Hollow?

Complete a Hollow quest in a partner game to earn a badge, then return to the Hollow and confirm the badge in the REDEEM BADGES panel for tickets.

What monsters were in the Roblox Hollow?

Seek, Twisted Dandy, Biter, The Deer, Hiachi, Mimic, The Inspector, The Overseer and Piggy, one for each of the nine days.
