How to AFK in The Forge and Farm Ores Without a Macro

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Want to farm while you're away from the keyboard? Fridge has three videos on how to AFK in The Forge: a quick Short, a full no-macro method for ores and essences, and a newer Island 3 spot for Divine ores. Here's what you need to know before you try it.

Watch Fridge's AFK Farming Videos

Fridge covered this topic in 2 full videos and 1 Short uploaded between December 17, 2025 and January 6, 2026. They are all embedded here, so you can watch and follow the written steps side by side.

The Shorts play in a vertical player. Tap one to watch it here, or open it on YouTube.

Video date check: The Forge gets updates often. The newest is version 1.09, Halloween & Guilds, from October 20, 2026. Fridge's videos show the game as it was when he filmed them. The written steps on this page match the game as of November 11, 2026, and were checked against the Forge Wiki.

How AFK Farming Works in The Forge

The Forge doesn't have an official auto-mine button. Fridge's method, shown in the videos, uses a spot where the game keeps giving you ore and essences while you stand still, so you don't need a macro or auto-clicker. Watch the exact position in the video: AFK spots depend on map layouts that change with updates, and we only list facts we could check.

Avoid macros and auto-clickers: third-party automation can break Roblox's rules and get accounts flagged. Fridge's videos specifically say no macro is needed. Never download "AFK scripts" from random sites.

Roblox's 20-Minute Idle Kick

Roblox disconnects players who have been idle for about 20 minutes. Any AFK method has to deal with that, or you'll wake up in the main menu. Our how to AFK in Roblox guide covers the safe options.

Island 3: Farming Divine Ores

Fridge's January 6, 2026 video is about farming Divine ores on Island 3, the Frostspire Expanse. The Forge Wiki lists these Divine ores there:

  • Heavenite: found only in Floating Crystals at The Peak, with a 1 in 26,666 chance and a 13.7x multiplier. It adds an Angel Smite trait to weapons.
  • Golem Heart: dropped by the Golem boss at The Peak, with a 1 in 8 drop chance from the boss and an 8.5x multiplier.
  • Gargantuan and Stolen Heart: crafted Divine ores. Stolen Heart is made from 1 Arcane Crystal and 3 Golem Heart.

You'll need at least a Void Pickaxe for The Peak's crystals, according to Pro Game Guides.

Get More Out of Each AFK Session

  • Rune your pickaxe. Miner Shard runes can add Luck (5–16%) and Yield (5–15% chance of an extra ore). See our runes guide.
  • Use Double Storage (249 Robux) if your stash fills up overnight. It's optional.
  • Start at the top of the hour. A new world event rolls every hour, and Fortune or Ultra Fortune boosts mining.
  • Private servers (added December 13, 2025) keep other players from crowding your spot.

When you're back, turn the haul into Gold with our money guide.

The Forge FAQ

Can you AFK farm in The Forge?

Yes. Fridge shows spots where you can farm ore and essences without a macro, but Roblox still kicks idle players after about 20 minutes.

Is using a macro in The Forge allowed?

Third-party macros and auto-clickers can break Roblox's rules. Fridge's methods don't use them.

Where do you farm Divine ores in The Forge?

At The Peak on the Frostspire Expanse. Heavenite comes from Floating Crystals and Golem Heart from the Golem boss.
