How to Emote on Roblox in 2026: Emote Wheel, /e Commands and Fixes

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Here's how to emote on Roblox in 2026. Emotes are short animations such as waving, dancing and cheering. You can play them from the emote wheel or by typing /e commands in chat. They only work in games that allow them.

Step 1: Open the Emote Wheel

  • PC: press the period key (.) to open the emote wheel, or open it from the Roblox menu (the emote icon).
  • Mobile: tap the Roblox menu or chat area and look for the Emotes option.
  • Select an emote to play it. Moving usually cancels it.

Step 2: Use Emote Chat Commands

Press / to open chat, type a command and press Enter. Roblox Support lists these free emote commands:

Free Roblox emote commands
CommandWhat it does
/e waveWaves
/e pointPoints forward
/e cheerCheers
/e laughLaughs
/e danceDance 1
/e dance2Dance 2
/e dance3Dance 3

Chat commands need in-experience chat to be turned on for your account and in that game.

Step 3: Equip Emotes on Your Avatar

  1. Get emotes from the Marketplace (Animations → Emotes). Some are free, others cost Robux.
  2. Open the Avatar Editor and go to Animations → Emotes.
  3. Assign emotes to the slots on the wheel.
  4. Join a game and open the wheel to use them.

How to Emote on Roblox PS5, Xbox and Mobile

  • PS5 and Xbox: open the Roblox menu with the controller's menu button and look for the Emotes option. Controller layouts can change with updates, so check the in-game menu if a shortcut doesn't work.
  • Mobile: use the Emotes option in the menu, or type /e dance in chat if chat is enabled.
  • Typing commands on console requires a keyboard or the on-screen keyboard.

Roblox Emotes Not Working? Fixes

  1. Game blocks emotes. Many fighting, obby or custom-character games turn them off.
  2. R6 vs R15: some games use older R6 avatars where many emotes don't play.
  3. Chat is off: /e commands need chat. Parents control chat for younger kids.
  4. Nothing equipped: the wheel is empty until you add emotes in the Avatar Editor.

  • Celebrate wins with friends, and say hi on voice; see how to turn on voice chat on Roblox.

Free Emotes vs Paid Emotes

Every account can use the default emotes through the /e commands in the table above. Extra emotes, such as dances and special poses, come from the Marketplace. Some are free during promotions, and some games also hand out emotes as rewards. For example, our Jujutsu Shenanigans codes page lists codes that unlock free in-game emotes. Emotes bought from the Marketplace belong to your account and work in any game that supports them. Emotes earned inside a specific game usually only work in that game.

Quick Checklist

  1. Check that the game allows emotes (most hangout and roleplay games do).
  2. Equip at least one emote in the Avatar Editor so the wheel isn't empty.
  3. Press the period key on PC, or open Emotes from the menu.
  4. Use /e commands in chat as a backup.

Roblox FAQ

How do you emote on Roblox?

Press the period key on PC or open Emotes from the Roblox menu to use the emote wheel, or type commands like /e wave or /e dance in chat.

What are the Roblox emote commands?

Free commands include /e wave, /e point, /e cheer, /e laugh, /e dance, /e dance2 and /e dance3.

Why can't I emote in some Roblox games?

The game may have disabled emotes, use R6 avatars or have chat turned off, which blocks /e commands.

How do I equip emotes on Roblox?

Open the Avatar Editor, go to Animations > Emotes, and assign emotes you own to the wheel slots.

Are Roblox emotes free?

The basic /e emotes like wave, point, cheer, laugh and dance are free. Extra emotes come from the Marketplace, and some games give emotes as rewards.

Do game emotes work in other Roblox games?

Usually not. Emotes you unlock inside a game normally only work there, while Marketplace emotes work wherever emotes are allowed.
