How to Get Galaxite in The Forge: Then and Now

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Fridge's Galaxite Short is one of his most-watched The Forge uploads, with about 494,000 views. But how you get the "galaxy ore" has changed completely since December 2025. Here's how it worked then and how it works now.

Watch Fridge's Galaxite Short

Fridge covered this topic in 1 Short uploaded on December 18, 2025. They are all embedded here, so you can watch and follow the written steps side by side.

The Shorts play in a vertical player. Tap one to watch it here, or open it on YouTube.

Video date check: The Forge gets updates often. The newest is version 1.09, Halloween & Guilds, from October 20, 2026. Fridge's videos show the game as it was when he filmed them. The written steps on this page match the game as of November 11, 2026, and were checked against the Forge Wiki.

How Galaxite Worked in December 2025

When Fridge posted his Short on December 18, 2025, Galaxite was a Divine ore with a 1 in 1,000,000 drop chance. Allthings.how reported that it came from the crystal rocks in the Goblin Cave on the Forgotten Kingdom. It had an 11.5x multiplier, the highest in the game at the time, and no traits. The only way to farm it was to mine huge numbers of rocks with Luck and Yield runes and hope.

How to Get Galaxite Now (November 2026)

The Forge Wiki now lists Galaxite as a Divine ore that's crafted in the Void Cave of the Crimson Sakura Isles, by talking to the Galaxy Guardian. The Void Cave arrived in version 1.07 on May 29, 2026.

Galaxite recipe (Forge Wiki, November 11, 2026)
IngredientAmount
Anti Matter3
Singularity1
Supermassive Black Hole1
Gold25,000
Craft time30 minutes
  1. Reach the Crimson Sakura Isles, the fourth island, and find the Void Cave.
  2. Collect 3 Anti Matter, 1 Singularity and 1 Supermassive Black Hole.
  3. Talk to the Galaxy Guardian and craft Galaxite for 25,000 Gold.
  4. Wait 30 minutes for the craft to finish.

What Galaxite Does Now

Galaxite's multiplier is now 25x, and it has traits for both weapons and armor:

  • Weapon, Blackhole: a 30% chance to create a black hole that pulls in enemies within 65 studs, then explodes for 60% of base weapon damage.
  • Weapon: +45% Critical Chance and +20% Critical Damage.
  • Armor: +8% max Health, 8 HP regeneration per second, a 50% chance to halve incoming damage, and 2% thorns.

The Forge Wiki's endgame recipes for Crimson Sakura use Galaxite in almost every build, for example 1 Galaxite with Yin-Yang, Wolfarite and Kyubite for a straight sword.

Galaxite Tips

  • Don't sell it. One Galaxite item is worth far more than its sell value.
  • Galaxite works best when it makes up a large share of the recipe, because the item multiplier depends on the ores you use. See our forging guide.
  • The old Goblin Cave route still has the crystal ores you need for other recipes. See our Goblin Cave guide.

The Forge FAQ

How do you get Galaxite in The Forge?

Craft it at the Galaxy Guardian in the Void Cave on the Crimson Sakura Isles from 3 Anti Matter, 1 Singularity, 1 Supermassive Black Hole and 25,000 Gold.

Is Galaxite still 1 in a million in The Forge?

No. That was the December 2025 drop rate. It's now a crafted Divine ore.

What does Galaxite do in The Forge?

On weapons, it adds a Blackhole explosion plus big crit bonuses. On armor, it adds Health, regeneration, a damage shield and thorns.
