How to Get the Heavenly ("Heavenite") Pickaxe in The Forge and Beat the Secret Maze

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Fridge calls it the "Heavenite Pickaxe", and the game calls it the Heavenly Pickaxe. It's one of the best pickaxes on Island 3, and you get the Heavenly Pickaxe by finishing the Secret Maze. This page combines his three videos on the pickaxe and the Maze with the current steps from the Forge Wiki.

Watch Fridge's Heavenly Pickaxe and Maze Videos

Fridge covered this topic in 2 full videos and 1 Short uploaded between December 25, 2025 and January 11, 2026. They are all embedded here, so you can watch and follow the written steps side by side.

The Shorts play in a vertical player. Tap one to watch it here, or open it on YouTube.

Video date check: The Forge gets updates often. The newest is version 1.09, Halloween & Guilds, from October 20, 2026. Fridge's videos show the game as it was when he filmed them. The written steps on this page match the game as of November 11, 2026, and were checked against the Forge Wiki.

Its +85% Luck is the highest of the Frostspire pickaxes listed here. "Heavenite" is actually a separate Divine ore from Floating Crystals at The Peak, which is why the two names get mixed up.

How to Reach the Secret Maze

The Secret Maze came with version 1.04, The Maze Update, on January 11, 2026, the same day as Fridge's Maze Short and his second Heavenly Pickaxe video. His first video, from December 25, 2025, shows the pickaxe before the Maze existed, so the unlock has changed since.

  1. On the Frostspire Expanse, take the Ferry. It carries you to the Maze entrance for a small fare.
  2. Enter the Secret Maze, an "ever-shifting labyrinth" beneath the frozen island. The layout moves, so follow Fridge's route in the video rather than a fixed map.
  3. In the heart of the maze is Fungi's Shop, which sells weapon blueprints and Fungi's Potion.
  4. Keep going to the very end, where the angels Zachariel and Zarall wait.

The Three Maze Quests

The Secret Maze questline
QuestKillReward
Maze Quest 110 Skeleton Pirates, 2 Cthulhu150,000 Gold, 10,000 XP, 1 Luck Totem
Maze Quest 225 Skeleton Pirates, 7 Cthulhu250,000 Gold, 14,000 XP
Maze Quest 350 Skeleton Pirates, 15 Cthulhu400,000 Gold, 23,000 XP

Finishing all three is how you get the Heavenly Pickaxe: it unlocks it for purchase. The quests pay 800,000 Gold in total, which covers a big part of the 2,000,000 price. The Masked Stranger at the Maze entrance also has a daily Pirate Raid quest with the same targets, worth 50,000 Gold, so you can do both at once.

Tips

  • Bring your best weapon and use block and parry against Cthulhu. See our combat guide.
  • Roll for Goblin to save 240,000 Gold on the price.
  • Compare it with the Prismatic Pickaxe and the Dragon Head Pickaxe.

The Forge FAQ

How do you get the Heavenly Pickaxe in The Forge?

Take the Ferry to the Secret Maze, reach the end, finish all three Maze quests for Zachariel and Zarall, then buy it for 2,000,000 Gold.

Is the Heavenite Pickaxe the same as the Heavenly Pickaxe?

Yes. Players often call it the Heavenite Pickaxe, but the in-game name is Heavenly Pickaxe. Heavenite is a separate ore.

How do you unlock the maze in The Forge?

Take the Ferry on the Frostspire Expanse to the Secret Maze entrance. It was added on January 11, 2026.
