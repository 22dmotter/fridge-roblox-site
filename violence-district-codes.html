Violence District Codes (November 2026): Does It Have Codes?

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Searching for Violence District codes? As of last checked November 11, 2026, there are none, and the game has no code redemption system. There's no Codes button or code field, and no code has ever been released. Violence District, a Dead by Daylight style horror game, was the #16 most-played game on Roblox on November 11, with about 61,000 players online during its "Week 3 x Butcher" Halloween event.

What We Checked

  • The official Roblox game page explains survivors, killers and pallets and points players to the Discord. It doesn't mention codes.
  • Timesaver (checked November 8, 2026) found 0 active and 0 expired codes. The verified VIOLENCE DISTRICT group has no code posts, and the community wiki has no Codes page.
  • RBX Radar, CodesHub and UrGameTips also list no working codes in November 2026.
  • Older checks by TheTechBasket, TechWiser and Beebom (cited by Timesaver) agree the game has no redeem system.

Fake Codes to Ignore

Not real: VIOLENCE10, KILLERBOOST, ESCAPEPLAN and GENERATORFIX. Timesaver traced them to an unofficial look-alike site that also gets basic game facts wrong. With no redeem box, there's nowhere to type them anyway. Skip "free Screws" generators and script downloads; they're after your account.

How to Earn Screws Fast Without Codes

Screws are the main currency. They buy killers, items and emotes and pay for perk upgrades. Emblems are the rarer currency for perks, emotes and skins. Here are the rough Screw payouts from the community wiki, as reported by Timesaver (treat them as a guide):

Approximate Screw payouts
ActionScrews
Repairing a generator (survivor)About 1 per second
Stunning the killer with a palletAbout 12 each
Healing yourself or a teammateAbout 10 each
Surviving a chase2 per second, up to 202
EscapingAbout 200
Spiking a survivor (killer)About 50 each
Killing a survivor (killer)About 100 each
  1. As a survivor, stay on generators, use pallets well and escape. Escaping pays the most.
  2. As the killer, spike survivors repeatedly; each spike pays on top of the kill.
  3. Save for killers. The Butcher, added October 24, 2026, costs 150,000 Screws according to the wiki. Older killers like The Killer (8,500) are much cheaper.

Chases are won and lost on frame rate, so check our fix Roblox lag guide before a long session.

Where Codes Would Appear

If the developers ever add codes, they'll most likely show up first in the official VIOLENCE DISTRICT Discord (linked on the game page) and the verified Roblox group. A new tag in the game's title usually means a big update, which is the best time to check. We'll update this page with the first confirmed code and its redeem steps.

Want a horror game that does have codes? See Dandy's World codes or 99 Nights in the Forest codes. Forsaken is in the same no-codes boat as Violence District.

What Is Violence District?

Violence District is an asymmetrical horror game in the style of Dead by Daylight. Five survivors repair generators and escape while one killer hunts them, and pallets are used to stun the killer. It was created on October 31, 2024, and the community wiki lists nine killers: The Slasher, The Stalker, The Killer, The Masked, The Hidden, The Abysswalker, The Veil, The Cure and The Butcher. Two optional game passes exist, V.I.P (599 Robux) and 2x Emotes (149 Robux), but neither is needed to play.

Violence District FAQ

Are there any Violence District codes?

No. As of November 11, 2026, Violence District has no codes and no code redemption system.

Does VIOLENCE10 work in Violence District?

No. VIOLENCE10, KILLERBOOST and ESCAPEPLAN come from an unofficial look-alike site and aren't real codes.

How do you get Screws fast in Violence District?

Repair generators, stun the killer with pallets, survive chases and escape as a survivor, or spike and kill survivors as the killer.

Where would Violence District codes be posted?

Most likely in the official VIOLENCE DISTRICT Discord and the verified Roblox group.

How much does The Butcher cost in Violence District?

150,000 Screws, according to the community wiki.

Is there a Violence District code for The Butcher?

No. The Butcher costs 150,000 Screws, and there are no codes of any kind in the game.
