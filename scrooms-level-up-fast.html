How to Level Up FAST in Scrooms: The Best XP Method

By Fridge · Published October 13, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Want to level up fast in Scrooms? The good news is that Scrooms XP is simple. Only two things raise your level: catching Scrooms and completing quests. Catches give a steady trickle of XP, and quests pay out in huge lumps. This guide shows you the fastest catching routine, which quests are worth chasing, and which purchases actually help (most don't).

Where Scrooms XP Comes From

The main quest itself spells it out: you gain experience by taming Scrooms and helping others. Here's how the two sources compare:

Scrooms XP sources
SourceXPNotes
Successful catchSmall, steady (grows as you level)Shown above your head right after each catch
Failed catchNone"Your Scroom got away…" gives 0 XP
Tamer in Training (main quest)2,025 XP + 350 ChimesWorth roughly a full level on its own
A Leg Up (next main quest)675 XPFind and speak to Shax
Beekeeper's Request (side quest)1,350 XP + 450 ChimesNeeds a rare Funglebee

A single song takes about 30–34 seconds from first click to result, so catching back-to-back gets you close to two catches per minute. That's the ceiling for catch-only leveling, which is why quests matter so much.

The Fastest Way to Level Up Fast in Scrooms

The best XP method is a tight loop with zero walking between catches:

  1. Stand on the grass near the spawn signpost in Songkeeper's Grove. Scrooms here are mostly Common and Uncommon, so catches are fast and reliable.
  2. Keep your instrument in hotbar slot 1. Start the next song the instant the catch notice disappears.
  3. Don't stop to sort your inventory. Keep catching until your backpack is full.
  4. Sell everything at Julian with "Sell Inventory." In one test, around 185 mostly Common and Uncommon Scrooms sold for about 14,000 Chimes.
  5. Spend Chimes at Jane's stall on a better instrument, then run straight back to the signpost.
Pro tip: Failures are rare (around 5–7 per 100 songs), so don't slow down to play "perfectly." Speed beats precision for XP.

Is the 1.5x Catch EXP Pass Worth It?

If you're going to spend Robux in Scrooms, this is the one purchase that affects leveling. The 1.5x Catch EXP pass costs 99 Robux in the Store, gives 50% more XP from catches, and is permanent once bought.

The difference shows up in catch counts. In testing, a level in the low teens took around 13 catches without the pass, and similar levels dropped to roughly 8–11 catches with it. Over a long grind that adds up to hours saved.

The cheaper Server Luck offer (19 Robux) only affects luck, not XP. Skip it if your goal is leveling.

Take Every Quest (The Real Shortcut)

One quest turn-in can be worth dozens of catches. Look for NPCs with a green "?" above their head and accept everything you walk past. Your Quest Log (hotbar slot 4) tracks side quests too.

Tamer in Training

This main quest comes from Halden at his campfire. Its objective is to reach a level milestone and then return to him. Most guides list level 25, though some community notes report a lower number, so check the live quest text in your log. Here's the key detail: reaching the level doesn't complete the quest. You have to walk back to Halden and press E. The 2,025 XP reward can push you up an entire level instantly.

A Leg Up

This unlocks right after Tamer in Training. Find and talk to Shax for 675 XP. Easy XP for a short walk.

Beekeeper's Request

Jones the beekeeper asks for a Funglebee, a Wondrous-rarity Scroom. It's worth 1,350 XP, but Funglebees are very rare. Accept the quest so it's ready when one shows up, then keep leveling instead of hunting for it.

Haven't unlocked Halden yet? Follow our Scrooms beginner's guide first, then grab the five Scrooms statue locations to get the Ocarina.

Do Instruments Make You Level Faster?

No. Instrument cards list Precision, Harmony, Tension, Luck, Tune Speed and Resonance, and none of them touch XP. Songs stay about 30 seconds long whichever instrument you use. That doesn't mean upgrades are useless:

  • The Tamer's Guitar (100 Chimes) makes catches more reliable early on.
  • The Violin of Pilgrimage (4,500 Chimes) adds +40% Luck for rarer Scrooms.

Buy instruments for better catches and rarer finds. Buy the 1.5x pass for XP.

How Long Does Each Level Take?

XP per level keeps rising, so early levels fly by and later ones slow down. With the pass and nonstop catching, expect roughly 6–7 minutes per level in the mid-teens and around 11–14 minutes per level in the low-to-mid twenties. Big milestones to aim for afterward: level 50 unlocks the Seasoned Tamer title and level 100 unlocks Living Legend.

Mistakes That Stop You From Leveling Up Fast in Scrooms

  • Walking around looking for rares. Rarity doesn't clearly give more XP, but it definitely costs time.
  • Forgetting to turn in quests. Hitting the level isn't enough. Go back to the quest giver.
  • Going AFK. Roblox disconnects idle players, and auto-farming tools can risk your account.
  • Buying Server Luck for XP. It doesn't increase experience.

Scrooms FAQ

What is the fastest way to level up in Scrooms?

Catch Scrooms nonstop near the spawn signpost in Songkeeper's Grove, sell when your bag is full, and turn in every quest you can. Quests like Tamer in Training give thousands of XP at once.

Does the instrument affect XP in Scrooms?

No. Instruments improve catching stats like Precision and Luck, but none of them increase the XP you get per catch.

Is the 1.5x Catch EXP pass worth it in Scrooms?

If you plan to grind, yes. It costs 99 Robux, is permanent, and gives 50% more XP from every catch. It's the only purchase that affects XP.

How do I finish Tamer in Training in Scrooms?

Reach the required level shown in your Quest Log, then go back to Halden at his campfire and talk to him. Reaching the level alone doesn't complete it.
