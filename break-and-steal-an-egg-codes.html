Break and Steal an Egg Codes (November 2026): Free Rewards

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Looking for Break and Steal an Egg codes? As of November 11, 2026, there are no codes and no code box in the game. But there are two real freebies most players miss: a free +10,000 Speed reward and a free Sabertooth Egg. Here's how to claim both.

Working Break and Steal an Egg Codes

Active codes – last checked November 11, 2026
CodeRewardStatus
No codes. The game has no code system.

The Break and Steal an Egg Wiki (November 3), CodesHub (November 9), RollerCodes and Raretip (November 5) all report zero codes. There are no expired codes either. The only text box in the game is the Community Rewards username field, and it checks your Roblox username, not a code.

Free +10,000 Speed

  1. On the game's Roblox page, like the game (thumbs up) and favorite it (star).
  2. Join the game and go to the Free Rewards area to the right of the Trails Shop.
  3. Claim the +10,000 Speed reward.

Free Legendary Sabertooth Egg

  1. Join the ILGames x Eggs community on Roblox (the group that runs the game).
  2. Launch the game and open the Shop on the left side.
  3. Scroll to Community Rewards.
  4. Type your exact Roblox username (matching capital letters) and press Verify.
  5. The free Sabertooth Egg is added to your account.
Note: only type your username into this box. The game never needs your password.

Break and Steal an Egg vs Steal an Egg

These are two different games made by different creators. Break and Steal an Egg comes from ILGames x Eggs, and progress, pets and rewards don't carry over to Steal an Egg. In Break and Steal an Egg you break eggs with a pickaxe to hatch animals, steal animals past guards, place them in your base for cash, and spend that cash on Speed and pickaxe upgrades.

Quick Tips While You Wait for Codes

  • Upgrade Speed early. Faster escapes mean fewer lost animals.
  • Place every animal you steal in your base, since only placed animals earn cash.
  • Upgrade your pickaxe to break eggs faster.

Break and Steal an Egg FAQ

Are there Break and Steal an Egg promo codes?

No. As of November 11, 2026, the game has no codes and no code redemption box.

How do I get the free Sabertooth Egg?

Join the ILGames x Eggs community, then open the Shop, go to Community Rewards, type your exact username and press Verify.

How do I get free Speed in Break and Steal an Egg?

Like and favorite the game, then claim the +10,000 Speed reward at the Free Rewards area next to the Trails Shop.

Is Break and Steal an Egg the same as Steal an Egg?

No. They're separate games by different creators, and progress doesn't transfer.

What do you type in the Community Rewards box?

Your exact Roblox username, after joining the ILGames x Eggs community. It verifies membership and gives the free Sabertooth Egg. Never type your password.

Is Break and Steal an Egg the same game as Steal an Egg?

No. They are separate games made by different creators, so progress, pets and rewards do not carry over between them.
