How to AFK in Roblox Safely Without Getting Kicked (2026)

By Fridge · Published November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Disclaimer (ToS risk): Roblox disconnects idle players on purpose. Roblox has said account farming, "accelerated play" and automated activity aren't aligned with its vision, and it uses AI detection against abnormal automated behavior. Bots violate the Terms of Use. Auto-clickers, macros, scripts and "anti-AFK" programs can break Roblox's rules or a game's own rules and can get you kicked, game-banned or actioned on your account. This guide only recommends methods that don't automate your inputs.

Wondering how to AFK in Roblox without losing your progress? The key thing to know is that Roblox kicks players who are idle for about 20 minutes. That's a platform feature, not a bug. The safest way to "AFK" is to use systems games build for it, or to stay lightly active yourself, rather than running software that plays for you.

Why Roblox Kicks You for Being Idle

Roblox tracks when a player stops sending input. Developers can listen for this through the Player.Idled event, and Roblox automatically disconnects players after roughly 20 minutes of inactivity. The disconnect message tells you you've been idle for more than 20 minutes.

Why it exists: idle players take up server slots that active players could use, and idle-farming hurts game economies. That's also why many developers add their own anti-AFK systems on top.

What counts as idle in Roblox
SituationResult
No keyboard, mouse, touch or controller input for ~20 minutesDisconnected by Roblox
Game's own anti-AFK timer runs outKicked by that game (time varies)
Standing in an official in-game AFK zoneDepends on the game; many keep you connected or move you to an AFK server

Legit Ways to AFK in Roblox

So, how to AFK in Roblox the right way? Use features the developer built for it, or stay lightly active.

1. Use the game's built-in AFK features

Many popular games include AFK zones, AFK worlds, idle rewards or offline earnings. These are designed by the developer, so using them is allowed. Look for:

  • AFK areas or "AFK worlds" that give rewards over time.
  • Offline earnings that pay out when you log back in.
  • Treadmills or passive trainers like the one in Steal an Egg (see our Steal an Egg speed guide).
  • Gamepasses or settings the developer sells or provides for idle play.

2. Stay lightly active

If you're grinding something repetitive, the honest fix is to keep playing, just less intensely. In our Scrooms leveling guide, the fastest XP method is a tight active loop anyway. Watching a video on a second screen while you play is fine. Leaving the PC entirely isn't.

3. Plan around the timer

  1. Do your active grinding first.
  2. Before you step away, park your character in a safe spot or AFK zone.
  3. Collect offline rewards when you come back instead of trying to stay connected.

Macros, Auto-Clickers and Anti-AFK Tools: The Risks

Search results are full of "Roblox AFK macro" downloads. Here's why Fridge doesn't recommend them:

  • Roblox is cracking down on automation. Roblox has said it's working on automated action against abnormal activity, targeting responsible accounts and their alts.
  • Games set their own rules. Many games ban macro use outright, and developers can ban you from their game even if Roblox doesn't act.
  • Anything that injects into Roblox is a modified client. Roblox may take action up to account termination for modified clients.
  • Malware risk. "Free AFK scripts" are a common way to spread account-stealing software.
Rule of thumb: if a tool plays the game for you while you're away, assume it can get you banned, even if a website says it's "undetected" or "allowed."

How to AFK in Roblox on Mobile, Xbox and PlayStation

The 20-minute idle disconnect applies on every platform. Some players change their device's screen-timeout settings so the screen stays on, but that doesn't stop the Roblox idle kick. You still need in-game input. On consoles, the same rules apply: use built-in AFK features and don't rely on third-party turbo or macro controllers.

AFK With Multiple Accounts

Running alt accounts to idle-farm is exactly the kind of behavior Roblox targets with its bot and account-farming crackdowns. If you want to play with a second account, read our how to run multiple Roblox accounts guide, which explains the official options and the risks.

Getting Disconnected While Active?

If Roblox kicks you even though you're playing, it's probably a connection or performance problem, not the idle timer. Check our how to fix Roblox lag guide for network and graphics fixes.

Roblox FAQ

How long can you be AFK in Roblox before getting kicked?

Roblox disconnects players after about 20 minutes without any input. Individual games can use shorter anti-AFK timers of their own.

Is it bannable to use an AFK macro in Roblox?

It can be. Roblox targets automated and accelerated play, many games ban macros, and anything that injects into Roblox counts as a modified client. Use built-in AFK features instead.

How do you AFK in Roblox without getting kicked?

Use the game's official AFK zones, idle rewards or offline earnings, or stay lightly active. Avoid third-party anti-AFK software.

Does keeping my phone screen on stop the Roblox AFK kick?

No. Screen-timeout settings keep the display on, but Roblox still disconnects you if there's no in-game input for about 20 minutes.
