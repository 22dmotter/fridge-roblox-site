Steal a Brainrot Codes (November 2026): Free Secret Brainrot

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Here are the working Steal a Brainrot codes for November 2026, last checked November 11, 2026. There's 1 working code: BESTBRAINROTEVER, which spawns the secret La Vacca Saturno Saturnita. Codes in this game work differently from most: they don't add the brainrot to your base, they spawn it on the red carpet, and you still have to buy it. Steal a Brainrot by BRAZILIAN SPYDER was the #18 most-played game on Roblox on November 11, with about 60,000 players online.

Get Ready Before You Redeem

GamesRadar+ explains the catch: La Vacca Saturno Saturnita costs $80M in-game cash, appears only once, and other players in your server can buy it first. It earns $325K/s, so it's worth the prep.

  1. Save at least $80M before you type the code.
  2. Stand at the start of the red carpet so you reach it first.
  3. Pick a quieter server if you can, so fewer players compete for it.
  4. Redeem, then buy it the moment it appears and get it back to your base.

How to Redeem Steal a Brainrot Codes

  1. Launch Steal a Brainrot on Roblox.
  2. Click the CODES button on the left side of the screen. (Since June 2026 it has its own button instead of being in the Shop.)
  3. Type the code into the Code Here... box. GamesRadar+ says codes aren't case-sensitive.
  4. Click Submit, then watch the red carpet.

Codes Some Sites Still List

Roblox Den still marks saturn, Bluesammy67, DIVINECURSED, MAYMEOWLJANDEL and FREEOCTOBLOCK777 as "check". GamesRadar+ and the wiki list them as expired or out of stock, so we don't count them.

Most Steal a Brainrot promo codes had limited stock and ran out fast. Where New Codes Come From

Dexerto says new codes are usually announced in the official Discord, and the wiki notes that the developer often drops limited-stock codes during Admin Abuse events. How Steal a Brainrot Promo Codes Work

In Steal a Brainrot you buy brainrots as they walk down the red carpet, place them in your base for income per second, and defend them while raiding other bases. Rarer brainrots earn more, so a secret like La Vacca Saturno Saturnita is a big jump. The wiki says codes were added on December 13, 2025, and the developer moved them to their own button on June 13, 2026. Most public codes have had small stock limits, which is why they disappear so quickly.

Steal a Brainrot FAQ

What Steal a Brainrot promo codes work right now?

As of November 11, 2026, only BESTBRAINROTEVER works. It spawns La Vacca Saturno Saturnita on the red carpet.

Does a Steal a Brainrot code give the brainrot for free?

No. It spawns the brainrot on the red carpet, and you still need to buy it ($80M for La Vacca Saturno Saturnita) before other players do.

Where is the codes button in Steal a Brainrot?

On the left side of the screen. Type the code in the box and press Submit.

Why did my Steal a Brainrot code say expired?

Most codes have limited stock and run out within hours. Only BESTBRAINROTEVER is confirmed working now.

Are Steal a Brainrot promo codes case-sensitive?

No, according to GamesRadar+.

How much does La Vacca Saturno Saturnita earn?

About $325K per second, according to GamesRadar+, which is why it's worth saving $80M to buy it.

Who makes Steal a Brainrot?

The game page lists BRAZILIAN SPYDER. The wiki says the developer, Sammy, creates the codes.

When were codes added to Steal a Brainrot?

December 13, 2025, according to the Steal a Brainrot Wiki.
