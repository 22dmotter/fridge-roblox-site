Yeti Heart in The Forge: Stats and Trait

Last checked: November 15, 2026

Yeti Heart is a Mythic ore on the official The Peak list. Sources agree it is obtained from Yeti, with a $150 sell price. They do not agree on the drop chance and multiplier, so those fields are left blank on this page. Its confirmed trait is Increases Attack Speed by 10%; Increases Critical Chance by 30%; Increases Critical Damage by 10% (Weapons).

How to Get Yeti Heart

On the official Forge Wiki list, Yeti Heart is a The Peak ore, obtained from Yeti. the official Forge Wiki, Game8, BloxInformer, Destructoid, and Pocket Tactics all mention this ore. Game8 lists the area as raven cave; bloxinformer places it under raven cave; pocket tactics places it under raven cave ore. This page follows the official wiki list for the location label (The Peak). Sources do not agree on the drop chance (see below).

What Yeti Heart Is Good For

Yeti Heart's confirmed trait is Increases Attack Speed by 10%; Increases Critical Chance by 30%; Increases Critical Damage by 10% (Weapons). It has a $150 sell price. The multiplier is left blank because sources do not agree. See the Island 3 / Frostspire Expanse guide and the forging guide for how later World 3 ores fit the crafting loop.
