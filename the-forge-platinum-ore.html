Platinum Ore in The Forge: Drop Chance and Price

Last checked: November 14, 2026

Platinum is a Rare ore with the same 0.8x multiplier and $12 sell price as Mushroomite, but a lower drop chance. Sources agree it comes from Boulders in Stonewake's Cross at 1-in-28, and that it has no trait.

How to Get Platinum

Mine Boulders in Stonewake's Cross. The official Forge Wiki, Game8, Destructoid, and BloxInformer all list a 1/28 drop chance from Boulders there.

What Platinum Is Good For

Platinum has no trait, a 0.8x multiplier, and a $12 sell price, matching Mushroomite's confirmed multiplier and price. For reaching Boulders, see the pickaxe progression guide. Our money guide generally recommends forging ores into gear rather than selling the raw ore.
