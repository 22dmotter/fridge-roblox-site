Best Pickaxe in The Forge: The Full Upgrade Path from Island 1 to Island 3 (and Beyond)

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Your pickaxe decides which rocks you can break, so choosing the best pickaxe in The Forge at each stage is the fastest way to progress. Fridge's full upgrade video takes you from Island 1 to Island 3. This page follows the same path with the Forge Wiki's current order and prices.

Watch Fridge's Pickaxe Upgrade Guide

Fridge covered this topic in 1 full video uploaded on December 27, 2025. They are all embedded here, so you can watch and follow the written steps side by side.

Video date check: The Forge gets updates often. The newest is version 1.09, Halloween & Guilds, from October 20, 2026. Fridge's videos show the game as it was when he filmed them. The written steps on this page match the game as of November 11, 2026, and were checked against the Forge Wiki.

How Pickaxe Stats Work

Each pickaxe has Mine Power (damage per swing), Mine Speed, Luck Boost and Rune Slots. Some rocks require a minimum Mine Power. For example, Volcanic Rocks need 100. Rarity runs from Common Stone up to Mythic Dragon Head and the Heavenly Pickaxe.

Island 1 to Island 2

Buy the Iron Pickaxe, then grab the Stonewake's Pickaxe (33 Mine Power, +10% Luck, 3 slots). Pro Game Guides suggests buying the Gold Pickaxe last and skipping the Platinum, because the Stonewake's is cheaper and better. The Forgotten Kingdom opens at level 10 after Sensei Moro's questline. The Arcane Pickaxe (115 Mine Power, +50% Luck, 125,000 Gold) from the Bard quest is an option on Island 2, but the wiki now considers it outclassed. See our Bard quest guide.

Island 2 to Island 3

On the Forgotten Kingdom, buy Cobalt, then Mythril, then Lightite from Miner Fred. The Demonic Pickaxe (200 Mine Power, +53% Luck) is behind Skal's quest in the Volcanic Depths. Pro Game Guides suggests skipping the Magma Pickaxe for its price. The Frostspire Expanse opens at level 70+, which is the "get to Island 3 fast" goal in Fridge's video. Some Frostspire shop pickaxes, from the Mist Pickaxe up, also need specific Sensei Moro quests done.

The Forge FAQ

What is the best pickaxe in The Forge?

For Island 3, the Dragon Head Pickaxe has the most Mine Power (750). The Forge Wiki's full route ends with the Holy and Yin-Yang pickaxes on Island 4.

What pickaxe should I buy first in The Forge?

The Iron Pickaxe, then the Stonewake's Pickaxe in the Stonewake's Cross Inner Cave for 3,333 Gold.

What level do you need for Island 3 in The Forge?

Level 70 or higher, according to Pro Game Guides.
