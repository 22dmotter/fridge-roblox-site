How to Make Roblox Thumbnails for Free (YouTube and Game Icons)

By Fridge · Published November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Want to know how to make Roblox thumbnails without paying for software? You only need two free tools: Roblox Studio to pose your avatar and take clean screenshots, and Photopea, a free browser-based editor, to add text and effects. This guide covers YouTube video thumbnails and Roblox game thumbnails and icons, with the official sizes.

Fair-use reminder: Use your own avatar, your own builds or screenshots from games you're allowed to show. Don't copy other creators' thumbnail art, and don't make misleading thumbnails (like fake codes or fake "free Robux"), which can break YouTube and Roblox rules.

Step 1: Pose Your Avatar in Roblox Studio

  1. Install Roblox Studio for free from the official Roblox Creator Hub and open a Baseplate.
  2. Insert a character. Use the Rig Builder in Studio's Avatar tab to add a character rig, then dress it with accessories to match your look.
  3. Pose it. Rotate the limbs with the Rotate tool or use an animation editor to pick a dramatic frame.
  4. Set the lighting. In the Explorer, select Lighting and try different times of day, then add effects like Bloom, ColorCorrection or DepthOfField for a "GFX" look.
  5. Hide what you don't need: delete or move the Baseplate if you want a transparent-looking background, and turn off grid and selection boxes.
  6. Take a screenshot with your system's screenshot tool, or use a large window for higher resolution.

Studio lighting is the official, safe way to get shader-style visuals. See our Roblox shaders 2026 guide for why injected shaders aren't worth the risk.

Option: Grab Screenshots In-Game

For gameplay thumbnails, capture real moments from the game:

  • Turn Graphics Quality up for cleaner shots (our fix Roblox lag guide shows where the setting is).
  • Hide the UI where the game allows it, and use a free camera if the game offers one.
  • Capture several angles so you can choose the best one later.

Step 2: Edit in Photopea

  1. Open photopea.com in your browser and choose New Project at 1920 × 1080 (or 1280 × 720).
  2. Drag your background screenshot in and scale it to fill the canvas.
  3. Cut out your avatar using the Magic Wand or Quick Selection tool, or Photopea's background remover, and place it on its own layer.
  4. Add an outline or glow with layer styles (Stroke or Outer Glow) so your character pops.
  5. Add 2–5 words of bold text. Keep it short and readable on a phone.
  6. Adjust colors with Brightness/Contrast or Hue/Saturation for a punchier look.
  7. Export with File → Export as → PNG or JPG, and keep it under the size limit.

Design Tips That Get Clicks

Learning how to make Roblox thumbnails that people actually click comes down to a few simple rules:

  • One clear focal point: a big character face, an item or a shocking moment.
  • High contrast colors so the thumbnail stands out next to others.
  • Test at small size. Zoom out to phone-thumbnail size and make sure you can still read it.
  • Keep a consistent style across your videos so viewers recognize your channel.
  • Be honest. If the video is a guide, the thumbnail should show what it actually covers.

How to Upload Thumbnails to a Roblox Game

  1. Open the Creator Dashboard on create.roblox.com and select your experience.
  2. Go to the experience's configuration area, then Places, and open the Thumbnails section for your start place.
  3. Upload your 1920 × 1080 image and wait for moderation.
  4. Upload a 512 × 512 icon in the icon section.

How to Add a Custom Thumbnail on YouTube

In YouTube Studio, open your video's details and choose Upload file under Thumbnail. Custom thumbnails need a verified YouTube account. Once it's live, share the video in your community and invite friends; our join someone on Roblox guide shows the official ways viewers can play with you.

Roblox FAQ

What size is a Roblox thumbnail?

Roblox game thumbnails are 16:9, ideally 1920 × 1080 and under 3 MB. Game icons are 512 × 512.

What size should a Roblox YouTube thumbnail be?

Use a 16:9 image. 1280 × 720 works well; YouTube recommends up to 3840 × 2160, with a minimum width of 640 pixels, as JPG or PNG.

Can I make Roblox thumbnails for free?

Yes. Roblox Studio is free for posing and lighting your avatar, and Photopea is a free browser-based editor for text and effects.

Do I need Blender for Roblox GFX?

No. Blender gives advanced results, but Roblox Studio lighting plus Photopea editing is enough for clean, free thumbnails.
