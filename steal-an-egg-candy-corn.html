How to Get TONS of Candy Corn FAST in Steal an Egg

By Fridge · Published October 20, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Steal an Egg Candy Corn is the currency for the Halloween Dimension, the game's first event world, and you need 2,250 of it for every Halloween Dimension Egg. This guide shows the fastest way to farm Candy Corn: which monsters pay the most, how egg returns stack up, and how to time Blood Moons for double rewards. It's the same method Fridge shows in his Candy Corn video.

Event status: Roblox listed the Halloween Dimension for October 17–24, 2026, but individual features didn't all have end dates, and seasonal content can stay or return. If the portal by the Trails Shop is still open in your server, this guide applies. Bookmark it for next Halloween either way.

How to Enter the Halloween Dimension

  1. Walk to the glowing Halloween portal next to the Trails Shop at spawn.
  2. Step through it to load into the Corrupted Realm. No quest is required.
  3. Some guides report you need around 2.5M Speed to get in. If the portal won't let you through, train up first with our Steal an Egg speed guide.

Inside, the loop works like the main game: defeat monsters for Candy Corn, grab spooky eggs around the map, and carry them back through the portal to hatch. Watch out: ghosts chase anyone holding an egg, and a single hit knocks it out of your hands.

The Fastest Candy Corn Method

Here's the loop that gets you the most Candy Corn per minute:

  1. Hunt Soul Phantoms first. They pay 65 each and go down faster than Pumpkin Golems, which have 500 HP. Fight them with your Bat.
  2. Grab high-rarity eggs on the way. Legendary eggs return around 50, while Mythic and Cosmic eggs like the Crowshade or Hexed Pumpkin return 100 each.
  3. Return eggs carefully. Ghosts target egg carriers, so take the shortest path to the portal and don't fight while holding one.
  4. Fish between fights. Once you have the Haunted Rod from Echo's quest, pier fishing pays 20–40 per catch and can even pull up a Hexed Pumpkin Egg.
  5. Drop everything for events. Blood Moons and Pumpkin Collection Events are worth more than anything else.

Blood Moon and the Pumpkin Collection Event

Blood Moon (2x Candy Corn)

When the water turns red and a banner announces x2 Candy Corn, a Blood Moon has started. It lasts about five minutes. Spend every second killing Soul Phantoms and returning your rarest eggs, since all sources pay double.

Pumpkin Collection Event (1,000 for a win)

This competitive event takes place in the Graveyard. Pumpkins spawn anywhere in the dimension, and you catch them and deliver them to the Pumpkin Patch. Your placement decides your payout, and a win is worth 1,000 Candy Corn, almost half an egg. High Speed helps a lot here.

Halloween Dimension Egg: Price and Odds

The Halloween Event Shop in the safe zone sells the limited-time Halloween Dimension Egg for 2,250 Candy Corn. Here are the hatch odds reported from the shop screen:

Halloween Dimension Map Quick Reference

Knowing the map makes every Candy Corn run shorter. Here's where everything is:

  • Graveyard: Echo the ghost dog, near three mausoleums. Pumpkin Collection Event.
  • Haunted Swamp: Big Pumpkin Altar, Gilbert's Hut and the fishing pier.
  • Abandoned Manor: rare egg spawns around the grounds.
  • Safe zone: return portal and Halloween Event Shop.
  • Phantom Express station: right side of the map.

Each biome has six new pets, 18 in total, tracked under a limited tab in your Pet Index.

Is Steal an Egg Candy Corn Worth Grinding?

If you like collecting, yes. The Halloween Dimension pets are limited, and the 0.5% Soul Phantom is one of the rarest event pets in the game. If you only care about income, focus on your main-world pets and treat Candy Corn as a bonus while you hunt monsters. Either way, spend all your Candy Corn before the event ends, since event currencies usually have no use once the season is over.

Steal an Egg FAQ

What is the fastest way to get Candy Corn in Steal an Egg?

Hunt Soul Phantoms (65 each), return high-rarity eggs (up to 100 each), fish with the Haunted Rod, and farm hard during Blood Moons, which double all Candy Corn.

How much is the Halloween Dimension Egg in Steal an Egg?

The Halloween Dimension Egg costs 2,250 Candy Corn at the Halloween Event Shop in the safe zone.

How do you enter the Halloween Dimension in Steal an Egg?

Walk through the glowing Halloween portal next to the Trails Shop at spawn. Some guides report a Speed requirement of around 2.5M.

What does a Blood Moon do in Steal an Egg?

A Blood Moon turns the water red and gives x2 Candy Corn from every source for about five minutes.
