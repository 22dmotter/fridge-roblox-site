The Forge Christmas Event: Santa's Quests, Xmas Tickets and the Christmas Pickaxe

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

The Forge Christmas event of 2025 ran on the Frostspire Expanse alongside the Winter Expansion, and Fridge posted three guides on finishing it fast. The event has ended, but this page keeps a full record of how it worked, checked against the Forge Wiki, in case it returns this December.

Watch Fridge's Christmas Event Videos

Fridge covered this topic in 2 full videos and 1 Short uploaded between December 25, 2025 and December 26, 2025. They are all embedded here, so you can watch and follow the written steps side by side.

The Shorts play in a vertical player. Tap one to watch it here, or open it on YouTube.

Video date check: The Forge gets updates often. The newest is version 1.09, Halloween & Guilds, from October 20, 2026. Fridge's videos show the game as it was when he filmed them. The written steps on this page match the game as of November 11, 2026, and were checked against the Forge Wiki.

Is the Christmas Event Still Live?

No. The Forge Wiki lists Santa as "Limited-Time (Event Ended)". The current seasonal update is version 1.09, Halloween & Guilds, released October 20, 2026. If a 2026 Christmas event goes live, we'll update this page, so check back in December.

Where Santa Was

Santa sat on a golden throne on a festive boat behind the player spawn, by the harbor on the Frostspire Expanse (Island 3, level 70+). He ran the event's quests and its shop.

Santa's Three Daily Quests

Christmas event quests (Forge Wiki)
QuestObjectiveXmas Tickets
Santa's Workshop Warm-UpForge 3 armor and 3 weapons140
Mine RocksMine 50 Icy Pebbles120
Santa's Pest ControlKill 25 Crystal Spiders and 5 Diamond Spiders160

The quests reset 24 hours after you completed them, so doing all three every day earned 420 Xmas Tickets. Fridge's "farm points fast" video shows his order. The forging quest was the quickest if you had spare ore, and the spiders in the Frost Webs cave covered the third.

What the Tickets Bought

  • Christmas Pickaxe: 3,600 Xmas Tickets. It has 550 Mine Power, +72% Luck and 3 rune slots, with Exotic rarity. That's about 9 days of all three quests.
  • Christmas Potion (event only): +25% Mining Speed, +20% Mining Damage and +25% Luck for 900 seconds.
  • Some Frostspire forge recipes were tied to the Santa event, according to Pro Game Guides.

If you missed it, the Prismatic Pickaxe matches the Christmas Pickaxe's +72% Luck with more Mine Power, and it's always available.

How to Prepare If It Returns

If the next The Forge Christmas event uses the same format, you can get ahead now:

  • Stockpile ore so you can finish the forging quest straight away each day.
  • Unlock the Frostspire Expanse (level 70+) before December, because the 2025 event lived there.
  • Upgrade your weapon so the spider quest goes fast. Crystal and Diamond Spiders live in the Frost Webs cave.
  • Log in daily. The quests reset 24 hours after you finish them, so missing a day costs 420 tickets.
