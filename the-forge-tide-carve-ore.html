Tide Carve Ore in The Forge: Multiplier and Price

Last checked: November 14, 2026

Tide Carve is an Epic ore from the Frostspire Expanse. Sources agree it has a 3.5x multiplier, an $88.13 sell price, and no trait. Confirmed nodes are Icy Boulders, Small Ice Crystals, and Medium Ice Crystals. They do not agree on the drop chance, so that field is left blank on this page.

How to Get Tide Carve

Mine Icy Boulders, Small Ice Crystals, or Medium Ice Crystals in the Frostspire Expanse. Small Ice Crystal is listed by all four sources, Icy Boulder by the official wiki, Game8, and BloxInformer, and Medium Ice Crystal by the official wiki, Destructoid, and BloxInformer. They do not agree on the drop chance (see below).

What Tide Carve Is Good For

Tide Carve has no trait, a 3.5x multiplier, and an $88.13 sell price. See the Island 3 / Frostspire Expanse guide and the forging guide for how later-game ores fit the crafting loop.

Sources disagree:
  • Drop chance: the official Forge Wiki lists 1/163. Game8 (February 22, 2026), BloxInformer, and Destructoid (January 8, 2026) list 1/980. Because the official wiki does not agree with the other three sources, this page shows a dash (—) instead of choosing one.
  • Destructoid lists the multiplier as 4.7x. The official Forge Wiki, Game8 (February 22, 2026), and BloxInformer agree on 3.5x, so this page uses 3.5x.
  • The official wiki ores list also names All Red Crystals and Heart Of The Island. The official item page lists Small Red Crystal, The Peak, and Raven Cave, and it does not repeat the Frostspire Expanse header used on other World 3 pages. Game8 still lists the area as Frostspire Expanse. Those extra nodes are not confirmed by the other sources, so they are not listed under Obtained from.
