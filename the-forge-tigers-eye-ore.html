Tiger's Eye in The Forge: Stats and Trait

Last checked: November 15, 2026

Tiger's Eye is an Epic ore on the official Bamboo Forest list. Sources agree it is obtained from Bamboo Rock, with a 1-in-210 drop chance, a 6x multiplier, and a $135.94 sell price. Its confirmed trait is Increases Attack Speed by 7.5%; Increases Physical Damage by 5%; Increases Movement Speed by 5%; Decreases max Health by 10% (Weapons).

How to Get Tiger's Eye

On the official Forge Wiki list, Tiger's Eye is a Bamboo Forest ore, obtained from Bamboo Rock. the official Forge Wiki, Game8, BloxInformer, and Pocket Tactics all mention this ore. The confirmed drop chance on this page is 1-in-210.

What Tiger's Eye Is Good For

Tiger's Eye's confirmed trait is Increases Attack Speed by 7.5%; Increases Physical Damage by 5%; Increases Movement Speed by 5%; Decreases max Health by 10% (Weapons). It has a 6x multiplier and a $135.94 sell price. See the forging guide and the pickaxe progression guide for how later-island ores fit the crafting loop.
