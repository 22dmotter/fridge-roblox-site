How to Gain TONS of Speed FAST in Steal an Egg

By Fridge · Published October 21, 2026 · Last updated: November 10, 2026

If you keep getting caught by guardians or can't reach the next biome, you need more Speed. This guide shows you how to get speed in Steal an Egg as fast as possible: the treadmill upgrade path, which trails to buy (and which to skip), the Speed you need for every biome, and the little timing tricks that add up to billions. It's the same method Fridge uses in his speed video.

Why Speed Is Everything in Steal an Egg

Speed gates every biome. You can walk into a zone without enough Speed, but the guardian will catch you on the way back and knock the egg out of your hands. Hitting the right number before you enter is the difference between a clean steal and a wasted run. The whole game runs on one loop:

  1. Train on the treadmill to increase Speed.
  2. Reach a new biome and steal an egg.
  3. Carry it back to your base and hatch a pet.
  4. Your pets generate cash.
  5. Spend that cash on treadmill upgrades and trails. Repeat.
No rebirths (for now): Steal an Egg doesn't currently have a Rebirth system, so don't hold back cash waiting for one. Spend it on Speed.

Step 1: Unlock and Upgrade the Treadmill

The treadmill sits right in front of your base and is the only unlimited Speed source in the game. It unlocks after the $1,000 garden upgrade. Every step on it gives Speed, and each upgrade raises how much you get per step.

The named treadmill tiers climb from the base treadmill through Celebrity, Hacker, Demonic and Angelic versions, with even stronger treadmills for late-game gates. Early tiers give a few hundred Speed per step, while mid-game ones like the Hacker treadmill jump into the thousands. Always upgrade the treadmill the moment you can afford the next tier, because every future minute of training gets better.

Step 2: Buy the Best Trail You Can Afford

Trails aren't just cosmetic. They multiply the Speed you get from the treadmill, and that's the secret to fast progress. Only your equipped trail counts, so buying every tier on the way up wastes cash. Save and jump straight to the highest trail you can afford.

Order matters: buy the trail first, then start a long training session. Every point you earn after equipping a better trail is worth more.

Speed Tricks Most Players Miss

Knowing how to get speed in Steal an Egg is mostly about the treadmill and trails, but these extra habits add up fast:

Train during night cycles

You can't steal eggs at night, so that downtime is the perfect time to stand on the treadmill and bank Speed before the next reset.

Claim the free rewards panel

Liking the game, favoriting it and joining the creator group pays out a big one-time Speed gift. Since there are no Steal an Egg codes, this is the closest thing to a free code you'll get.

Farm the deepest biome you can escape

Higher-zone eggs hatch pets that earn far more cash, and cash buys Speed. Don't stay in a biome you've outgrown.

Use boosters and events

Limited-time shops have sold 2x treadmill boosters and 1.25x speed boosts, and Admin Abuse events hand out massive temporary Speed. Stack a booster with your best trail for the biggest jump.

What Doesn't Work

  • The Wi-Fi purchase trick. Cutting your internet mid-purchase doesn't give a free Speed gamepass. The Robux still leaves your account.
  • "Infinite speed" glitches. What people call infinite speed is just a top trail plus a maxed treadmill stacked together.
  • Scripts. They risk bans and malware. Not worth it.

Putting Your Speed to Work

Once you're fast, use it. Speed helps you snag rare egg spawns before other players and makes your Halloween Dimension runs much smoother. Our Steal an Egg Candy Corn guide shows the fastest farming loop, and the how to win races in Steal an Egg guide covers the race event (fun fact: in races, everyone drives at the same speed, so skill wins).

Steal an Egg FAQ

What is the fastest way to get speed in Steal an Egg?

Upgrade your treadmill whenever you can, equip the best trail you can afford before training, and train during night cycles when you can't steal eggs.

Do trails increase speed in Steal an Egg?

Yes. Trails multiply the Speed you gain from the treadmill, from 1.5x for the Gray trail up to 14x for Divine and higher on newer trails.

How much speed do you need for each biome in Steal an Egg?

Lake needs 900, Desert 10,000, Jungle 40,000, Snow 170,000, Volcano 700,000, Abyss Ocean 2.5M, and late biomes need billions.

Does Steal an Egg have rebirths?

No. Steal an Egg doesn't currently have a Rebirth system, so put your cash into treadmill upgrades and trails.
