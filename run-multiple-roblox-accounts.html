How to Run Multiple Roblox Accounts at Once (2026 Guide)

By Fridge · Published November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Read this first (ToS and ban risk): Before you learn how to run multiple Roblox accounts at once, know that Roblox does not officially support running several Roblox Player clients on one computer. Having more than one account is fine, but tools that bypass the client's limits, modify the Roblox app, or automate play can break Roblox's Terms of Use and Community Standards and can lead to warnings, bans or termination, including action against your other accounts. Individual games can also ban alt accounts under their own rules. Everything below is ordered from safest to riskiest, and you use third-party tools at your own risk.

Want to know how to run multiple Roblox accounts at once? It depends on what you mean. If you just want to hop between accounts quickly, Roblox has an official Account Switcher built in. If you want two accounts playing at the same time, the only reliable and fully supported way is one account per device. This guide covers both, explains Error 264, and is honest about what third-party multi-instance tools really involve in 2026.

Method 1: The Official Roblox Account Switcher (Safest)

Roblox's own Account Switcher lets you keep up to five accounts signed in on one device and swap between them without typing passwords every time. It doesn't run them at the same time, but for most people it's exactly what they need.

  1. Log in to Roblox in your browser and go to the Home page.
  2. Click the gear icon in the top right.
  3. Select Switch Account.
  4. Choose Add Account and sign in to your other account.
  5. From now on, use the same menu to jump between saved accounts.

According to Roblox Support, the switcher works for users 13 and older and isn't available on accounts with parent privileges. Saved accounts are stored privately on that device only, so only use it on a personal computer, never a school or shared one.

Method 2: One Account Per Device (Best for Playing Together)

If you want two accounts in the same game at the same time, the safest approach is simple: run each account on its own device. For example, your main on a PC and your alt on a phone, tablet or console.

  1. Install Roblox only from an official source: roblox.com, the Microsoft Store, the App Store, Google Play or your console store.
  2. Sign in to a different account on each device.
  3. Join the same experience from each device. Use the profile Join button or a private server (see our how to join someone on Roblox without being friends guide).

This is the method Roblox's own guidance points toward, and it carries no modified-client risk.

What Is Roblox Error 264?

Roblox Support describes Error Code 264 as what happens when the same account tries to access the same game from more than one device at once. It's not a ban and it's not dangerous. To fix it:

  • Leave the game on your other device, or close Roblox there entirely.
  • Make sure each device is signed in to a different account.
  • Rejoin after a few seconds.

For Developers: Studio's Server & Clients Test

If you're building a game and need several players to test with, Roblox Studio has a supported option. In the Test tab, choose a multi-client test (Server & Clients) and pick how many clients to simulate. These aren't real accounts in a public server, but they're perfect for testing multiplayer features without any risk.

Method 3: Third-Party Multi-Instance Tools (Risky)

Ban-risk warning: Roblox staff have said they don't officially support running multiple player clients at once. In their 2025 announcement on modified clients, Roblox said tools that don't modify the client binary weren't affected by that enforcement wave, but tools that do modify it would trigger consequences. Roblox also gives no guarantee about third-party tools, and its anti-cheat changes over time. There is no "safe" multi-instance tool.

Roblox Account Manager

Fridge does not recommend unofficial multi-instance software, does not host it, and does not provide setup steps for it. The best-known name that comes up in searches is Roblox Account Manager. If you research it, these are facts about the project, not an endorsement:

  • The original repository is github.com/ic3w0lf22/Roblox-Account-Manager. As of November 2026 it's archived (read-only), and its last release dates to 2024, so it's no longer maintained.
  • Its own README says its Multi Roblox option is off by default because a Roblox anti-cheat developer said using multiple clients "may be considered as malicious behavior."
  • The README also warns: never generate an "rbx-player link" for anyone, because it can let them use your account.
  • Copies and "continued" forks exist. Fridge hasn't verified them, and fake downloads are a common way to steal accounts. Never download from YouTube descriptions, Discord DMs or file-hosting sites.

Bloxstrap no longer does this

Bloxstrap used to have a multi-instance option, but its developers removed it in v2.10.0, saying Roblox broke it and is "cracking down on multi-instance launching." See our Bloxstrap guide for what it still does.

Emulators and old tricks

Android emulators with multi-instance features and the old "browser plus Microsoft Store app" trick come up a lot. Both are unofficial, can break after any update, and use a lot of RAM and CPU. Treat them as experiments, not reliable methods.

What You Should Never Do

  • Never use injectors, "mutex bypass" scripts or modified Roblox executables. These count as modified clients and can get you banned.
  • Never enter your password into an unofficial launcher or a site that claims to "link" accounts.
  • Never use alts to evade a ban or get around a game's rules. That's a separate violation.
  • Never use accounts to bot or farm. Roblox actively removes bot accounts. Read our how to AFK in Roblox safely guide for what's allowed.

Performance Tips for Running Two Devices

  • Lower graphics quality on the weaker device so both stay smooth.
  • Use a wired connection or 5 GHz Wi-Fi if both devices share one network.
  • Close background apps on each device.
  • Our fix Roblox lag guide covers every setting that helps.

If one of your accounts ever gets compromised while you're juggling logins, follow our Roblox account recovery guide right away.

Roblox FAQ

Can you run multiple Roblox accounts at once?

Roblox doesn't officially support multiple player clients on one computer. The supported way to play two accounts at the same time is one account per device. The Account Switcher lets you swap between up to five accounts on one device.

Is Roblox Account Manager safe?

It is a third-party tool, not made by Roblox. The original GitHub repository is archived, its Multi Roblox option is disabled by default because of anti-cheat concerns, and fake copies are common. Using it is at your own risk.

What does Error 264 mean on Roblox?

Error Code 264 means the same account tried to join the same game from more than one device at once. Leave the game on the other device or use a different account there.

Can I get banned for having alt accounts on Roblox?

Owning more than one account isn't against the rules by itself, but using alts to break rules, evade bans, bot or farm can lead to action on all linked accounts. Some games ban alts under their own rules.
