The Forge Races: What Every Race Does (Golem, Minotaur, Dwarf and More)

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Fridge made a quick Short on what each popular race does. This page puts all of them together with the full list of The Forge races, their rarity, odds and every trait, as listed on the Forge Wiki on November 11, 2026.

Video date check: The Forge gets updates often. The newest is version 1.09, Halloween & Guilds, from October 20, 2026. Fridge's videos show the game as it was when he filmed them. The written steps on this page match the game as of November 11, 2026, and were checked against the Forge Wiki.

The Races Fridge Covered

  • Golem (Legendary, 1.25%): Tank gives +30% Health and +30% Size but -10% Movement Speed. Heavy Hitter adds +15% Attack Speed with heavy weapons, and Stone Heart gives a 50% chance to cut incoming damage by 25%. It's the tank pick.
  • Minotaur (Legendary, 1.25%): Beast gives +20% Health, +10% Height and +20% Width and Depth, with -10% Movement Speed and -10% Stamina. Bull's Fury enters Rage Mode below 50% Health for +30% Movement Speed and Physical Damage.
  • Dwarf (Epic, 4%): Gifted Miner gives +15% Mining Damage and +5% Better Forge. Critical Mining gives a 20% chance to deal +50% damage to rocks. The downside is -20% Height and -5% Movement Speed. The Forge Wiki's guide calls it the best race for mining.
  • Shadow (Epic, 4%): Shadow Pact gives +15% Movement Speed, +10% Attack Speed, +10% Stamina and +5% Physical Damage for -10% Health. Phantom Step gives a 15% chance to dodge an attack by turning into a shadow.
  • Dragonborn (Legendary, 1.25%): +20% Health, +10% Height and +12% Physical Damage. Dragon's Breath has a 40% chance to burn enemies for 30% of base weapon damage per second for 3 seconds.

Fridge's Every New Race Short covers the races added in the Winter Expansion on December 25, 2025. More races have been added since. Version 1.08.1 on October 10, 2026, was titled "New Races & Questboard", so the table below is the current full list.

All The Forge races come only from rerolls. None can be bought directly, and luck boosts don't change the odds.

Best Race for Each Playstyle

Not all The Forge races suit every build. The Forge Wiki's community guide gives these picks:

  • Mining: Dwarf
  • Fighting: Demon
  • Buying gear: Goblin (12% discount)
  • Essences: Angel
  • Best overall: ArchAngel. See our Archangel race guide.

If you only have free rerolls, a Legendary like Golem or Dragonborn is a strong place to stop. Mythicals are 0.25% each, so chasing one can take hundreds of rolls. Learn where to roll in our reroll guide.

The Forge FAQ

What is the best race in The Forge?

ArchAngel is the best overall, according to the Forge Wiki's guide. Demon is best for fighting and Dwarf for mining.

What does the Golem race do in The Forge?

It gives +30% Health and +30% Size, +15% Attack Speed with heavy weapons, and a 50% chance to reduce incoming damage by 25%. It also has -10% Movement Speed.

How many races are in The Forge?

22, from Common Human to the two Relic races, ArchAngel and Kitsune, on the Forge Wiki as of November 11, 2026.
