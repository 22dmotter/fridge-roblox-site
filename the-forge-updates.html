The Forge Update History: Every Major Update, and Is The Forge Dying?

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

In December 2025, Fridge predicted that the next The Forge update would be "insane" and bring Orcs. In January 2026, he asked whether The Forge was dying. Ten months later, here's how both calls held up, with the full update history from the Forge Wiki.

Watch Fridge's The Forge News Videos

Fridge covered this topic in 1 full video and 1 Short uploaded between December 17, 2025 and January 6, 2026. They are all embedded here, so you can watch and follow the written steps side by side.

The Shorts play in a vertical player. Tap one to watch it here, or open it on YouTube.

Video date check: The Forge gets updates often. The newest is version 1.09, Halloween & Guilds, from October 20, 2026. Fridge's videos show the game as it was when he filmed them. The written steps on this page match the game as of November 11, 2026, and were checked against the Forge Wiki.

Did the Orcs Arrive?

Yes. Fridge's December 17, 2025 Short teased Orcs. The current race list includes the Orc (Rare, 7.5%: +10% Physical Damage and +15% Health) and the Orc Lord (Mythical, 0.25%: +40% Physical Damage and a Titanic Earthquake slam). Common and Elite Orcs also appear as enemies on the wiki. See them in our races guide.

Is The Forge Dying?

Fridge's January 6, 2026 video looked at why players thought the game was losing steam after its huge launch. Here's where it stands now:

  • Players: about 22,800 online on November 11, 2026, according to Roblox's own game data.
  • Visits: over 1.04 billion.
  • Updates: five major updates since that video, including a whole new island, with the latest less than a month ago.

The game has cooled from its launch peak, and the gap between May and September 2026 was its longest without a major update. Still, 22,800 concurrent players is a large audience for a Roblox RPG. In early November 2026, the developers handed out apology codes after downtime: SORRYFORSHUTDOWNN!, AIDASAYSSORRY and FORGECOMEBACK. Grab them on our The Forge codes page.

What to Expect Next

Updates have landed every few weeks to few months, usually with new codes. Each update tends to add a new area, new ores and new recipes. Bookmark our codes page, which we refresh regularly, and the other The Forge walkthroughs in Roblox Guides.
