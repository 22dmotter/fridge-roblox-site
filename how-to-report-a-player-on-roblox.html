How to Report a Player on Roblox: Official Steps for Games, Chat and Profiles

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Here's how to report a player on Roblox using the official Report Abuse tools, taken from Roblox Support's "How to Report Rule Violations" article. The most important rule: report the specific chat, message or game where the problem happened. Roblox says a report from a profile page alone doesn't show moderators what the player did. Why Report the Content, Not Just the Profile

Report Abuse sends the report straight to Roblox moderators and helps them track repeat offenders. Reporting from inside the game or chat attaches what actually happened. So report while it's happening, from where it happened.

How to Report a Player in a Roblox Game

  1. Open the menu. Select the Roblox menu button at the upper left of the screen.
  2. Open Report. Select the flag icon next to the player's name, or the Report tab at the top of the menu.
  3. Choose Person. For "Experience or Person?", select Person.
  4. Pick the player from the "Which Person?" menu.
  5. Pick a reason from "Reason for Abuse?", like bullying, scamming or inappropriate content.
  6. Add details in the Short Description box: what they said or did, and when.
  7. Select Submit.

If the game itself breaks the rules, use the same Report tab and choose Experience instead.

How to Report a Player in Roblox App Chat

On a computer (browser)

  1. Select the gear icon at the upper right of the chat window to open Chat Details.
  2. Select the three dots next to the username, then Report.
  3. Select the red Report button, fill out the form and select Report Abuse.

On mobile

  1. Tap the information (i) icon at the upper right of the chat.
  2. Pick the user from the participant list (or tap the … next to their name).
  3. Tap Report Abuse and fill out the form.

How to Report a Roblox Game, Item or Other Content

  • A whole experience: use the red Report Abuse button at the lower right of the Description box on the game's About page.
  • A Marketplace item, game pass or model: select the three dots at the upper right of the item box, then Report item.

Roblox also has separate legal reporting forms for some regions, including the EU (Digital Services Act), the UK (Online Safety Act), Australia, Brazil and California. They're linked from the same Support article.

Block the Player Too

Reporting doesn't stop someone messaging you right away, so also block them. Blocking stops them chatting with or contacting you. Then tighten who can reach you: our parental controls guide covers chat limits, and Trusted Friends explains who gets fewer chat filters.

What Happens After You Report Someone?

  • Moderators review the report against the Roblox Community Standards.
  • Action can range from a warning to a ban. Don't expect full details of what happened to the other account; moderation records are private.
  • The Report Abuse form doesn't show your name to the player you report.
  • Don't spam reports. One clear report with details beats ten empty ones.

If you got moderated after a false report, check your account status and appeal there. If someone stole your account, see how to recover a Roblox account.

What You Should Report

  • Scams, like "free Robux" links or fake trades.
  • Bullying, harassment or hate speech.
  • Asking for personal info or to move to another app.
  • Exploiting or cheating in a game.
  • Inappropriate avatars, items or games.
Danger first: if someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Roblox reports aren't a replacement for that.

Roblox FAQ

How do I report a player on Roblox?

In the game, open the Roblox menu, select the flag next to their name or the Report tab, choose Person, pick the player and reason, add details and submit.

Can I report someone from their profile?

You can, but Roblox says moderators can't see what they did. Report the specific chat, message or game instead.

Does the player see who reported them?

The Report Abuse form sends your report to Roblox moderators, not to the player.

What happens after I report someone on Roblox?

Moderators review it against the Community Standards and can warn or ban the account. Block the player in the meantime.

How do I report a Roblox game?

Use the red Report Abuse button in the game's Description box, or the Report tab with Experience selected while playing.
