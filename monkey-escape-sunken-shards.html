All 9 Sunken Shard Locations in +1 Speed Monkey Escape

By Fridge · Published August 8, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Looking for all the Monkey Escape shards? There are nine Sunken Shards hidden in +1 Speed Monkey Escape, and collecting every one unlocks a free x2 Speed Treadmill that doubles your training gains. Two shards are in the main lobby and the other seven are spread across the stages. A few require a deliberate drop into a gap. This guide shows exactly where each one is.

Why the Sunken Shards Are Worth It

In +1 Speed Monkey Escape, Speed is everything, and the x2 Speed Treadmill doubles how fast you build it while training. It's permanent and completely free, so getting it early saves you hours of grinding. Just as in our Steal an Egg speed guide, a better treadmill means every training minute after it counts for more.

Lobby Shards (1 and 2)

  1. Shard 1: after spawning, turn left and head to the leaderboards. There are three boards, and the shard is tucked directly behind the Top Robux one.
  2. Shard 2: still in the lobby, find the big vertical jump booster. Build up speed, launch off it, and land on the ledge to the left. The shard sits in the corner.

Stage 2 to Stage 4 Shards (3, 4 and 5)

  1. Shard 3 (Stage 2): go to the center and find the small pillar on the left. The shard hangs below the platform, so drop down and grab it as you fall. The fall kills you, but the shard still counts.
  2. Shard 4 (Stage 3): at the end of the stage, go right and clear the right-hand booster with plenty of speed. Drop behind the large wooden block to grab it.
  3. Shard 5 (Stage 4): use the vertical boosters at the start to reach the top level, then run left to the end of the platform just before the Stage 5 entrance.

Stage 6 Shard (6)

Jump onto the first center platform, then straight onto the second. Move to the edge and look below the side of the middle cube. The shard sits in the water gap near the swimming shark. Drop for it. Like shard 3, it registers even if the drop kills you.

Stage 7 Shards (7 and 8)

  1. Shard 7: head to the three green platforms near the end of the stage. The shard is on the middle one, hidden on the lower level behind a wall section.
  2. Shard 8: right next to it, the last green platform has a small side ledge. Reach it with a jump pad or by swinging across. The shard is in the upper corner.
Most missed: players often grab shard 7 and leave without shard 8 because they're so close together. Double-check before you move on.

Stage 9 Shard (9)

Jump across the center platforms while dodging the tsunami waves. On the third center platform, check the right-side corner behind the main path for the final Sunken Shard.

Confirming Your Monkey Escape Shards Reward

Once you've touched all nine shards, the x2 Speed Treadmill becomes available for training. If your count is off, the usual culprits are:

  • Skipping the two lobby shards before heading into the stages.
  • Missing one of the two Stage 7 shards.
  • Not touching shard 3 or 6 during the drop. Aim to pass right through them.

Tips for Collecting Monkey Escape Shards Fast

  • Train a little first. Shards 2, 4 and 8 need extra speed to reach ledges.
  • Don't fear the drops. Dying on shards 3 and 6 doesn't undo your pickup.
  • Do the lobby first so you never have to backtrack.

+1 Speed Monkey Escape FAQ

How many shards are in +1 Speed Monkey Escape?

There are nine Sunken Shards: two in the main lobby and one each on Stages 2, 3, 4, 6 and 9, plus two on Stage 7.

What do you get for all 9 shards in Monkey Escape?

Collecting all nine Sunken Shards unlocks a free, permanent x2 Speed Treadmill that doubles your Speed gains while training.

Which stages have no shards in Monkey Escape?

Stage 5 and Stage 8 have no Sunken Shards, so you can run straight through them.

Do shards count if you die in Monkey Escape?

Yes. Shards 3 and 6 need a drop that can kill your character, but the pickup still counts once you touch the shard.
