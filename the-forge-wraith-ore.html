Wraith Ore in The Forge: Drop Chance and Price

Last checked: November 15, 2026

Wraith is an Epic ore on the official Bamboo Forest list. Sources agree it is obtained from Bamboo Rock, Bamboo Boulder, with a 1-in-142 drop chance, a 5.75x multiplier, and a $134.06 sell price. It has no confirmed forge trait.

How to Get Wraith

On the official Forge Wiki list, Wraith is a Bamboo Forest ore, obtained from Bamboo Rock, Bamboo Boulder. the official Forge Wiki, Game8, BloxInformer, and Pocket Tactics all mention this ore. The official wiki item page also lists holy tree. This page follows the official wiki list for the location label (Bamboo Forest). The confirmed drop chance on this page is 1-in-142. The official item page also lists Holy Tree (Hana Pebble). This page follows the official wiki list for the location label (Bamboo Forest).

What Wraith Is Good For

Wraith has no confirmed forge trait. It has a 5.75x multiplier and a $134.06 sell price. See the forging guide and the pickaxe progression guide for how later-island ores fit the crafting loop.
