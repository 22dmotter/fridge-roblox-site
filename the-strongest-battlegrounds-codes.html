The Strongest Battlegrounds Codes (November 2026): No Reward Codes

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Searching for The Strongest Battlegrounds codes? As of last checked November 11, 2026, TSB has no reward codes and no redeem box. The "codes" you see online are Roblox sound IDs for custom kill sounds, and they only work if you own the Kill Sound game pass (199 Robux). The Strongest Battlegrounds by Yielding Arts was the #20 most-played game on Roblox on November 11, with about 53,000 players online.

Expired codes

There are no expired reward codes, because TSB has never had a redeem box. Gaming Route (checked November 6) and Timesaver both confirm there are no free-reward codes, and the official game page mentions none.

What "TSB Codes" Really Are: Kill Sound IDs

Trackers like Roblox Den list nearly 200 "codes" for this game, but they're all audio IDs. A kill sound plays when you finish an opponent. Here are a few that Gaming Route cross-checked against Roblox's public asset records on November 6, 2026:

Gaming Route found that some IDs on long lists aren't public audio or aren't sounds at all, usually because of a typo. For example, the commonly listed Open Hood ID ending in 953 doesn't exist; use the one ending in 956.

How to Set a Kill Sound in TSB

  1. Open The Strongest Battlegrounds on Roblox.
  2. Buy the Kill Sound pass (199 Robux) from the game's store. It's a one-time purchase.
  3. Open Settings (gear icon) and choose Customize Kill Sound.
  4. Type a sound ID into the box and confirm. A new ID replaces the old one.
Keep it clean: the fan wiki warns that players can be reported and banned for inappropriate sounds. And no TSB "code" gives free spins, emotes or auras; sites that claim otherwise are wrong.

TSB Cape Codes

"Cape codes" are image IDs for the custom cape that comes with the VIP pass (299 Robux). Set the cape's color and picture under Customize Custom Cape in Settings, using any image ID from the Roblox Creator Store.

TSB Halloween Event End Date

The game's official Halloween event listing ends on Sunday, November 22, 2026 at 9 PM ET (November 23, 02:00 UTC), according to Gaming Route. The event adds a spooky map, a carnival showtime every 30 minutes and a Candy Shop where Candy buys Halloween rolls for event emotes, accessories and auras. Spend your Candy before it ends.

The Strongest Battlegrounds FAQ

Are The Strongest Battlegrounds codes real?

The kill sound IDs are real Roblox audio IDs, but they aren't reward codes. They only work with the Kill Sound pass.

Does The Strongest Battlegrounds have codes?

Not reward codes. As of November 11, 2026, TSB has no redeem box. The codes people share are sound IDs for custom kill sounds.

Are TSB kill sound codes free?

The IDs are free to copy, but you need the Kill Sound game pass (199 Robux) to use them.

How do you use kill sound IDs in TSB?

Open Settings, choose Customize Kill Sound, and enter a sound ID. You need the Kill Sound pass first.

When does the TSB Halloween event end?

The official listing ends November 22, 2026 at 9 PM ET (November 23, 02:00 UTC).

Why doesn't my TSB sound ID work?

Some listed IDs aren't public audio or contain a typo. Use an ID that's been checked against Roblox's asset records.
