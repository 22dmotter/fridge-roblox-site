How to Unlock FPS in Roblox in 2026 (Official Max Frame Rate)

By Fridge · Published November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Safety note: Roblox now has an official frame-rate setting, so you don't need third-party FPS unlockers. Old unlockers changed an internal value that Roblox says isn't a stable API, and the FPS Fast Flag isn't on Roblox's 2025 allowlist, so it no longer works. Tools that inject into or modify the Roblox client can violate Roblox's rules and lead to account action. Stick to the in-game option below.

Here's how to unlock FPS in Roblox the official way. For years players relied on separate "FPS unlocker" programs to get past Roblox's 60 FPS cap. Since 2024, Roblox has its own Maximum Frame Rate setting with options up to 240 FPS, and older unlocker methods have been phased out.

Method 1: Roblox's Maximum Frame Rate Setting (Official)

Roblox announced the setting on the Developer Forum in May 2024. It's available in the Roblox desktop app on Windows.

  1. Join any Roblox experience.
  2. Press Esc to open the Roblox menu.
  3. Open the Settings tab.
  4. Find Maximum Frame Rate.
  5. Pick 60, 120, 144 or 240. Match it to your monitor's refresh rate for the best result.
  6. Close the menu. The setting saves for future sessions.

Roblox explained there's no option above 240 because its physics simulation runs at 240 Hz. Going higher wouldn't make the game respond faster.

How to Check Your FPS in Roblox

  • Press Ctrl + Shift + F5 (Shift + ⌘ + F5 on Mac) in-game to open Roblox's debug stats overlay, which includes FPS.
  • Press Ctrl + Alt + F7 for the Performance Stats bar (memory, CPU, GPU and ping).
  • Press F9 to open the Developer Console for deeper details. Press the same keys again to hide them.

If you set 240 but only see 70, your PC is the limit, not the cap. Lower graphics quality (see our how to fix Roblox lag guide) to get closer to your target.

What Happened to Roblox FPS Unlockers?

The popular standalone "rbxfpsunlocker" tool and similar programs used to change Roblox's frame-rate cap from outside the game. A few things changed:

  • The official setting arrived (2024), and Roblox recommended it over external tools, noting those tools depend on internal values that can change without warning.
  • Roblox's anti-cheat blocks many programs that touch the Roblox process.
  • Modified-client enforcement (2025) means tools that modify Roblox at runtime can lead to account action.

Method 2: Bloxstrap's FPS Option (Removed)

Bloxstrap used to include a "framerate limit" preset that set an FPS Fast Flag. In v2.10.0, Bloxstrap removed it, telling users to use the in-game setting, because Roblox's Fast Flag allowlist made it stop working. If an old guide tells you to type a frame-rate flag into Bloxstrap, it's outdated. Our Bloxstrap guide covers what Bloxstrap still does in 2026.

Method 3: Fast Flags (Mostly Not for FPS Anymore)

Since September 29, 2025, Roblox only honors local Fast Flags from a published allowlist. Flags not on the list are ignored. The frame-rate flag people used for unlocking isn't on it, so editing it has no effect. Roblox staff have said trying to bypass the allowlist counts as client modification, which is bannable. Some allowlisted flags can help performance indirectly, such as lowering texture quality, choosing a graphics API, or reducing grass distance, but always check the current list on the Roblox Developer Forum first.

Tips to Actually Hit High FPS

Knowing how to unlock FPS in Roblox only helps if your device can keep up. These tips close the gap:

  1. Plug in your laptop and use a high-performance power plan.
  2. Update your GPU drivers from NVIDIA, AMD or Intel.
  3. Lower Graphics Quality a few notches. You'll often double your FPS.
  4. Close heavy background apps like browsers with many tabs, recording software and launchers.
  5. Play in fullscreen (F11 toggles fullscreen on Windows).

Higher FPS matters most in fast games. In our Steal an Egg speed guide, smoother movement makes escaping much easier. If you want prettier visuals instead, read our Roblox shaders 2026 breakdown.

FPS on Mobile, Mac and Console

Roblox launched the Maximum Frame Rate setting on Windows first and said other platforms would come later. Availability varies by device, so if you don't see the option on Mac, mobile or console, that platform uses its own cap. Don't install "FPS unlocker" apps or modified APKs on phones, since those are modified clients.

Roblox FAQ

How do you unlock FPS in Roblox without an unlocker?

Open the Roblox menu with Esc, go to Settings and change Maximum Frame Rate to 120, 144 or 240. It's an official setting, so no download is needed.

What is the max FPS in Roblox?

The official setting goes up to 240 FPS. Roblox says it doesn't offer higher values because its physics runs at 240 Hz.

Does the Bloxstrap FPS unlocker still work?

No. Bloxstrap removed its framerate limit preset in v2.10.0 after Roblox introduced the Fast Flag allowlist. Use the in-game setting instead.

Can using an FPS unlocker get you banned on Roblox?

Roblox doesn't support external FPS unlockers, and tools that modify the client at runtime can lead to action under its modified-client rules. The official setting is the safe choice.
