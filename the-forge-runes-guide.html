How to Equip and Use Runes in The Forge (Best Runes for Your Pickaxe)

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Runes add extra traits to your gear. On a pickaxe, each Miner Shard can add up to 12% mining speed or 15% mine damage. Fridge has three Shorts on runes in The Forge: how to equip them, the best pickaxe runes, and the fastest mining setup for Island 3. Here they are with the current rune stats from the Forge Wiki.

What Runes Do

Runes are items that add special traits to weapons, armor and pickaxes. They come in three groups, according to the Forge Wiki:

  • Pickaxe runes change mining speed, damage or luck.
  • Weapon runes add damage effects such as freeze, poison, burn, explosions and lifesteal.
  • Armor runes improve survivability or movement.

Most runes drop from enemies. Each trait rolls inside a range, so two copies of the same rune can have different numbers.

How to Equip Runes

  1. Get a rune by defeating enemies, for example Delver Zombies for Miner Shards.
  2. Go to the Runemaker in any island hub. On Stonewake's Cross, he's at spawn across from the Enhancer.
  3. Pick the item with an open rune slot and choose the rune to socket in.
  4. Pay the fee. The Runemaker only attaches runes; he doesn't make them.

Pickaxes come with rune slots already: 3 on most mid-game picks and 4 on the top Frostspire pickaxes. Weapons and armor unlock slots through enhancing. Pro Game Guides warns that a failed enhancement can lose a slot, and the rune in it, so finish enhancing before you add runes. Socketing a rune into a pickaxe is the most expensive kind, so only rune the pickaxe you'll keep using.

Best Pickaxe Rune: Miner Shard

Miner Shard traits (Forge Wiki)
TraitRangeBest for
Luck5–16% overall luckRare ores
Yield5–15% chance of 1 extra oreBulk farming
Swift Mining4–12% faster miningSpeed
Mine Power8–15% extra mine damageBreaking tough rocks

Miner Shards drop from Delver Zombies, Bombers and the Golem boss. For the fastest Island 3 mining, which Fridge's December 28 Short covers, fill every slot with Miner Shards rolled for Swift Mining and Mine Power. Swap to Luck and Yield shards when you're farming rare ores.

Top Weapon Runes

  • Drain Edge (from the Reaper): heals 4–28% of damage dealt, capped at 8% of max Health per hit. Pro Game Guides calls it the best rune on the Forgotten Kingdom.
  • Frost Speck (spiders, Golem, Frostburn Oni): up to a 60% chance to freeze enemies for 3–5 seconds. It's the top Frostspire rune.
  • Blast Chip (Bomber): on-hit explosions of 20–80% weapon damage.
  • Venom Crumb (Blight Pyromancer): poison damage over time.
  • Chill Dust (Golem): slows enemy attacks and movement.

The Forge FAQ

How do you equip runes in The Forge?

Take the rune and an item with an open rune slot to the Runemaker in any island hub, then pay to socket it.

What is the best pickaxe rune in The Forge?

Miner Shard. Roll it for Swift Mining and Mine Power for speed, or for Luck and Yield for rare ores.

Where do you get Miner Shards?

From Delver Zombies, Bombers and the Golem boss.
