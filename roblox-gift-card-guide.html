Roblox Gift Card Guide: Where to Buy, Which to Pick and Scams to Avoid

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Where to Buy Roblox Gift Cards

Roblox sells cards directly at roblox.com/giftcards, and its FAQ lists these retailers (they vary by region):

  • Albertsons
  • Amazon
  • CVS
  • Dollar General
  • Dollar Tree
  • Family Dollar
  • Kroger
  • Target
  • Walgreens
  • Walmart

Buying straight from Roblox online has a perk: you get one extra bonus item by email, on top of the card's normal item (limit one bonus item per month per account).

Party Packs and Mini Packs are only sold in some regions and are Robux only, with no virtual item.

Getting the Most Value

  • Up to 25% more Robux. Roblox says redeeming a card for Roblox credit and then buying Robux gives up to 25% more Robux than buying on other platforms (like phone app stores).
  • A free virtual item. Most gift cards include an avatar item, limited to one per month per account. If you redeem three cards in one month, you still get one item.
  • Roblox Plus. Premium is now called Roblox Plus. Credit can buy a 3-, 6- or 12-month Plus subscription, and Plus 500, 1000 or 2000 add Robux every month.
  • Cards never expire and work from any currency; the value converts on redemption.
  • Cards can't be split. The full value goes to one account.

Planning a big purchase like a name change? Check how to change your Roblox username (it costs 1,000 Robux).

Roblox Credit vs Robux Codes

A Roblox Gift Card adds credit, which you can spend on Robux or Roblox Plus. Some digital codes sold by online retailers like Amazon, plus Party and Mini Pack codes, are Robux codes: they turn straight into Robux and can't be used for credit. If you redeem a card and see credit instead of Robux, that's normal. Spend it from the Robux screen.

Roblox Gift Card Scams to Avoid

Roblox's own warning: cards are only for Robux or Roblox credit. Never use one to pay a bill, a debt or a non-Roblox purchase, and never give the PIN to someone you don't know. Report scams at ftc.gov/giftcards.
  • "Free Robux" codes don't exist. Roblox says any person, site or video offering them is a scam.
  • Gift card generators are fake and often steal logins.
  • Discounted cards from strangers on Discord, social media or resale sites may be stolen or already used.
  • Tampered cards in stores. Check the PIN strip isn't scratched or re-glued, and buy from behind-the-counter displays when you can.
  • "Roblox support" asking for a PIN. Real Roblox staff never ask for one.

Tips for Parents

Gift cards are a good way to cap spending since there's no card on file. Pair them with Roblox parental controls to set monthly spending limits and notifications. Roblox's own safety hub at roblox.com/safety covers spending too.

There's no legit way to get free Robux, but there are free items. See how to get free items in Roblox and the Roblox codes hub.

Roblox FAQ

Where can I buy Roblox gift cards?

Online at roblox.com/giftcards, or at retailers including Albertsons, Amazon, CVS, Dollar General, Dollar Tree, Family Dollar, Kroger, Target, Walgreens and Walmart (varies by region).

Do Roblox gift cards expire?

No. Roblox says its gift cards don't expire.

Do Roblox gift cards give more Robux?

Yes. Redeeming for credit and buying Robux gives up to 25% more Robux than other platforms, per Roblox.

How many free items do I get from Roblox gift cards?

One virtual item per month per account, plus one bonus item per month if you buy online from Roblox.

What's in a Roblox Party Pack?

25 cards worth 200 Robux each. Party Packs are Robux only and don't include a virtual item.
