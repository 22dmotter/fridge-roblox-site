How to Get the Archangel Race in The Forge: Odds, Traits and Free Rolls

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

The ArchAngel is the race everyone wants in The Forge. This guide covers how to get the Archangel race, what it does, and what the odds mean for your rerolls. It pairs Fridge's "Archangel for free" video with his Short on its traits.

How to Get the Archangel Race

There's no quest or shop for Archangel. It's a Relic race you can only get by rerolling at the Wizard's Tower or in the Race Menu, according to the Forge Wiki. "For free" means rolling with free rerolls from codes instead of buying them with Robux:

  1. Redeem every working code in the Mailbox. Right now that's 45 rerolls: SORRYFORSHUTDOWNN!, AIDASAYSSORRY, FORGECOMEBACK and TWILIGHTWAR. See The Forge codes.
  2. Go to the Wizard's Tower and open the Race Menu.
  3. Reroll until you land ArchAngel or run out of rerolls.
No guaranteed method: any video, site or script that promises a guaranteed Archangel, "infinite rerolls" or a race glitch is either patched or a scam. Never give your Roblox login to anyone for rerolls.

Archangel Odds: How Many Rerolls?

The Forge Wiki lists ArchAngel at a 0.1% chance, which is 1 in 1,000 per roll. Kitsune, the other Relic, has the same chance. Luck boosts don't change race odds.

So free codes alone give you a small shot. Most players will land a Legendary or Mythical long before a Relic.

What the Archangel Race Does

  • Wings: -35% Dash Cooldown, +75% Dash Distance, +15% longer dash invincibility, +25% Movement Speed, +25% Jump Boost and +25% Stamina.
  • Demonic Powers: +20% Attack Speed and +20% Physical Damage.
  • Mighty Clover: +30% Luck Boost, which helps with mining rare ores.
  • Trial of Judgement: a 50% chance when dashing to create a Holy Circle that deals 45% of base weapon damage per second for 3 seconds (3-second cooldown).
  • Archangel's Smite: a 33% chance to call down 3 beams of light, each dealing 50% of base weapon damage (150% total, 10-second cooldown).
  • Heaven's Invincibility: taking physical damage below 20% Health gives 10 seconds of invincibility and restores Health to 25% (5-minute cooldown).

It mixes the Angel's mobility and luck with the Demon's damage, which is why the Forge Wiki's guide names it the best race overall.

Good Races to Keep While You Roll

Don't throw away a strong race chasing a 1-in-1,000 roll. Angel (0.25%) already gives +30% Luck and Smite. Demon (0.25%) gives +20% Movement Speed, Attack Speed and Physical Damage. Golem and Dragonborn are solid Legendaries. Compare them all in our races guide, and learn where to roll in the reroll guide.
