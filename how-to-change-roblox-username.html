How to Change Your Roblox Username: Cost, Steps and Rules

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Want to change your Roblox username? It takes a minute in Settings, but it costs Robux and your old names stay visible. Steps here follow Roblox Support's "Changing Your Username" article. Last checked November 11, 2026.

Username vs Display Name

  • Username: your unique account ID and the name you log in with. Changing it costs Robux.
  • Display name: the name shown in games and on your profile. It's free to change and doesn't have to be unique.

If you only want to look different in games, change your display name instead (see below).

How Much Does a Roblox Username Change Cost?

The standard price is 1,000 Robux. Roblox's help page just says each change "requires Robux" without naming the amount, but Roblox developer forum reports from September 2026 and guides like Endgame Tools put it at 1,000 Robux. Always check the price on the confirmation screen before you press Buy.

  • Changing back to an old name costs Robux again.
  • There's no discount for small changes, like capitalization.
  • Robux is paid from your balance. Need some? Read where to buy Roblox gift cards and how to redeem one.

How to Change Your Roblox Username (Step by Step)

  1. Log in to your account. If you can't, recover your account first.
  2. Open Settings. Browser: the gear icon at the upper right. Mobile app: More (…), then Settings.
  3. Select Account Info.
  4. Select Change Username (the pencil icon).
  5. Enter your new username and your current password.
  6. Select Buy.

From now on, log in with the new username and your same password. Your account creation date stays the same.

Roblox Username Rules

  • 3 to 20 characters.
  • Letters and numbers only (A–Z, 0–9), plus at most one underscore, and not at the start or end.
  • No inappropriate words. All names go through Roblox's text filter.
  • No personal info: no real names, phone numbers or addresses.
  • You can't take a name another account owns, or swap names with your alt account.

If a name is taken or filtered, Roblox tells you before you pay.

What Happens to Your Old Username?

Roblox says previous usernames can be seen in your Settings, on your profile and through People search. A new username doesn't hide your old one. Under a right-to-be-forgotten request, past usernames are permanently retired. If an old name shows personal info, contact Roblox Support.

How to Change Your Display Name for Free

  1. Go to Settings > Account Info.
  2. Select the pencil next to Display Name.
  3. Type the new name and select Save.

Display names can be changed once every 7 days and must follow the Community Standards, or Roblox may reset them to your username.

Tips Before You Pay

  • Add a verified email or phone first. If you forget your new name, that's how Roblox finds your account.
  • Check your account status so a moderation issue doesn't block the change.
  • Parents: spending limits in Parental Controls also apply to username changes.
Scam warning: nobody can change your username for free or sell you a "rare name" safely. Only change it in Roblox's own Settings, and never share your password.

Picking a Good New Username

Before you change your Roblox username, pick something you'll still like in a year, since every change costs 1,000 Robux. Short names are easier for friends to search for. Avoid your real name, birth year or school, because old usernames stay visible. Check the name is free by typing it into the Change Username box; Roblox tells you if it's taken or filtered before you pay.

Many players keep a fun display name and a simple, permanent username, so they only ever pay once.

Roblox FAQ

How much does it cost to change your Roblox username?

1,000 Robux per change. Roblox's help page just says it requires Robux, so check the confirmation screen.

How do I change my Roblox username?

Go to Settings, Account Info, select the pencil next to your username, enter the new name and your password, then select Buy.

Can I change my Roblox username for free?

No. Only your display name is free to change.

Can people see my old Roblox username?

Yes. Previous usernames show in Settings, on your profile and in People search.

How often can I change my Roblox display name?

Once every 7 days, for free.
