99 Nights in the Forest Beginner Guide: Survive Your First Nights

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

This 99 Nights in the Forest guide covers what to do from your very first night. The goal of the Roblox survival hit is to keep your group alive in a dark forest, protect the campfire and rescue lost children, all while something called the Deer hunts you after sunset. With around 150,000 players online on November 11, 2026, it's still one of Roblox's top survival games. Follow these steps in order and you'll last far longer.

Step 1: Fuel the Campfire

  1. As soon as you spawn, start cutting trees near camp.
  2. Throw logs into the campfire. A bigger fire lights a bigger safe area at night.
  3. Stockpile extra wood before dark so you don't have to leave the light.

Step 2: Handle the Deer

  • The Deer comes out at night. Stay near the fire when you can.
  • Shine a flashlight at it to stun it, then move away. Fighting it is a waste of time.
  • Listen for it; panicking into the dark is how most first runs end.

Step 3: Make a Map and Upgrade the Crafting Bench

  1. Craft the map for 3 wood and press M to open it.
  2. Upgrade the crafting bench with scrap and wood to unlock tools like the compass and sundial.
  3. At tier 3, build the crock pot for better food and the lightning rod.
  4. Keep bandages and medkits on hand. They can revive downed teammates.

Step 4: Trade with the Pelt Trader

On day 2 the Pelt Trader shows up. Hunt rabbits for a Rabbit's Foot and trade it for a Good Axe, Good Sack or Old Flashlight. Any of the three makes the next nights easier: the axe chops faster, the sack carries more and the flashlight handles the Deer.

Step 5: Cultists and Later Nights

  • Replant saplings around camp to slow Cultists down.
  • After night 5, watch for the Owl. Treat it like the Deer: stun it and move.
  • Tame wolves with a taming flute for extra help.
  • The Ram roams near the volcano biome; scout carefully.

Classes and Team Tips

  • Start with a cheap class. PC Gamer suggests Scavenger (25 diamonds); others like Camper also suit beginners.
  • Get diamonds faster with the active rewards on our 99 Nights in the Forest codes page.
  • In teams, split roles: one chops, one scouts for children, one guards the fire.
  • Solo, stay close to camp at night and do your exploring by day.

First-Night Checklist

  1. Chop trees and stack wood by the fire before dark.
  2. Craft the map (3 wood) so you can find your way back.
  3. Keep a flashlight equipped for the Deer.
  4. Carry a bandage in case a teammate goes down.
  5. Stay inside the firelight once night falls.

99 Nights in the Forest FAQ

How do you survive the first night in 99 Nights in the Forest?

Chop trees right away, keep the campfire fueled, stay in its light after dark and use a flashlight to stun the Deer instead of fighting it.

Can you kill the Deer in 99 Nights in the Forest?

No. Shine a flashlight at it to stun it, then move away.

When does the Pelt Trader come in 99 Nights in the Forest?

On day 2. Trade a Rabbit's Foot for a Good Axe, Good Sack or Old Flashlight.

What is the best beginner class in 99 Nights in the Forest?

A cheap class like Scavenger (25 diamonds) or Camper is a good start while you learn the game.

Can you play 99 Nights in the Forest solo?

Yes. Solo is harder, so stay near camp at night and explore by day. Servers hold up to 25 players if you'd rather team up.
