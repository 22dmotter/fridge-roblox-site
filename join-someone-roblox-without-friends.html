How to Join Someone on Roblox Without Being Friends (2026)

By Fridge · Published November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Official methods only: This guide only covers features built into Roblox. Skip browser extensions or websites that claim to find any player's server. They can break Roblox's rules, they're often used for stalking or harassment, and some steal logins. If someone has hidden where they are, respect that.

Want to know how to join someone on Roblox without being friends? It's possible, but only when the other player allows it. Roblox lets each player choose who can see and join the experience they're in. If they've opened that up to everyone (or to people they follow), you can join straight from their profile without sending a friend request.

Method 1: Join From Their Profile

  1. Search their username in the Roblox search bar and choose the People results.
  2. Open their profile.
  3. If they're in a game and their settings allow it, you'll see a Join button (or a game card showing where they are).
  4. Click Join to enter the same server.

If there's no Join button, the player's privacy settings don't allow you to join, they aren't in a public experience, or the server is full.

The Setting That Controls Joining

Whether you can join depends on the other player's privacy settings, not yours. In 2026 the setting lives under:

  1. Open Settings (the gear icon).
  2. Go to Privacy & content restrictions.
  3. Find Visibility & private servers, then the option for who can see the experience you're in (shown as something like "Show current experience"; older versions called it "Who can join me?").
  4. Choose an audience. Depending on age and region, options include Friends, Trusted Friends, Followers, People you follow, Everyone or No one.

Menu names and options can vary by account age, region and parental controls. Roblox limits these options for younger users for safety.

If you're a streamer or YouTuber, this is how viewers can play with you without filling your friends list. Set it to Followers so viewers who follow you can join.

Method 2: Private Server Links

Many experiences let you buy or create a private server (some are free). The owner can invite people who aren't friends:

  1. On the experience page, open the Servers tab.
  2. Find your private server and select the three dots (…), then Configure.
  3. Generate a private server link and share it, or add specific members (Roblox allows up to 50 invited users).
  4. Anyone with a working link can join that server, friend or not, unless the owner regenerates or disables it.
Link safety: Only open private server links from people you trust, and check that the URL is on roblox.com. Fake "server links" are a common phishing trick to steal accounts. If you lose access, our Roblox account recovery guide walks you through the official fix.

Method 3: Groups, Communities and In-Game Features

  • Same server via in-game parties: some games have their own party or invite systems that work without being Roblox friends.
  • Join codes: some experiences have server codes or lobbies you can share.
  • Follow first: following someone (without friending) can unlock joins if they allow Followers.

Why Can't I Join Someone on Roblox?

  • Their privacy setting doesn't include you. Ask them to change it.
  • The server is full. Wait for a spot or ask them to join a less busy server.
  • Age or parental controls restrict who either account can join or chat with.
  • The experience is age-restricted or private.
  • Error 264 means the same account is in that experience on another device; see our running multiple Roblox accounts guide.

Stay Safe When Joining Strangers

Joining non-friends is common for streams, groups and trading, but keep it safe: never share passwords or personal info, report and block anyone who harasses you, and use the Report Abuse tools in-game. Roblox FAQ

Can you join someone on Roblox without being friends?

Yes, if their privacy settings allow it. When they've set who can see and join their current experience to Everyone (or Followers and you follow them), you can join from their profile.

Why is the Join button missing on someone's Roblox profile?

Their privacy settings don't allow you to join, they're not in a joinable experience, or the server is full.

How do I let people join me on Roblox without friending them?

Go to Settings, Privacy & content restrictions, Visibility & private servers, and change who can see your current experience to Followers or Everyone, where available for your age.

Are Roblox server finder extensions safe?

Fridge doesn't recommend them. They're unofficial, can violate Roblox's rules or other players' privacy, and some are used to steal accounts.
