Lucky Cat in The Forge: Stats and Trait

Last checked: November 15, 2026

Lucky Cat is an Epic ore on the official Holy Tree list. Sources agree it is obtained from Hana Pebble, Glowy Rock, Blossom Boulder, with a 1-in-1,111 drop chance and an 11.1x multiplier. They do not agree on the sell price, so that field is left blank on this page. Its confirmed trait is Increases Luck by 5%; Increases the chance to drop 1 extra Ore when mining a Rock by 5% (Armor).

How to Get Lucky Cat

On the official Forge Wiki list, Lucky Cat is a Holy Tree ore, obtained from Hana Pebble, Glowy Rock, Blossom Boulder. the official Forge Wiki, Game8, BloxInformer, and Pocket Tactics all mention this ore. The official wiki item page also lists jade spirit cave. This page follows the official wiki list for the location label (Holy Tree). The confirmed drop chance on this page is 1-in-1,111. The official item page also lists Jade Spirit Cave (Spirit Rock, Soul Boulder). This page follows the official wiki list for the location label (Holy Tree).

What Lucky Cat Is Good For

Lucky Cat's confirmed trait is Increases Luck by 5%; Increases the chance to drop 1 extra Ore when mining a Rock by 5% (Armor). It has a 11.1x multiplier. The sell price is left blank because sources do not agree. See the forging guide and the pickaxe progression guide for how later-island ores fit the crafting loop.
