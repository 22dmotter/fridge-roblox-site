How to Join a Roblox Group: Joining Communities in 2026

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Looking for how to join a Roblox group? First, the name changed: Roblox renamed Groups to Communities in late 2024, so the button now says Join Community. Everything else works the same. Steps and limits below come from Roblox Support. Last checked November 11, 2026.

Roblox Groups Are Now Called Communities

Old videos and guides say "Groups". Roblox's help articles, website menu and app now say "Communities". Searching "group" still finds most of them, and old group links still work.

How to Find a Roblox Community

  • From a game: most games link their community under the title on the game page, often as the game's creator.
  • From search: search on Roblox and switch the results to Communities.
  • From a link: developers share community links on X, Discord and YouTube. Check the URL is on roblox.com before you click.

Joining a game's community often gets you codes, perks or free items. See how to get free items in Roblox and our Roblox codes hub.

How to Join a Roblox Group (Community)

  1. Open the community page. Check the name, owner and member count so you know it's the real one.
  2. Press Join Community.
  3. If it joins right away, you're in. You'll see community chat or posts and your role.
  4. If it uses Manual Approval, your request goes to the owner, who reviews it under Configure Community > Members > Requests. Wait for approval.

Community Verification Requirements

Owners can set a Verification Level under Configure Community > Settings, per Roblox Support:

LevelWhat you need before joining
None (default)Nothing extra
LowPhone, email or ID verified
MediumID verified, or phone and email verified
HighID verified

If you don't meet it, Roblox prompts you to verify when you press Join Community. Our Roblox age verification guide explains ID and age checks.

Community Limits and How to Leave

  • You can join up to 100 communities.
  • At the limit, the Join button won't work. Leave one first.
  • To leave: open the community, press the overflow (⋯) menu and choose Leave Community.
  • If an owner leaves, any member can claim ownership.

How to Create Your Own Roblox Community

  1. Log in. On the website, click Communities in the left menu; in the app, tap More (…).
  2. Click Create.
  3. Add a name (up to 50 characters), description and emblem.
  4. In Settings, choose open entry or Manual Approval.
  5. Pay the one-time 100 Robux fee and click Purchase.

You can own up to 100 communities. Manage roles, members, payouts and settings from Configure Community.

Staying Safe in Communities

  • Don't join communities that promise free Robux; that's always a scam.
  • Never share your password or move to another app because a community member asked.
  • Report rule-breaking posts or members. See how to report a player on Roblox.
  • Only add people you know in real life as Trusted Friends.

Why Join a Community?

Most players join a community for one of these reasons:

  • Game perks: many developers give in-game rewards, codes or free items only to community members.
  • Updates: community announcements are often the first place new codes and events show up.
  • Roleplay and clans: military, cafe and clan groups use ranks and roles for in-game teams.
  • Developer teams: communities can own games and pay members from community funds.

Getting a Rank After You Join

New members start at the lowest role. Owners and admins promote people manually or with ranking bots in the community's own game. Read the community's description for how its ranks work, and never pay a stranger for a promotion.

Join Button Not Working?

If nothing happens when you press Join Community, check that you're under the 100-community limit, that you aren't banned from that community, and that your account meets its verification level. Logging out and back in, or trying the website instead of the app, fixes most display glitches.

Roblox FAQ

How do I join a Roblox group?

Groups are now called Communities. Open the community page and press Join Community. If manual approval is on, wait for the owner to accept.

Why are Roblox groups called Communities now?

Roblox renamed Groups to Communities in late 2024. They work the same way.

How many Roblox communities can I join?

Up to 100, per Roblox Support.

How much does it cost to make a Roblox community?

A one-time 100 Robux fee per community.

Why can't I join a Roblox community?

You may be at the 100 limit, the community may need approval, or it may require phone, email or ID verification.
