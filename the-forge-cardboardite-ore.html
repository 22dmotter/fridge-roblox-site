Cardboardite Ore in The Forge: Drop Chance and Uses

Last checked: November 15, 2026

Cardboardite is a Common ore from Stonewake's Cross, but its drop chance is lower than several Uncommon and Rare ores in this set. Sources agree it drops from Rocks and Boulders at 1-in-31, with a 0.7x multiplier, a $10.50 sell price, and no trait.

How to Get Cardboardite

Mine Rocks or Boulders in Stonewake's Cross. The official Forge Wiki, Game8, Destructoid, and BloxInformer all list a 1/31 drop chance from those nodes.

What Cardboardite Is Good For

Cardboardite has no trait, a 0.7x multiplier, and a $10.50 sell price. That multiplier is higher than the earlier Common ores in this list (Stone through Iron) and sits just below Mushroomite and Platinum. See the forging guide for how multipliers work.
