Copper Ore in The Forge: Drop Chance and Uses

Last checked: November 14, 2026

Copper is a Common Stonewake's Cross ore that drops from all three early rock types: Pebbles, Rocks, and Boulders. It has no trait, but its multiplier and sell price sit a step above Sand Stone.

How to Get Copper

Mine Pebbles, Rocks, or Boulders in Stonewake's Cross. Sources agree the drop chance is 1/3 from those nodes.

What Copper Is Good For

Copper has no trait, a 0.3x multiplier, and a $4.50 sell price. It is a solid early forging ore once you can break the same nodes that also drop Iron. For pickaxe upgrades that reach Boulders, see the pickaxe progression guide.
