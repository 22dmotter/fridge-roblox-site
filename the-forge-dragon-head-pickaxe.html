How to Get the Dragon Head Pickaxe in The Forge: Full Quest Guide

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

The Dragon Head Pickaxe has the highest Mine Power of the Island 3 pickaxes. It's carved from a dragon's skull and sits at the bottom of a quest chain that starts in the Raven Cave. Fridge has a Short and a full video on how to get the Dragon Head Pickaxe, both from January 3, 2026, the day the Raven Cave update launched.

How to Unlock the Dragon Head Pickaxe

  1. Finish two of the three Raven quests to open the cave. Our Raven Cave guide lists every objective.
  2. Go down to Corruption's Heart, the hidden core of the island and home of the Demonic Spiders. The Forge Wiki notes that you buy the Dragon Head Pickaxe there.
  3. Do Auron's quest: bring 1 Prismatic Heart.
  4. Do Mjelatkhan's quest: bring 1 Yeti Heart.
  5. Do Morveth's quest: bring 1 Golem Heart.
  6. Buy the pickaxe for 2,200,000 Gold.

The three quests form "The Cult's Questline" on the wiki and must be done in that order: Auron, then Mjelatkhan, then Morveth.

Is It Worth It?

If you're mining The Peak's crystals, it's worth the effort. It has 750 Mine Power against the Heavenly Pickaxe's 725 and the Prismatic's 650, plus +10% speed. The Heavenly has more Luck (+85% vs +78%), so pick based on whether you need raw power or rare-ore luck. Fridge's video calls it the "Crimson Cavern Update". On the wiki, the cave is the Raven Cave, also described as the Crimson Depths.

The Forge FAQ

How do you get the Dragon Head Pickaxe in The Forge?

Complete two Raven quests to open the cave, finish the Auron, Mjelatkhan and Morveth quests with a Prismatic, Yeti and Golem Heart, then buy it for 2,200,000 Gold.

Where is the Dragon Head Pickaxe?

At Corruption's Heart, the hidden core below the Raven Cave on the Frostspire Expanse.

How much is the Dragon Head Pickaxe in The Forge?

2,200,000 Gold, or 1,936,000 with the Goblin race discount.
