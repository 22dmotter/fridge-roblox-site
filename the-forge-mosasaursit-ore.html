Mosasaursit in The Forge: Stats and Trait

Last checked: November 15, 2026

Mosasaursit is an Exotic ore on the official The Peak list. Sources agree it is obtained from Iceberg, with a guaranteed drop and a 7.5x multiplier. They do not agree on the sell price, so that field is left blank on this page. Its confirmed trait is Increases max Health by 35%; Decreases Movement Speed by 25% (Armor).

How to Get Mosasaursit

On the official Forge Wiki list, Mosasaursit is a The Peak ore, obtained from Iceberg. the official Forge Wiki, Game8, BloxInformer, and Destructoid all mention this ore. The confirmed drop chance on this page is 1-in-1.

What Mosasaursit Is Good For

Mosasaursit's confirmed trait is Increases max Health by 35%; Decreases Movement Speed by 25% (Armor). It has a 7.5x multiplier. The sell price is left blank because sources do not agree. See the Island 3 / Frostspire Expanse guide and the forging guide for how later World 3 ores fit the crafting loop.
