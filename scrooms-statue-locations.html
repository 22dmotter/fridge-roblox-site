All Scrooms Statue Locations (and How to Find Halden)

By Fridge · Published October 13, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Stuck on the Scrooms statues? You need to Pay Respects at five different statues in Songkeeper's Grove to finish Halden's quest, The Next Step, and unlock the Ocarina. This guide gives you a simple landmark checklist for all five statue locations, explains exactly where Halden is, and covers the common mistakes that leave your counter stuck at 4/5.

Where Is Halden in Scrooms?

Halden is an NPC, not a creature, so don't go hunting for a Scroom with that name. Here's how to find him:

  1. Finish Leo's opening tutorial: first catch, sell to Julian, buy and equip the Tamer's Guitar.
  2. Talk to Leo again. He points you toward Halden.
  3. Head to the campsite beside the large Grove tree. Look for red tents, a campfire and a log.
  4. Halden is sitting on the log. Walk up until his Talk prompt appears and press E.
  5. Accept The Next Step. Check that your tracker now asks for five respects.

All 5 Scrooms Statue Locations

You can visit the statues in any order. Use the table as a checklist and tick each one off as your counter goes up.

4Lantern by a stone moundOn top of the stone mound, with a small glowing lantern in the grass beside it.

There's no set route for the Scrooms statues. The order above is a search checklist, not a required route. Start with whichever statue is closest to you.

How to Pay Respects at Scrooms Statues

This is where most players get stuck. Looking at a statue or walking past it does not count.

  1. Walk right up to the statue until the Pay Respects prompt appears.
  2. Press E on PC, or tap the prompt on mobile.
  3. Check your counter at the top of the screen. It should go up by one.
  4. If it didn't change, try the prompt again before moving on.
Stuck at 4/5? You probably interacted with the same statue twice. Judge progress by the counter, not by how many pedestals you've seen. The two statues near lanterns (#4 and #5) are the easiest to mix up.

Return to Halden for the Ocarina

When your tracker hits 5/5, go back to the same campfire and speak to Halden. The reward only unlocks in that return conversation, so finishing the counter isn't enough. You'll receive the Ocarina, and unlocking it is tied to the Magic Melody badge.

The Ocarina doesn't replace your catching instrument. It plays utility songs from a Known Songs list:

  • Homeward Melody teleports you back to your set spawn point. Select it and enter the notes shown on screen. Players have reported the sequence as A, S, W, A on keyboard, but always follow the notes your game displays.
  • Song of Passage is a later song used for opening gates.

If a song doesn't work, reselect it and restart the note sequence from the first note.

What to Do After the Scrooms Statues Quest

Finishing the Scrooms statues is the first real milestone after the tutorial, and it opens up the rest of the early game.

With the Ocarina in hand, your next main goal is Tamer in Training, which asks you to hit a level milestone and return to Halden for a big XP reward. That's a grind, so use the routine from our level up fast in Scrooms guide: catch nonstop near the spawn signpost, sell to Julian when full, and grab every quest with a green "?" marker.

Quick Tips for Finding Scrooms Statues

  • Accept the quest first. Paying respects before talking to Halden won't register.
  • Keep a mental list of which landmarks you've done. Re-running the whole loop wastes minutes.
  • Use the river as a guide. Statues #2 and #3 are both near the water, so grab them together.
  • Use Homeward Melody afterward to warp back to spawn instead of walking.

Scrooms FAQ

How many statues are in Scrooms?

Halden's quest The Next Step needs five distinct statues in Songkeeper's Grove. You can visit them in any order.

Where is Halden in Scrooms?

Halden sits on a log by a campfire and red tents next to the large tree in Songkeeper's Grove. Leo directs you there after the tutorial.

Why isn't my Scrooms statue counter going up?

You need to use the Pay Respects prompt (E on PC). Walking past doesn't count, and interacting with the same statue twice won't add progress.

What does the Ocarina do in Scrooms?

The Ocarina plays utility songs. Homeward Melody teleports you to your spawn point, and later songs like Song of Passage open gates.
