Roblox Account Recovery: How to Get a Hacked or Lost Account Back

By Fridge · Published November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Scam warning: Roblox Support will never ask for your password, and no one on YouTube, Discord, TikTok or in DMs can "recover" your account for you. People offering paid recovery, "Roblox staff" contacts or "hack-back" services are scammers. Only use roblox.com and the official Roblox Support pages linked in this guide.

This Roblox account recovery guide walks you through the official steps, in order, for the most common situations: you forgot your password, someone hacked your account, or you lost access to your email or phone. Move quickly. The sooner you act, the better your chances.

If You Forgot Your Password

  1. Go to the Roblox login page and select Forgot Password or Username?
  2. Enter the email address or phone number on your account.
  3. Open the reset email or text and follow the link to choose a new password.
  4. If you don't see the email, check spam and confirm you used the right address.

If no email or phone was ever added, the reset tool can't help. Jump to contacting Roblox Support.

If Your Roblox Account Was Hacked

Roblox Support's official checklist for compromised accounts comes down to these steps:

  1. Clean your device first. Run a trusted antivirus scan and remove suspicious programs and browser extensions. If malware stole your login once, it can steal the new one too.
  2. Reset your password using the steps above, if you can still receive emails or texts.
  3. Log out other sessions: in Settings, under Security, use the option to log out of all other sessions.
  4. Turn on 2-Step Verification. An authenticator app is the strongest option Roblox offers.
  5. Check your email and phone in account settings and remove anything the hacker added.
  6. Secure your email account too. Change its password and enable two-factor authentication there.

How to Contact Roblox Support for Recovery

If you can't get in at all, use Roblox's official account recovery support page or contact form at roblox.com/support. To give your request the best chance:

  • Send it from the first email address ever added to the account, or the email tied to the account's first purchase, if you can.
  • Include the exact username, roughly when it was created, and what happened.
  • Add proof if you have it, such as purchase receipts or the original verification email.
  • Submit one request and wait. Sending many duplicates can slow things down.
Be realistic: Roblox says it can't guarantee every account can be recovered, especially if it's missing a verified email or phone. That's why setting those up now matters.

Can You Get Lost Items or Robux Back?

Roblox Support explains that restoring lost items is only possible in limited cases, and you generally need to report it within 30 days. Items lost through trades made off-platform (like deals on Discord or third-party websites) aren't supported. In-game items belong to each game's developer, so you may need to contact that game's team.

Lost 2-Step Verification deviceUse your saved recovery codes, or contact Support

Common Roblox Recovery Scams

  • "I'm Roblox staff, give me your password." Real staff never ask.
  • Paid recovery services on social media. They take your money and often your account too.
  • "Free Robux" or "free item" sites that ask you to log in. These are how many accounts get stolen in the first place.
  • Files called "account recovery tool.exe" or browser extensions. These are malware.
  • Requests to run code in your browser console or to paste a link. This can hand over your login session.

Also be careful with third-party launchers and account tools. Only download well-known ones from their official sources (see our Bloxstrap guide and our multiple Roblox accounts guide for the official-source rules).

How to Protect Your Account From Now On

  1. Add and verify an email address and phone number.
  2. Turn on 2-Step Verification and save the recovery codes somewhere safe.
  3. Use a unique password you don't use anywhere else.
  4. Never log in through links sent in DMs.
  5. Review your privacy settings, including who can see and join you (our join someone on Roblox guide explains those settings).

If you'd rather close an old account you no longer use, follow our how to delete a Roblox account guide.

Roblox FAQ

How do I recover my Roblox account?

If you still have access to the email or phone on the account, use Forgot Password on the login page. If not, contact Roblox Support through roblox.com/support, ideally from the first email address added to the account.

Can I recover a hacked Roblox account without email?

You can contact Roblox Support with as much proof as possible, such as purchase receipts, but Roblox says recovery isn't guaranteed without a verified email or phone.

Will Roblox give back stolen items?

Only in limited cases and usually only if reported within 30 days. Trades made off-platform aren't supported.

Is Roblox account recovery free?

Yes. Official Roblox Support is free. Anyone charging to recover your account is a scam.
