Adopt Me Codes (November 2026): Working Code and Free Bucks

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Here's the latest on Adopt Me codes, last checked November 11, 2026. Adopt Me has a real in-game code kiosk, but codes are rare and short-lived. Right now one code, PROUDFROG, is still listed as working by two trackers. It gives a sticker, not pets or Bucks. Below are the redeem steps, past official codes, and the free Bucks routes that work every week.

How to Redeem Adopt Me Codes

These steps follow Uplift Games' official redeem page:

  1. Go to the Safety Hub building.
  2. Find Agent Ruhi, who stands next to a white and gold computer kiosk.
  3. Interact with the kiosk to open the Code Redemption Kiosk.
  4. Type the code into "Enter Your Code" and press Redeem!
  5. The reward goes straight to your backpack.

Uplift's terms say no purchase is needed, each code is one use, and items from codes can't be traded or sold. The same kiosk also accepts codes that come with official Adopt Me merchandise.

Free Bucks and Pets Without Codes

  • Pet needs and jobs pay Bucks any time, and 2x weekends double it.
  • Star Rewards: log in daily for Bucks and a Star. According to Uplift's September 2026 notes, 14 Stars buy a Royal Fairytale Egg (10% Legendary) and 28 Stars buy a Gemstone Egg.
  • Task Board daily tasks give rewards and sometimes extra Bucks.
  • 2D Tuesday: a free temporary 2D Kitty gives 2x Bucks and XP each Tuesday.
  • Admin Abuse: the weekly Saturday event promises "Freebies & Chaos!"

Fake Adopt Me Promo Codes and Bucks Generators

Scam warning: "Legendary pet codes" and "free Bucks generators" are fake. They usually want your password, a download or a survey. Real codes only come from @PlayAdoptMe and Uplift's channels, and you type them only into the in-game kiosk. Adopt Me! FAQ

Are there any working Adopt Me promo codes?

PROUDFROG (Proud Frog Sticker) is still listed as working by two trackers as of November 2026, but it has no official end date and may expire at any time.

Where do you put codes in Adopt Me?

At the Code Redemption Kiosk next to Agent Ruhi in the Safety Hub, according to Uplift's official redeem page.

Are there Adopt Me promo codes for legendary pets?

No official Adopt Me code has given a legendary pet. The free legendary chance comes from Star Rewards eggs.

How do I get free Bucks in Adopt Me?

Complete pet needs and jobs, claim daily Star Rewards, do Task Board tasks and use the weekly 2D Tuesday 2x Bucks pet.

Why does my Adopt Me code say invalid?

It's probably expired. Adopt Me promo codes usually last a short time, and PROUDFROG may already have ended.
