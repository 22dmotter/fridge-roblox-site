How to Add Trusted Friends on Roblox in 2026 (QR, Contacts, Link)

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

This guide explains how to add Trusted Friends on Roblox, the 2026 system for marking people you know in real life. Trusted Friends can unlock extra chat options depending on your age and region. The method you use depends on your age, and everyone must complete an age check first. Every step below comes from Roblox Support.

What Are Trusted Friends on Roblox?

Trusted Friends are a level above regular friends, meant for people you know and trust. They show a Trusted indicator next to their username on profiles, in experience chat and in friends chat. Availability varies by country, and Roblox is still rolling it out to more regions.

Step 1: Complete the Age Check

Roblox Support says you must estimate your age before Trusted Friends options appear. Open Settings → Account info and complete the age check (Facial Age Estimation with your camera, or ID verification where offered). Your friend needs to be age checked too.

How to Add Trusted Friends on Roblox by Age

Trusted Friends methods by age group
Your ageHow you can add Trusted Friends
12 and underRequest from a friend's profile; a parent must approve each request
13–17Contact importer (mobile), live QR code scan, or invite link; 16–17 may also use profile opt-in
18+Profile opt-in with other adults; contacts, QR or link for 13–17 year olds

Teens (13–17): QR code, contacts or invite link

  1. Live QR code: go to Home, tap the Add Friends (+) icon, then My QR. Your friend scans it live with their camera or the scanner icon on their My QR page. Scanning a screenshot only adds a regular friend.
  2. Contacts (mobile only): Home → Add Friends (+) → Contacts to add people from your phone's contact list.
  3. Invite link: if you're both age checked 13+, open your friend's profile, tap the three dots, choose Add trusted friend, then Add via link. Links expire after 30 days.
  4. Profile opt-in (16–17): if you're both 16–17, you may be able to choose Add trusted friend straight from their profile and wait for them to accept.

Under 13: parent approval

  1. Open the friend's profile, tap the three dots, choose Add trusted friend, then Add.
  2. Tap Ask now to request your parent's permission (or enter a parent's email if you haven't linked one).
  3. Your parent approves by email or in Parental controls → Pending requests. Requests expire after 24 hours.
  4. If your friend is also under 13, their parent must approve too.

Linked, age-checked parents and children who are friends are upgraded to Trusted Friends automatically. Parents can learn more in our Roblox parental controls guide.

Adults (18+)

Two age-checked adults can upgrade each other from the profile menu (three dots → Add trusted friend). To add a 13–17 year old, adults must use contacts, a live QR scan or an invite link, which Roblox uses to make sure you know each other in real life.

How to Remove or Report a Trusted Friend

  • Downgrade: open their profile menu and tap Remove trusted friend. They go back to being a regular friend.
  • Remove completely: tap Remove friend.
  • Report: you can report a Trusted Friend like anyone else.
  • View your list: in the Friends tab, filter by Trusted.

Why Add Trusted Friends?

Depending on your age and region, Trusted Friends can unlock features like chatting across age groups. They also work with visibility settings, so you can let only Trusted Friends see when you're online; see how to appear offline on Roblox. For talking out loud in games, see our how to turn on voice chat on Roblox guide.

Safety: only add people you actually know in real life. Never scan a QR code or open a "trusted friend" link from a stranger.

Roblox FAQ

How do you add Trusted Friends on Roblox?

Complete the age check first. Teens can add Trusted Friends with a live QR scan, phone contacts or an invite link; under-13s need a parent to approve each request; adults can opt in from each other's profiles.

Can you add Trusted Friends on Roblox without a QR code?

Yes. Teens and adults can use the contact importer on mobile or send an invite link from the friend's profile, which expires after 30 days.

Why can't I see Trusted Friends on Roblox?

You may not have completed the age check, or Trusted Friends may not be available in your country yet.

Does scanning a screenshot of a QR code work?

No. Scanning a screenshot or using a shared profile link only adds a regular friend. The QR code must be scanned live.
