Dandy's World Codes (November 2026): Free Ichor and Profile Print

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

These are the working Dandy's World codes for November 2026, last checked November 11, 2026. There are 2 working codes: ICHOR for 50 Ichor and AD2026 for a profile print. The Halloween sticker code QoM expired when the event ended on November 6. Dandy's World by BlushCrunch Studio was the #12 most-played Roblox game on November 11, with about 71,000 players online.

How to Redeem Dandy's World Codes

  1. Launch Dandy's World and stay in the lobby. The code computer isn't available during a run.
  2. Walk to the computer next to Dyle's Store (the clock shop), under the "Redeem Codes Here" sign.
  3. Interact with it (press E on keyboard or tap it) to open the code box.
  4. Type a code and press Use. The wiki says codes aren't case-sensitive.
Mid-run? Finish or leave the floor first. Levvvel notes that the code computer only exists in the lobby.

What to Spend Free Ichor On

Ichor is the main currency in Dandy's World. GamesRadar+ says you can use it on Toons, Trinkets and Skins. 50 Ichor isn't much on its own, so save it with the Ichor you earn from runs and put it toward a Toon or Trinket that fits how you play. The profile print from AD2026 is cosmetic and shows on your profile.

When Do New Codes Come Out?

Dandy's World promo codes are rare. Early codes celebrated visit milestones, and newer ones arrive with seasonal events. BloxGuidesGG notes that the 2026 Halloween event (October 2 to November 6, 3 PM ET) didn't get a currency code. The next likely moment is a holiday event or a big milestone. What Is Dandy's World?

Dandy's World is a survival horror game from BlushCrunch Studio, first published in January 2024 and still labeled ALPHA on Roblox. It has more than 7.9 billion visits. You pick a Toon and work through floors while avoiding Twisteds, and Ichor is what you spend on new Toons, Trinkets and skins.

The 2026 Halloween event ran from October 2 to November 6. BloxGuidesGG lists what it added: two limited-time Halloween Floors, the Haunted Gala floor event, the return of Gourdy's Event Shop with a new skin pack, and the four Halloween Toons (Gourdy, Eclipse, Soulvester and Ribecca). Event currency like Pumpkins is gone now that it has ended, which is why the Halloween codes are expired.

Dandy's World FAQ

What are the working Dandy's World promo codes?

As of November 11, 2026: ICHOR (50 Ichor) and AD2026 (American Dream 2026 Profile Print).

Where do you redeem codes in Dandy's World?

At the computer next to Dyle's Store in the lobby. Interact with it, type the code and press Use.

Are Dandy's World promo codes case-sensitive?

No. The wiki says codes work in upper or lower case.

Does QoM still work in Dandy's World?

No. QoM expired when the Halloween event ended on November 6, 2026.

Is the ICHOR code permanent?

Yes. The wiki lists ICHOR as never expiring. Like other codes, expect it to work once per account.

Is there a Dandy's World Halloween 2026 code?

No public Halloween 2026 currency code was released, and the event ended on November 6, 2026.

What is Ichor used for in Dandy's World?

Ichor is the main currency. You spend it on new Toons, Trinkets and skins.
