How to Reroll Your Race in The Forge (and Get Free Rerolls)

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Your race is one of the biggest power boosts in the game, so knowing how to reroll race in The Forge matters. This guide shows where to reroll on every island, what the odds are, and how to get rerolls for free. It brings together all eight of Fridge's reroll videos and Shorts.

Where to Reroll Your Race

According to the Forge Wiki, you change race in the Wizard's Tower by talking to the Wizard, or from the Race Menu. The Wizard opens the Race Menu for you. Here's where he stands on each island:

Wizard locations in The Forge
IslandWhere to find the Wizard
Stonewake's CrossUp the hill to the left of the cave, past Tomo, in a tower on the left
Forgotten KingdomUp the hill to the right of spawn, past Line, through a castle gate, in the tower straight ahead
Frostspire ExpanseStraight from spawn, past Wo, in a hut on the right

How to Reroll, Step by Step

  1. Walk to the Wizard's Tower on your island, or open the Race Menu.
  2. Talk to the Wizard to open the race screen.
  3. Press Reroll. Each press uses one reroll and gives you a random race based on the odds below.
  4. Keep rolling until you land a race you want. Rerolls are spent even if you get a worse race.

The Fast Roll gamepass (239 Robux) only makes the spin animation quicker. It doesn't change the odds.

How to Get Free Rerolls

The wiki lists two ways to get rerolls: Robux purchases or codes. Codes are the only free source. Right now four codes work, for 45 free rerolls in total:

  • SORRYFORSHUTDOWNN!: 15 rerolls
  • AIDASAYSSORRY: 10 rerolls
  • FORGECOMEBACK: 10 rerolls
  • TWILIGHTWAR: 10 rerolls

Redeem them in the Mailbox. The full list, with expiry notes, is on our The Forge codes page. Fridge's "free reroll" Shorts all use whatever codes were active that week, and most of those have since expired.

Race Odds by Rarity

The Forge race odds (Forge Wiki, November 11, 2026)
RarityChance per raceRaces
Common35.05%Human
Uncommon11.5%Elf, Zombie
Rare7.5%Goblin, Undead, Orc
Epic4%Dwarf, Shadow, Vampire
Legendary1.25%Minotaur, Dragonborn, Golem, Felynx, Fairy
Mythical0.25%Angel, Demon, Orc Lord, Dryad, Imp, Fallen Angel
Relic0.1%ArchAngel, Kitsune

Any Legendary race comes up about 6.25% of the time, so 45 free rerolls give you roughly a 94% chance of at least one Legendary. Mythical races total 1.5%, so 45 rolls give about a 49% chance of a Mythical. Relics total 0.2%, about 9% over 45 rolls.

The wiki's listed chances add up to just over 100% (100.5%), so treat these numbers as close estimates, not exact odds.

Do Luck Boosts Help?

No. The Forge Wiki says event luck, totem luck and potion luck don't affect race rerolls, so save your Luck Potions for mining. See what each race actually does in our races guide before you decide when to stop rolling.
