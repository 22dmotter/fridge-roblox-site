Throw Food For Animals Beginner Guide: Throw Power, Zones and Pets

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Throw Food For Animals by Goofy Chupacabra was one of the four biggest games on Roblox when we checked on November 11, 2026, with over 300,000 players online. The idea is simple: throw food as far as you can, catch the animals it lands near, and place them on your pen to earn cash. This beginner guide covers each step in the order you'll hit it, so you can progress through the zones faster.

How Throw Food For Animals Works

Step 1: Throw Your First Carrot

  1. You spawn with a starter Carrot. Equip it and throw it toward the field.
  2. Run to where it lands and collect the animal that comes to eat it.
  3. Keep throwing; early animals are common, but each one adds income.

Step 2: Place Animals on Your Pen

Caught animals only earn money once you place them on your pen, so don't leave them in your inventory. Use Equip Best to fill your slots with your highest earners automatically, and buy more slots as soon as you can afford them. More placed animals means more cash every second.

Step 3: Train Throw Power

  1. Use the pull-up stand to train Throw Power.
  2. Upgrade the bar as you go: Wooden, Iron, Cyan, Violet, Ruby, Chrome, Obsidian and Eclipse. Each tier trains faster.
  3. Alternate training and throwing: train until your throws clearly travel farther, then go catch better animals.

Training is grindy. Our how to AFK in Roblox guide explains how to stay in a server without getting kicked for idling. A smooth frame rate helps too; see our fix Roblox lag guide.

Step 4: Unlock New Food and Zones

  • When your throws start reaching the next zone, buy the next food in the shop.
  • Better food plus more Throw Power lets you hit farther zones with rarer animals.
  • Better food attracts rarer animals, while Throw Power only adds distance, so you need both.

Step 5: Server Luck, Passes and Free Items

  • Server Luck (2x up to 16x) boosts the whole lobby, and servers are small (5 players), so join servers where someone has bought it.
  • The x2 Training Power game pass costs 149 Robux if you want faster training.
  • A free item (the Hyena) and weekly Admin Abuse events give free boosts. Details are in our TFFA codes page, which also explains why the game has no codes.

Update 3: Cursed Carnival and Eclipse Kingdom

The wiki's update log says Update 3 (November 9, 2026) added the Cursed Carnival zone, which needs roughly 8,000 Throw Power, along with Eclipse Kingdom and a new Sovereign rarity. Aim for the Eclipse bar before pushing there. The next update is expected on November 14 at 9 AM ET.

Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

  • Forgetting to place new animals (no placement means no income).
  • Training forever without upgrading food.
  • Playing solo in a server with no luck boost.

When you're unsure what to do next, use this rule from Bloxron's guide: if your throws land short of the next zone, train; if they already reach it, buy the next food.

Throw Food For Animals FAQ

How do you get stronger in TFFA?

Train Throw Power on the pull-up stand, upgrade to better bars like Ruby, Obsidian and Eclipse, and buy the next food whenever your throws reach a new zone.

Why aren't my animals making money?

Animals only earn money per second after you place them on your pen. Use Equip Best and buy more slots.

What does Server Luck do in TFFA?

It applies a 2x to 16x luck boost to everyone in your server, making rarer animals more likely.

Are there codes for TFFA?

No. The game has no code redeem system right now; use the free item and Admin Abuse events instead.
