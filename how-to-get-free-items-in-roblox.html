How to Get Free Items in Roblox: Every Real Method (November 2026)

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Want to know how to get free items in Roblox without falling for a scam? There are four real ways in November 2026: free items in the Marketplace, the one working Roblox promo code, free UGC handed out inside games, and rewards from game codes and events. None of them gives free Robux, because nothing does. This guide walks through each method step by step.

Step 1: Find Free Items in the Marketplace

Roblox's Marketplace (the old Avatar Shop) has a permanent set of 0-Robux hair, faces, hats and clothes. Roblox Support says you can narrow any category or search with filters for Sales Type, Creator, Price, Sorts and Unavailable Items.

  1. Open Marketplace from the top of the Roblox site or app.
  2. Pick a category such as Hair, Heads or Accessories, or search a keyword.
  3. Open Filters and set the Price range to free (0 Robux).
  4. Set Creator to Roblox if you only want official items that won't sell out.
  5. Open an item, check the price says Free, and press Get.

Examples that Gaming Route confirmed were free and on sale on November 4, 2026 include Pal Hair (63690008), True Blue Hair (451221329) and the Roblox Visor (2646473721). More are on our Catalog Avatar Creator codes page.

Step 3: Claim Free UGC in Games

Some experiences give away limited 0-Robux UGC items made by community creators. They're real, but stock is limited and they disappear fast. Before you press Get, check the confirmation screen: the price must say 0 Robux, because "free" UGC can be switched to paid at any time. Never pay a reseller for an item that was free a week ago unless you really want it.

Step 4: Game Rewards, Codes and Events

  • Game codes: many games give free items, emotes or currency through codes. See our Blox Fruits codes or the full roundup.
  • Events: Roblox-wide events like The Hollow handed out free items; the Hollow ended on November 2, 2026, so watch for the next one.
  • Community items: some communities (groups) reward members with in-game items. Our how to join a Roblox group guide explains joining.
  • Gift card bonus items: each redeemed Roblox gift card includes a free virtual item (one per month per account).

Daily and Weekly Freebie Routine

  1. Check the Marketplace filtered by free once a week for new items.
  2. Check your favorite games' codes after each update.
  3. Follow official game communities for event announcements.
  4. Stack your new free items with our multiple accessories guide.

Scams to Avoid

No free Robux exists. Roblox's own gift card page says "codes for free Robux do not exist," and any person, site or video offering them is a scam. Never enter your password on a site that isn't roblox.com, never download "item generators," and never paste code into your browser console. Trading scams ("give me your item first") are also common.

Quick Checklist: Free Items Every Week

Still wondering how to get free items in Roblox every week without wasting time? Here's a fast routine. Check the Marketplace's Free filter on Monday, redeem any new promo or game codes from our codes hub, visit one or two UGC games during events, and log in to your favorite games for daily rewards. Skip anything that asks for your password or a download.

Roblox FAQ

How do you get free items in Roblox?

Filter the Marketplace by a price of 0 Robux, redeem the SPIDERCOLA promo code, claim free UGC in games, and use game codes and events.

Is there a Roblox promo code right now?

Yes. SPIDERCOLA gives the Spider Cola shoulder accessory at roblox.com/redeem. It's the only working Roblox promo code as of November 11, 2026.

Can you get free Robux on Roblox?

No. Roblox says codes for free Robux don't exist. Sites and videos offering them are scams.

Are free UGC items safe?

Usually, if you get them inside Roblox. Always check that the price says 0 Robux before confirming.
