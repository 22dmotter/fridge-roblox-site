Best Roblox Pets 2026: Top Adopt Me and Pet Simulator 99 Pets

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

The best Roblox pets depend on the game. In Adopt Me "best" means trade value, and in Pet Simulator 99 it means damage and value. This guide pulls the November 2026 picks for both games from community value trackers and the official PS99 patch notes. Last checked November 11, 2026.

What Makes a Roblox Pet the Best?

  • Trade value: how much other players will give for it (Adopt Me's main measure).
  • Stats: damage or coin bonus in simulators like Pet Simulator 99.
  • Rarity and demand: retired event pets usually climb in value.

Values are community estimates, not official Roblox numbers, and they move every day. Use them as a guide, not a price tag.

Neon, Mega Neon and Potions

Pet versions change value a lot:

  • Fly (F) and Ride (R) potions add value; an FR pet beats a plain one.
  • Neon needs four Full Grown copies of the same pet. A French value guide (m67.fr) estimates a Neon is worth about four times the regular pet on average.
  • Mega Neon needs four Full Grown Neons, worth about 16 times regular on average (less at the very top).

Aging pets is how you "level" in Adopt Me; see how to level up fast in Roblox.

Best Pet Simulator 99 Pets

PS99 pets come in Normal, Huge, Titanic and Gargantuan sizes, each with Golden, Rainbow and Shiny versions. For damage, Titanics lead. DungeonPath's tier list (last updated June 2026) ranks the Titanic Persimmony Cricket as the strongest Titanic, ahead of the Titanic Valkyrie Pegasus and Titanic Easter Golem.

For trade value, Gamecap's November 2026 tier list puts variants like the Shiny Huge Pixel Shadow Griffin, Shiny Titanic Partygoer Cat and Rainbow Gargantuan Grim Reaper in S tier.

Farming Team Tips

  • Run your highest-damage Huges and Titanics for breaking.
  • Add coin-bonus pets and enchants (like Magnet) for farming.
  • Hatch from the newest zone's eggs; normal pets there beat old Huges for raw stats.

New PS99 Pets in Update 101 (Masquerade Games)

Update 101 went live November 7, 2026 with 16 new pets, per the official BIG Games Index:

  • Three new zones: Witch Village, Masquerade Ball and Thorn Spire.
  • Combine-o-Matic chain: Huge Bat Hoodie Bunny, Huge Witch Fox and Huge Thorn Dragon lead up to the Titanic Masquerade Agony and Gargantuan Butler Penguin.
  • Leaderboard prizes include the Titanic Phantom Tiger variants and Huge Jester Lion.
  • Event pets stay untradable until November 14, and solo scoring ends November 13.

How to Trade Pets Safely

  • Trade only through the game's trade window. Never "go first" by dropping or gifting pets.
  • Check two value lists before you accept a big trade.
  • Ignore anyone offering pets for real money, account logins or "duping". Selling items for cash breaks Roblox's Terms of Use and is where most scams happen.
  • Free pets from codes are safe. Our Adopt Me codes page tracks them, and the Roblox codes hub covers every other game.

Want free cosmetics instead? See free items in Roblox.

How to Choose the Best Roblox Pets for You

The best Roblox pets for you depend on your goal. If you trade, pick high-demand Adopt Me legendaries and add Fly and Ride potions. If you grind, pick the highest-damage Pet Simulator 99 pets you can hatch or afford. And if you just want something cute, any pet you enjoy is the right one.
