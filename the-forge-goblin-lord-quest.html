The Forge Goblin Lord Quest: How to Unlock the Goblin's Crown Recipe

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

The Goblin Lord quest is the Island 3 follow-up to the Goblin King's quests. Finish it and you unlock new forging recipes, including the Goblin's Crown. Fridge has a full walkthrough and a Short. Here are the exact costs from the Forge Wiki.

Where to Find the Goblin Lord

The Goblin Lord is a quest giver on the Frostspire Expanse, Island 3. You need to be level 70 or higher to travel there. Save plenty of Gold before you talk to him: every step costs more than the last.

Every Goblin Lord Quest Step

Let's get over it!Final stepRecipe unlock

The first four steps cost 300,000 Gold in total, plus the ores. Velchire, Sanctis and Snowite are all Legendary Frostspire ores. Velchire can also be crafted from 1 Aetherit and 1 Lightite, and Snowite from 1 Blue Crystal Ore, 2 Diamond and 3 Sapphire at the Crafting Stall.

The Goblin's Crown Recipe

The Goblin's Crown is a heavy helmet and one of the few recipes you unlock through a quest. Its requirements are level 75 plus finishing these quests. You can forge it on the Frostspire Expanse and Crimson Sakura.

  • Health: +26%, with a movement speed debuff.
  • Chance: 1 in 6 within heavy helmets.
  • Ores: 20 minimum, 26 recommended for the best odds, 35 to Lock Forge it.
  • Base price: 1,020 Gold.

For comparison, the Raven's Helmet gives +26.5% and the Shogun Helmet +34%. Compare every piece in our armor guide.

Is It Worth It?

Mostly for collectors. The Forge Wiki's Island 3 quest priority list marks these quests as very low priority and says the Goblin's Crown "offers no real benefits" over other helmets. Spend your first 300,000 Gold on Sensei Moro's and Bjorn's questlines, then the Raven questline. Come back when you have spare money. The Island 1 version is in our Goblin Cave guide.

The Forge FAQ

How much does the Goblin Lord quest cost in The Forge?

300,000 Gold across four steps, plus 1 Velchire, 2 Sanctis and 3 Snowite.

What do the Goblin Lord's quests unlock?

New forging recipes, including the Goblin's Crown heavy helmet, which also needs level 75.

Is the Goblin's Crown good?

It gives +26% Health, but the Forge Wiki rates it low priority because other helmets match or beat it.
