How to Forge in The Forge: Better Quality, Masterwork and the Best Katana

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Every weapon and armor piece comes from the anvil, so learning how to forge in The Forge well pays off fast. Fridge has a Short on getting a "perfect forge every time" and a full video on the best katana. Here's how forging works today and the current katana numbers.

How Forging Works

According to the Forge Wiki, the number of ores you put in decides your chance at each weapon type. The ores you choose decide the item's look, traits and power. Each item's multiplier is based on the ores' multipliers, so higher-multiplier ores make stronger, more valuable gear. Every recipe page lists three amounts:

  • Minimum ores: the base outcome chance.
  • Recommended ores: the best outcome chance.
  • Lock Forge: enough ores to guarantee that category. This was added in version 1.05 on January 18, 2026.

Once you know these three numbers, you can forge in The Forge with a plan instead of guessing.

Molds, added in version 1.08 on September 5, 2026, boost the odds further. Weapon Molds drop from the Reaper (1 in 32), Cthulhu (1 in 8) and Asura's Incarnate (1 in 18), and enough of them can give a 100% chance for select weapons.

Masterwork and the "Perfect Forge" Short

Fridge's December 22, 2025 Short shows a glitch for getting a top-quality, Masterwork forge every time. Treat it as a snapshot: glitches like this are usually patched, and the game has had several forging updates since. The legitimate ways to raise forge quality now are:

  • Use the recommended ore count, or the Lock Forge count, instead of the minimum.
  • The Dwarf race gives +5% Better Forge.
  • The Better Forge gamepass (449 Robux) increases forge quality. Fast Forge (299 Robux) makes forging faster, and the wiki says it skips the forging minigames.
  • Forge during the Forge Fever world event.

"Masterwork" is also the name of a monthly Questboard quest: enhance any item to +6 or +7 for Gold and XP.

Which Ores to Use

The ores matter as much as the count when you forge in The Forge. The wiki's weapon guide works in ore ratios for straight swords, the closest type to katanas. On the Forgotten Kingdom, the endgame mix is 4 Darkryte + 2 Magmaite, and Eye Ore + Rivalite works for midgame. On the Frostspire Expanse, endgame is 2 Gargantuan + 2 Golem Heart + 2 Voidstar. Scale those ratios up to the katana's 12 recommended ores. Get the ores from our Volcanic Depths guide, and add weapon runes with the runes guide.

The Forge FAQ

How do you get better forges in The Forge?

Use the recommended or Lock Forge ore count, use high-multiplier ores, and add molds. The Dwarf race and the Better Forge gamepass also raise quality.

What is the best katana in The Forge?

The Straight Edge Katana, with 9.35 base damage. Its blueprint is sold by Fungi in the Secret Maze.

Does the Masterwork glitch still work in The Forge?

Probably not. It's from December 2025, and the game has had several forging updates since.
