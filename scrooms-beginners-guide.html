How to Play Scrooms: Full Beginner's Guide (Roblox)

By Fridge · Published October 20, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Scrooms guide takes you from your very first catch to your first real upgrade. Scrooms is a Roblox creature-catching game where you tame mushroom-like creatures called Scrooms by playing music. You tune an instrument, keep the creature inside a catch zone, sell what you catch for Chimes, and use those Chimes to buy better instruments. If you just loaded into Songkeeper's Grove and have no idea what to do, start here.

How Scrooms Works: The Core Loop

Everything in Scrooms comes back to one simple loop:

  1. Catch Scrooms with your instrument.
  2. Sell them to Julian for Chimes, the main currency.
  3. Upgrade your instrument at the shop.
  4. Quest with NPCs for big chunks of XP and new tools.

Each catch and each quest gives XP, and your level unlocks the next stage of the main story. Every step in this Scrooms guide builds on that loop. Once you get it, the rest of the game is about doing it faster and catching rarer Scrooms.

Step 1: Get the Wooden Lute and Make Your First Catch

When you spawn, talk to Leo. He hands you a free Wooden Lute, your first catching instrument.

  1. Equip the lute from your hotbar and start playing.
  2. Follow the timing display, then hold and drag the frequency slider until the waveform settles. That's the tuning stage.
  3. When the catching stage appears, drag the Scroom so it stays inside the catch zone. The progress bar fills while it's inside and drains when it slips out.
  4. Keep following the creature until the bar completes and you see "You caught!"
Beginner mistake: Lots of new players try to move the catch zone. Move the mushroom marker instead. A slow, controlled catch beats rushing and failing.

Don't chase rare Scrooms yet. A successful catch matters way more than rarity at this point. After your catch, go back to Leo. The "Getting Started!" quest needs that conversation to complete, so catching alone won't finish it.

Step 2: Sell to Julian and Buy the Tamer's Guitar

Next, head to Julian at the wooden stall. Choose Sell Held or Sell Inventory and confirm. You'll get Chimes for every Scroom you sell.

Now buy the Tamer's Guitar for 100 Chimes. It's a big step up from the lute, with better precision, harmony and a small Luck bonus. To use it:

  1. Open your Equipment Bag.
  2. Select Instruments.
  3. Press Equip on the Tamer's Guitar.
  4. Close the bag. The tutorial objective won't clear until you do.

Return to Leo to finish the tutorial. He'll point you toward Halden.

Step 3: Find Halden and Start "The Next Step"

Halden sits by a campfire on a log near red tents in Songkeeper's Grove, next to the big Grove tree. Talk to him to start The Next Step. He asks you to Pay Respects at five statues around the Grove. Press E at each statue. Walking past doesn't count.

You can visit the statues in any order. Our Scrooms statue locations guide lists every landmark so you don't waste time running in circles.

Step 4: Get the Ocarina

Once your tracker shows 5/5, return to Halden and accept his gift: the Ocarina. Unlike your guitar, the Ocarina doesn't catch Scrooms. It plays utility songs. The first one you'll use is Homeward Melody, which teleports you back to your spawn point. Select it under Known Songs and enter the notes shown on screen.

Step 5: Start Leveling Up

After the Ocarina, the main quest Tamer in Training asks you to reach a level milestone and return to Halden. This is where most players slow down. The fix is simple: catch nonstop near the spawn signpost, where Common and Uncommon Scrooms make catches quick, and grab every quest marked with a green "?". We break down the fastest routine, including the XP pass and quest rewards, in our how to level up fast in Scrooms guide.

Scrooms Guide: Beginner Tips

  • Keep your instrument in hotbar slot 1 so you can start the next song the second a catch ends.
  • Use the Quest Log when a tutorial step seems stuck. It tells you exactly which part is active.
  • Check the Index to see which Scroom species you're still missing.
  • Right-click once if menus stop responding. Camera control mode can lock your mouse to the camera.
  • Don't AFK. Roblox disconnects idle players, so stay active while you grind.
  • Save up for the Violin of Pilgrimage (4,500 Chimes) once you're comfortable. It has much higher Luck, which helps you find rarer Scrooms.

Scrooms FAQ

How do you catch a Scroom?

Equip your instrument, follow the timing display, drag the frequency slider until the waveform settles, then keep the Scroom inside the catch zone until the progress bar fills.

Where is Halden in Scrooms?

Halden sits on a log by a campfire and red tents near the large tree in Songkeeper's Grove. Leo sends you to him after the tutorial.

How do you get the Ocarina in Scrooms?

Accept Halden's quest The Next Step, Pay Respects at five different statues in the Grove, then return to Halden to receive the Ocarina.

What is the best beginner instrument in Scrooms?

Buy the Tamer's Guitar for 100 Chimes right after your first sale. Later, save up for the Violin of Pilgrimage for much higher Luck.
