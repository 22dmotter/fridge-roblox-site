Phantom Express Ticket in Steal an Egg: All 4 Lost Echo Dog Items

By Fridge · Published October 20, 2026 · Last updated: November 10, 2026

The Steal an Egg Phantom Express Ticket lets you ride the haunted train to a ghost town where you collect Souls for the Spectral Mutation. To get it, you need to finish Echo's Memories, the Lost Echo Dog quest, by finding four lost items, then beat a fishing challenge. This guide covers every item location, the Haunted Rod, the underwater sea-mine run, and how to use the ticket once you have it.

Event status: This quest is part of the Halloween Dimension (Update 9), which Roblox listed for October 17–24, 2026. If the Halloween portal is still open in your server, follow along. If not, keep this page handy in case the dimension returns.

Where Is the Ghost Dog (Echo) in Steal an Egg?

  1. Enter the Halloween Dimension through the portal next to the Trails Shop at spawn.
  2. Head left toward the Graveyard. Look for the house with an orange witch's hat on the roof, three small tombs and a cauldron outside.
  3. Echo is the blue-eared ghost dog under a yellow exclamation mark, near three mausoleums.
  4. Talk to him to start Echo's Memories. A tracker reading "Find the dog's lost things (0/4)" appears.

All 4 Lost Echo Dog Item Locations

You can pick the items up in any order. Echo reacts with a line of dialogue each time you collect one (like "My collar! I remember the jingle!"), so you'll know it counted.

Lost Echo Dog item locations
ItemLocation
Old CollarPast the Phantom Express platform, across the tracks, beside a tree
Chewed BoneIn the swamp grass in front of the station, near the tracks
Favorite Toy (Mr. Squeaky)At the base of the Big Pumpkin Altar, close to Echo's spawn
Old PhotographAt the Abandoned Manor gate, left side, behind rocks by the fence and orange crystals
Most missed: the Chewed Bone sits low in the grass and is easy to run right past. Slow down near the station.

Step 2: Follow Echo and Get the Haunted Rod

  1. Return all four items to Echo. He remembers his owner fishes at the old pier and runs off.
  2. Follow Echo through the swamp to the wooden pier.
  3. Talk to the Ghost Fisherman at Gilbert's Hut.
  4. He rewards you with the Haunted Rod, which you keep permanently.

Returning the items does not give you the ticket by itself. If you have the rod but no ticket, nothing's broken. You still need to finish the fishing challenge.

Step 3: Win the Fishing Challenge for the Ticket

  1. Equip the Haunted Rod and stand on the glowing circular platform at the edge of the pier.
  2. Cast into the glowing ripples ("Hold to Cast") and reel quickly to win the short minigame.
  3. A huge creature drags you underwater. Steer left and right (A and D on PC) to dodge the sea mines.
  4. You have three lives, so one mistake won't end the run.
  5. Reach the chest at the end. "PHANTOM EXPRESS TICKET UNLOCKED!" confirms you've got it.

How to Use the Steal an Egg Phantom Express Ticket

The Phantom Express stops at the station on the right side of the map every 30 minutes, at :00 and :30. When it arrives, a countdown shows how long you have to board. Walk on with your ticket and you'll ride to an isolated ghost town. Reports say you only need the ticket for your first ride.

Souls and the Spectral Mutation

In the ghost town, grab the Ghost Vacuum and suck up spirits for Souls:

Souls per spirit
SpiritSouls
Lost Spirit10
Haunted Spirit35
Vengeful Spirit150
Ghouler (boss, 900 HP)225+, 1,000+ for top damage

Take your Souls to the Soul Extractor at the Soul Shrine. It trades 1,000 Souls for one Spectral Mutation. Use "Apply Mutation" on an egg. Each one has about a 25% success chance and is used up even if it fails, so farm extra. Always go for the Ghouler when it spawns, since the top damage dealer gets a huge Soul payout.

Quick Tips

  • Keep fishing with the Haunted Rod after you get the ticket. It's a solid source of Steal an Egg Candy Corn.
  • Be at the station a minute early so you don't miss the :00 or :30 train.
  • High Speed makes running between the Graveyard, swamp and manor much faster. See how to get speed in Steal an Egg.

Steal an Egg FAQ

How do you get the Phantom Express Ticket in Steal an Egg?

Find Echo's four lost items, return them, follow Echo to the Ghost Fisherman for the Haunted Rod, then fish at the glowing ripples and survive the underwater sea-mine run to reach the chest with the ticket.

Where are Echo's lost items in Steal an Egg?

The Old Collar is across the tracks past the station, the Chewed Bone is in the swamp grass in front of the station, Mr. Squeaky is at the Big Pumpkin Altar, and the Old Photograph is behind rocks at the Abandoned Manor gate.

When does the Phantom Express arrive?

The Phantom Express arrives every 30 minutes, on the hour and half-hour (:00 and :30).

Where is the ghost dog in Steal an Egg?

Echo the ghost dog is in the Graveyard of the Halloween Dimension, near three mausoleums and a house with an orange witch's hat on the roof.
