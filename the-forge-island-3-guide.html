The Forge Island 3 Guide: How to Complete the Frostspire Expanse (Winter Expansion)

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

The Forge Island 3, the Frostspire Expanse, arrived with the Winter Expansion on December 25, 2025, as version 1.02. Fridge posted a walkthrough that day and a full guide the next. Here's how to unlock it and what to do there, checked against the Forge Wiki.

Watch Fridge's Island 3 Videos

Fridge covered this topic in 2 full videos uploaded between December 25, 2025 and December 26, 2025. They are all embedded here, so you can watch and follow the written steps side by side.

Video date check: The Forge gets updates often. The newest is version 1.09, Halloween & Guilds, from October 20, 2026. Fridge's videos show the game as it was when he filmed them. The written steps on this page match the game as of November 11, 2026, and were checked against the Forge Wiki.

How to Unlock Island 3

  1. Reach level 70. Pro Game Guides lists 70+ as the requirement.
  2. Use the Wizard's portal to travel to the Frostspire Expanse.
  3. Start Bjorn's questline with Getting to Island 3: meet Bjorn, who stands up the path to the left of the docks.

Quests to Do First

The Forge Island 3 quests aren't equal. Here is the Forge Wiki's priority list for World 3:

  1. Sensei Moro's questline (top priority). It also unlocks better shop pickaxes.
  2. Bjorn's questline (top priority). It ends with the Golem boss.
  3. Angel's questline and Masked Stranger (high). These are quick money, and you can do them together because they share targets.
  4. Raven questline (high). It unlocks the Raven Cave and Raven armor. See our Raven Cave guide.
  5. Goblin Lord (low). See the Goblin Lord quest guide.

Pro Game Guides describes the Geyser shortcut as a frozen cannon that the Ice Man unfreezes once you bring him 1 Demonite, 3 Fireite and 5 Magmaite. Save those from Island 2.

The Golem Boss

The Golem is an ancient Ice Titan and the first boss added to the game. Its 25,000 HP scales with party size, and it has four attacks. It gives 15,000–30,000 Gold and 3,500 XP, and it can drop Golem Heart (1 in 8), Armor Molds, Miner Shards and Raid Tickets. Fighting tips are in our combat guide.

Best Island 3 Ores

The rarest Frostspire ores include Heavenite and Gargantuan (Divine), Golem Heart and Stolen Heart (Divine), Suryafal and Heart of the Island (Relic), and Iceite, Etherealite, Duranite and Yeti Heart (Mythic). You'll need at least a Void Pickaxe for The Peak's crystals, then upgrade to the Prismatic Pickaxe. With that, you're set for the rest of The Forge Island 3 content, from the Raven Cave to the Secret Maze.
