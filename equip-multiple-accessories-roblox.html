How to Equip Multiple Accessories on Roblox with Asset IDs (2026)

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Want to wear two hairs, a hat and a face accessory all at once? Here's how to equip multiple accessories on Roblox. When you click an item in the Avatar Editor, it can swap out an item of the same type. The fix is the editor's Advanced option, where you paste Asset IDs directly. Roblox Support confirms you can wear up to 10 accessories this way.

Why Roblox Removes Your Other Accessory

The normal Avatar Editor treats some accessory types as one slot each, so equipping a second hat or hair can replace the first. The Advanced menu skips that check and lets you list several items of the same type, up to the limits below.

Roblox accessory limits (per Roblox Support)
ItemLimit
Accessories totalUp to 10 at once
HatsSoft limit of 3 in the normal editor
OwnershipYou must own every item you add

Step 1: Find the Accessory's Asset ID

  1. Go to your Inventory or the Marketplace on the Roblox website and open the item.
  2. Look at the page URL: roblox.com/catalog/123456789/Item-Name.
  3. Copy the number in the middle. That's the Asset ID.
  4. Repeat for every accessory you want to stack.

Step 2: Open Advanced in the Avatar Editor

  1. On a computer or phone browser, open the Avatar Editor at roblox.com/my/avatar.
  2. Open the Accessories (or Hair/Head) category.
  3. Click Advanced (sometimes shown as "Advanced" under the item list).
  4. A box appears with fields for each accessory type, already filled with the IDs of what you're wearing.

Step 3: Paste the IDs and Save

  1. In the matching field (Hat, Hair, Face and so on), add the new Asset ID after the existing ones, separated by a comma, e.g. 11111111,22222222.
  2. Click Save.
  3. Check the avatar preview. If an item clips, try a different combination or adjust body scale.
  4. Join a game to confirm it shows in-game. Want a nice picture of the result? See our how to make Roblox thumbnails guide.

How to Equip Multiple Accessories on Roblox Mobile

The Roblox app's avatar screen may not show the Advanced option. Developer Forum users report the mobile editor limits stacking. The reliable workaround is to open roblox.com/my/avatar in your phone's browser, request the desktop site if needed, and follow the steps above. The saved avatar then appears in the app and on console.

Accessories Won't Stack? Fixes

  • "Invalid" error: you don't own the item, or you copied the wrong number. Use only the digits from the URL.
  • Over the limit: remove something; you can't go past 10 accessories.
  • Layered clothing: 3D layered jackets and shirts work differently, are limited per type and don't use the Advanced field the same way.
  • Game overrides: some experiences force their own avatars or remove accessories, so your look won't show there.
  • Changes not showing: rejoin the game; avatars load when you join.

Ideas for Stacked Looks

  • Two hairs for a fuller, custom hairstyle.
  • A hat plus a hair plus a face accessory for a complete character.
  • Show off the new outfit with animations; see how to emote on Roblox.

Quick Checklist

  1. Own every item before adding its ID.
  2. Use the website editor, not the app.
  3. Separate IDs with commas, with no spaces.
  4. Stay at 10 accessories or fewer, then click Save.

Roblox FAQ

How do you equip multiple accessories on Roblox?

Open the Avatar Editor on the Roblox website, click Advanced, and paste the Asset IDs of items you own into the matching field, separated by commas. Then click Save.

How many accessories can you wear on Roblox?

Roblox Support says up to 10 accessories, with a soft limit of 3 hats in the normal editor.

Can you equip multiple accessories on Roblox mobile?

The app may not show the Advanced option. Open roblox.com/my/avatar in your phone's browser and use Advanced there.

Where do I find an accessory's Asset ID?

Open the item's page on the Roblox website. The number in the URL between /catalog/ and the item name is the Asset ID.

Can you wear two hairs on Roblox?

Yes. Add the second hair's Asset ID in the Hair field of the Advanced menu, separated by a comma, then save.
