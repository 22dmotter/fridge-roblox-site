Aite Ore in The Forge: Drop Chance and Price

Last checked: November 15, 2026

Aite is an Epic ore from Stonewake's Cross. Sources agree it drops from Boulders at 1-in-44, sells for $16.50, and has no trait. They do not agree on the multiplier, so that field is left blank on this page.

How to Get Aite

Mine Boulders in Stonewake's Cross. The official Forge Wiki, Game8, Destructoid, and BloxInformer all list a 1/44 drop chance from Boulders there.

What Aite Is Good For

Aite has no trait and a confirmed sell price of $16.50. We do not list a multiplier, because sources disagree (see below). For reaching Boulders, see the pickaxe progression guide.
