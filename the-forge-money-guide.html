How to Make Money in The Forge Fast: Every Method Fridge Uses

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Gold buys every pickaxe, potion and enhancement, so learning to make money in The Forge speeds up everything else. Fridge has made ten videos and Shorts on money farming. Here's what still works as of November 2026, checked against the Forge Wiki.

Watch Fridge's Money-Making Videos and Shorts

Fridge covered this topic in 3 full videos and 7 Shorts uploaded between December 9, 2025 and January 6, 2026. They are all embedded here, so you can watch and follow the written steps side by side.

The Shorts play in a vertical player. Tap one to watch it here, or open it on YouTube.

Video date check: The Forge gets updates often. The newest is version 1.09, Halloween & Guilds, from October 20, 2026. Fridge's videos show the game as it was when he filmed them. The written steps on this page match the game as of November 11, 2026, and were checked against the Forge Wiki.

Method 1: Forge and Sell Heavy Gear

The Forge Wiki's money guide lists forging and selling as the main money method. Craft heavy armor or Colossal Swords and sell them at the weapon shop (Marbles on Stonewake's Cross, Wo on the Forgotten Kingdom and Frostspire Expanse, Toshino on Crimson Sakura). Rarer items, and items made with higher-multiplier ores, sell for much more. The wiki currently recommends forging for profit on Crimson Sakura.

  • Heavy armor pieces start at the highest base prices. For example, heavy chestplates have a 1,355 Gold base price.
  • Use lots of ore. Heavy items need more ore, and the multiplier, which comes from the average of the ores you use, drives the price up.
  • Don't sell raw ores to Greedy Cey. Pro Game Guides points out that they're worth more forged into an item.

Method 2: Farm Mobs for Essences

The wiki's top tip is to farm enemies for essences in the Volcanic Depths (Forgotten Kingdom) and at The Peak (Frostspire Expanse). Mobs there drop Epic and Superior Essence, which sell well and also power your own enhancements. Our Volcanic Depths guide shows how to get in.

Method 4: Luck Potions and Events

Fridge's Short on Luck Potions is about mining more rare ores per trip. Maria's shop sells:

  • Luck Potion: 350 Gold, +20% Luck for 300 seconds.
  • Luck Potion II: 1,400 Gold, +40% Luck for 300 seconds.
  • Fungi's Potion: 17,500 Gold, 20% faster mining, +15% mining damage and +15% Luck for 600 seconds.

Stack them with world events. A new event rolls at the start of every real-life hour, and Fortune, Ultra Fortune and Gold Rush are the ones to farm. The Goblin race's 12% discount also cuts what you spend.

What Happened to the Lucky Block?

Fridge's December 2025 Short showed the secret Lucky Block in a hidden hut on Stonewake's Cross. It still exists. It has 10,000 HP and a 100x luck multiplier, but the Forge Wiki says it now drops a single Fichillium, a Relic "fish" ore with 0 value and no multiplier. It's a fun secret, but it's no longer a money farm.

Quick Money Tips

  • Claim every Mailbox reward first.
  • Remove runes before you sell an item.
  • Keep Demonite for Island 3, where the Ice Man's quest asks for it.
  • Want hands-off farming? See our AFK farming guide.

The Forge FAQ

What is the fastest way to make money in The Forge?

Forge heavy armor or Colossal Swords from high-multiplier ores and sell them at the weapon shop, while farming essences from Volcanic Depths or The Peak mobs.

Does the Lucky Block still give money in The Forge?

No. It drops one Fichillium, which has 0 value, according to the Forge Wiki.

Should I sell ores in The Forge?

Usually not. Ores are worth more forged into weapons and armor.
