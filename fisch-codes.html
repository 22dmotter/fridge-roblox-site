How to Redeem Fisch Codes

  1. Launch Fisch on Roblox.
  2. Press the Menu button at the top of the screen.
  3. Scroll all the way down to the Codes section.
  4. Click the Type Code Here box and enter the code.
  5. Press Enter to claim it.

GamesRadar+ says codes aren't case-sensitive, but a trailing space will break them. Many limited-time codes need level 25.

Free 5,000C$ Group Chest

Under the code box there's a chest marked "Like and Join the Group to Claim." It isn't needed for codes, but GamesRadar+ says that if you join the Fisching community group on Roblox, then come back and tap the chest, you get a 5,000C$ bonus. It's the biggest free cash reward in the game right now.

Expired Fisch Codes

Recently expired according to the official wiki: FischFright2026 (ended November 9, 11 AM ET), TheVenueEncore (November 2), ChapelTroubles, ThanksFor5B, TemporarySubmarine, SaveNate, Happy2ndYear, omoji and spookyomoji. Older expired codes include VroomVroom, MarianaIsGoingCrazy, LittleBudlingUpdate, SkycrestIsInTheSky and many more in Destructoid's archive.

How Often New Codes Drop

Fisch releases codes often, usually with each update or milestone, but most last only a day or two. That's why so many lists are out of date. Check the official wiki's dates before trusting any list. Fisch FAQ

What Fisch promo codes work right now?

As of November 11, 2026: SCARLET (Scarlet skin for Nate's Blade, until December 15) and CARBON (Carbon Bobber, permanent).

Does ThanksFor2MillionMembers still work in Fisch?

The official Fisch Wiki says it expired on November 9, 2026, though a few sites still list it.

How do you redeem codes in Fisch?

Press Menu at the top of the screen, scroll to the Codes section, type the code in the box and press Enter.

Are Fisch promo codes case-sensitive?

No, but extra spaces will break them. Many limited codes also need level 25.

How do I get free C$ in Fisch?

Join the Fisching group on Roblox, then tap the chest under the code box for 5,000C$.

When does the SCARLET code expire in Fisch?

December 15, 2026 at 11 AM ET (16:00 UTC), according to the official Fisch Wiki.

Does CARBON expire in Fisch?

No. The official Fisch Wiki lists the CARBON code, which gives the Carbon Bobber, as never expiring.
