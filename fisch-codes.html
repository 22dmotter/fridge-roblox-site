Unverified Fisch Codes and Claims

Single-source codes and claims stay in this unverified list until a second independent source agrees. None of the current lists had a new code that only one source called active.

  • GamesRadar+ is the only source in this check that describes a 5,000 C$ bonus chest under the code box after you join the Fisching community group on Roblox. That is not a promo code, and it is not confirmed here.
  • Destructoid (updated November 16, 2026) still showed ThanksFor2MillionMembers, TheVenueEncore and FischFright2026 in its active list. The official wiki, GamesRadar+, Radio Times and Beebom all list those as expired, so they are in the expired list below, not treated as unverified actives.
  • Older Pocket Tactics and PCGamesN pages last updated October 7, 2026 still showed TemporarySubmarine as working. The official wiki, GamesRadar+ and Radio Times list it as expired (the wiki's expiry is October 18, 2026, 11 AM ET).

How to Redeem Fisch Codes

  1. Launch Fisch on Roblox.
  2. Press the Menu button at the top of the screen.
  3. Scroll all the way down to the Codes section.
  4. Click the Type Code Here box and enter the code.
  5. Press Enter to claim it.

These steps match GamesRadar+ and Radio Times (Menu, scroll to the Codes section at the bottom, Type Code Here, Enter). The official wiki says codes are redeemed in the Codes section of the Settings menu and are not case-sensitive. GamesRadar+ also says they are not case-sensitive, but a trailing space will break them. Beebom describes opening Codes from a left sidebar instead of scrolling; that layout difference is not treated as a conflict on the Menu or Codes field itself.

How Often New Codes Drop

Fisch often releases codes with updates or milestones, and many last only a day or two, which is why some lists stay out of date. The official wiki's Codes page is the best place to check expiry times, but it can lag after a new drop: it still omitted CompanionHatTime in this check because it was last revised November 9, 2026. Fisch FAQ

What Fisch codes work right now?

As of November 16, 2026, three codes are confirmed by at least two sources: CompanionHatTime (1,000 Coins, 50 Instant Catcher, 50 Luminous Larva, a hat title, and the Violet Valkyrie companion hat), SCARLET (Scarlet skin for Nate's Blade, until December 15, 2026, 11 AM ET), and CARBON (Carbon Bobber, permanent).

Is CompanionHatTime a working Fisch code?

Yes. Radio Times and Beebom both listed it as active on or after November 14, 2026, with the same rewards, and GamesRadar+ also listed it as a new active code on November 16. The official Fisch Wiki had not added it yet; that Codes page was last revised November 9, 2026.

When does the SCARLET code expire in Fisch?

December 15, 2026 at 11 AM ET (16:00 UTC). The official Fisch Wiki and GamesRadar+ both list that expiry date.

Does CARBON expire in Fisch?

No. The official Fisch Wiki lists CARBON, which gives the Carbon Bobber, as never expiring, and GamesRadar+ calls it a permanent code.

How do you redeem codes in Fisch?

Press Menu at the top of the screen, scroll to the Codes section at the bottom, type the code in the Type Code Here box, and press Enter. Codes are not case-sensitive, but a trailing space will break them.

Are Fisch promo codes case-sensitive?

No. The official Fisch Wiki and GamesRadar+ both say codes are not case-sensitive. Do not leave a trailing space.

Do any active Fisch codes require level 25?

No level requirement is confirmed for the three active codes in this check. The official wiki lists SCARLET and CARBON as having no level requirement. A level 25+ requirement for CompanionHatTime appeared in only one source and conflicted with that same article, so it is not listed here.

Does ThanksFor2MillionMembers still work in Fisch?

No. The official Fisch Wiki, GamesRadar+ and Beebom list it as expired. The wiki's expiry is November 9, 2026, at 12 PM ET. A few trackers still showed it as active.
