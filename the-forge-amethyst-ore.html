Amethyst Ore in The Forge: Drop Chance and Price

Last checked: November 14, 2026

Amethyst is a Rare ore from the Forgotten Kingdom. Sources agree it drops from Basalt Cores and Basalt Veins at 1-in-115, with a 1.65x multiplier, a $24.75 sell price, and no trait.

How to Get Amethyst

Mine Basalt Cores or Basalt Veins in the Forgotten Kingdom. The official Forge Wiki, Game8, Destructoid, and BloxInformer all list a 1/115 drop chance from those nodes.

What Amethyst Is Good For

Amethyst has no trait, a 1.65x multiplier, and a $24.75 sell price. See the forging guide and the beginner guide for how mined ores fit the crafting loop.
