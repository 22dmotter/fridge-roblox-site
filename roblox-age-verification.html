Roblox Age Verification: How to Complete an Age Check in 2026

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Roblox age verification now matters for everyone. Since January 2026, Roblox requires an age check to use chat, rolled out worldwide wherever chat is available. This guide covers the two official methods (selfie Facial Age Estimation and ID verification), the age groups and how to fix a wrong result, all from Roblox's own pages. Why Roblox Checks Your Age

Roblox announced on January 7, 2026 that users in the U.S. must complete an age check to chat, with the rule rolling out to all chat regions over the next week. Roblox calls itself the first large gaming platform to require age checks for chat for all ages. The goal is age-appropriate chat and fewer conversations between adults and minors. Age checks are optional, but features like chat stay locked until you complete one.

What Needs an Age Check on Roblox

Per Roblox Support, you need an age check to:

  • Use chat.
  • Chat without filters with Trusted Friends.
  • Use Team Create in Roblox Studio.
  • Access 16+ and 18+ content, and games rated "Content May Vary" (16+).
  • Play games outside the Roblox Kids and Roblox Select catalogs.
  • Invite or be an early tester for a game.

Without a check, Roblox uses your sign-up birthday: under-9s go to Roblox Kids and 9+ to Roblox Select, both with chat off. Voice chat has its own requirements too.

How to Do the Roblox Selfie Age Check

Facial Age Estimation is the fastest way. You need a device with a working camera.

  1. Open Settings. Desktop: the gear icon at top right. Mobile: More (…) at bottom right, then Settings.
  2. Select Account info.
  3. Select Age Group.
  4. Select Continue with selfie and allow camera access.
  5. Follow the prompts: center your face, then turn left and right.

Roblox says images and video are processed by its vendor, Persona, and deleted right after processing. In some regions a parent's approval is needed first.

Tip: virtual cameras, streaming software and camera-effect apps can break the check. Close them, pick your real webcam, or use your phone instead.

Roblox ID Verification (13+ Only)

If you're 13 or older you can use a government photo ID (driver's license, passport, residency card) instead:

  1. Go to Settings > Account Info.
  2. Under your birthday, select Continue on "Use an ID to verify your birthday".
  3. Allow camera access, scan your ID, and follow the steps.

If Roblox can't estimate your age from a selfie, you'll need ID verification. Some Roblox communities also require ID verification to join.

Roblox Age Groups and Who You Can Chat With

After a check you're placed in one of six age groups. Roblox's newsroom lists them as under 9, 9–12, 13–15, 16–17, 18–20 and 21+ (Roblox Support writes the youngest as 5–8). By default you can chat with your own group and the groups directly above and below. A 9–12 user can chat with under-9, 9–12 and 13–15, for example.

If the selfie check places you under 13, Roblox removes some personal data from the account, like email and phone number.

How to Fix a Wrong Roblox Age

  • Account age over 13: Settings > Account info > Continue with ID to verify with ID.
  • Account age under 13: a linked parent can change the birthday through Parental Controls, or use the pencil icon next to the birthday.
  • You might be asked to check again if Roblox spots a mismatch later.

Helping Your Child With an Age Check

Roblox suggests parents link their own account first and do a check themselves to learn the steps. When it's your child's turn, make sure they are in front of the camera, not you, so the result matches the player.

Scam warning: only do age checks inside the Roblox app or roblox.com. Ignore anyone offering "verified accounts" or asking you to verify on another site.

Is Roblox Age Verification Safe?

Roblox age verification runs through Roblox's own app and its vendor Persona, and Roblox says selfie images and video are deleted right after processing. ID checks need a real government photo ID. Only start an age check from Roblox Settings or an in-app prompt, never from a link in a message.

Roblox FAQ

Is Roblox age verification required?

Age checks are optional, but since January 2026 you need one to use chat and several other features.

How do I verify my age on Roblox?

Go to Settings, Account info, Age Group, then Continue with selfie, or use ID verification if you're 13 or older.

Does Roblox keep my selfie?

Roblox says images and video from Facial Age Estimation are deleted right after processing.

Can under-13s use ID verification on Roblox?

No. ID verification is only for users 13 and older; younger users use the selfie check or a parent's help.

What are the Roblox age groups?

Under 9, 9-12, 13-15, 16-17, 18-20 and 21+. You can chat with your own group and the ones next to it.
