The Forge Bard Quest: Find the Lost Guitar (Plus the Best Secret Quests)

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

The Bard quest ("Bard's Lost Guitar") is a short Stonewake's Cross side quest that leads to one of the best early pickaxes. Fridge has a full walkthrough, a Short on the guitar's location, and two videos on secret quests. This page combines all of them.

Where to Find the Bard

The Bard sits by the campfire near spawn in Stonewake's Cross, to the left of the Forge and past Maria's Potion Shop. He's a weary musician who has lost his guitar, according to the Forge Wiki. Talk to him to start Lost Guitar.

How to Complete the Lost Guitar Quest

  1. Talk to the Bard at the campfire to accept the quest.
  2. Go to the cave entrance and look for the green leaves beside it. They hide a secret cave, as described on the Forge Wiki's Arcane Pickaxe page.
  3. Pick up the guitar inside the secret cave.
  4. Bring it back to the Bard to finish the quest. You get 250 XP, 500 Gold and the Unknown Key.
  5. Use the key on the locked gate of the Fallen Angel's Cave. Pro Game Guides says to keep to the right wall inside the Stonewake cave until you see it.

What the Unknown Key Unlocks: The Arcane Pickaxe

Behind the gate is the Arcane Pickaxe. It has 115 Mine Power, +10% speed, +50% Luck and 3 rune slots. It's Legendary and costs 125,000 Gold, or 110,000 with the Goblin race discount. Pro Game Guides calls it the pickaxe to come back for once you've earned money on Island 2. The Forge Wiki's current guide is less keen: it marks the Bard quest as low priority on World 1 because the Arcane Pickaxe is "not viable" next to newer pickaxes, but top priority on World 2 alongside Sensei Moro.

The Forge FAQ

Where is the Bard's guitar in The Forge?

In a secret cave hidden behind green leaves beside the Stonewake's Cross cave entrance.

What do you get for the Bard quest in The Forge?

250 XP, 500 Gold and the Unknown Key, which opens the Fallen Angel's Cave and the Arcane Pickaxe.

How much is the Arcane Pickaxe in The Forge?

125,000 Gold, or 110,000 with the Goblin race discount.
