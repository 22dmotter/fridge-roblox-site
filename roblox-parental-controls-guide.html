Roblox Parental Controls in 2026: How to Set Them Up Step by Step

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Roblox parental controls let a parent choose what games a child can play, who they can chat with, how much they can spend and how long they can play. In 2026 you can manage them two ways: by linking your own parent account to your child's (best for remote control from your phone) or by using Parent mode on your child's device. Every Roblox parental controls setting below comes from Roblox Support's Parental Controls Overview.

Step 1: Link a Parent Account

  1. Create or sign in to your own Roblox account.
  2. Follow Roblox's "Parents: How to Link Your Account" steps (linked in Sources below) to connect your account to your child's.
  3. Open Settings (gear icon on desktop; More → Settings on mobile), choose Parental controls and select your child. Scroll to Settings you manage.

Prefer not to make an account? Use Parent mode: on your child's device, you can approve requests and manage most settings without your own Roblox account. Real-time alerts like spending notifications need a linked parent account.

Step 2: Set Content Maturity

Roblox content maturity levels
LevelWhat it allows
MinimalOccasional mild violence, light unrealistic blood and/or occasional mild fear
MildRepeated mild violence, heavy unrealistic blood, mild crude humor and/or repeated mild fear
ModerateModerate violence, light realistic blood, moderate crude humor and/or moderate fear (social hangouts in this tier are 16+ age checked only)
RestrictedStrong violence, heavy realistic blood, strong language and more; only for age-checked users 18+

Under Content restrictions you can also add Blocked games (blocks take effect the next time your child joins) and Allowed games for specific titles above the chosen level.

Step 3: Manage Chat

  • Experience chat: chat inside games. Children under 9 need parental consent to turn it on.
  • Direct chat: chat outside games. Children under 13 need parental consent.
  • Voice chat is only for age-checked users 13+; see how to turn on voice chat on Roblox.
  • Trusted Friends: under-13s need your approval for each one; see our how to add Trusted Friends on Roblox guide.

Step 4: Set Spending Limits

  • Monthly spending limit: under Spending restrictions, enter 0 to 10,000 in your local currency. It resets at the end of each calendar month, doesn't roll over, may not apply on some consoles, and stops applying at 13.
  • High spend alerts: on by default for linked parents; you get an email at $100, $250 and $500 USD spent in a month.
  • Robux transfers: kids can send Robux to Trusted Friends; defaults are 500 per day and 1,000 per month, and you can require approval for each transfer.
  • The spending limit doesn't affect gift card redemption; here's how to redeem a Roblox gift card.

Step 5: Set a Screen Time Limit

Under Screen time → Manage, pick a daily limit in 15-minute steps. When time runs out, the child can't use Roblox for the rest of the day. You can also see a 7-day chart and their top 20 games. From 13, teens manage their own limit.

Step 6: Visibility and Activity Updates

  • Under Visibility & private servers, choose who sees your child's online status and current game: Friends, Trusted friends or No one. Teens can do this themselves too; see how to appear offline on Roblox.
  • Turn on Activity updates for a weekly email with screen time and top games.
  • Set Private servers to Friends or No one.

Roblox Parental Controls Not Working?

  1. Wrong account: make sure you picked the right child in Parental controls.
  2. Setting missing: settings change with age. From 13 teens manage screen time and blocking, and for 16+ parents only see notifications and activity updates.
  3. Game still open: a blocked game only stops working the next time your child joins it.
  4. "Age check needed": many chat options need your child to finish an age check (Settings → Account info). Our how to verify age on Roblox guide covers the official selfie and ID steps.
  5. Region: some options, like Preview games, aren't available everywhere.

Roblox FAQ

How do I set up parental controls on Roblox?

Link your own parent account to your child's account or use Parent mode on the child's device, then open Settings > Parental controls to set content maturity, chat, spending, screen time and visibility.

Can I set a Roblox spending limit?

Yes. Monthly spending limits range from 0 to 10,000 in your local currency, reset monthly and apply until the child turns 13.

Can parents limit screen time on Roblox?

Yes. Parents can set a daily screen time limit in 15-minute steps for children under 13.

Are Roblox parental controls free?

Yes. All Roblox parental controls are free. You only need a Roblox account of your own if you want to link it and manage settings remotely.

Do I need a Roblox account to use parental controls?

No. You can use Parent mode on your child's device, but linking your own account lets you manage settings remotely.
