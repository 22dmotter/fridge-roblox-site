Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains who operates Roblox Guides, what information is collected when you visit robloxguides.org, how advertising and embedded videos may use cookies, and what choices you have. Please read it carefully.

Who we are

Roblox Guides is an independent informational website about Roblox codes, game guides, events and tips. It is the written companion to the Fridge YouTube channel (@Fridgetherealone) and is operated by Daniel Motter. The site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation or any game developer. “Roblox” and related marks belong to their owners.

For how to reach us, see the Contact page. There is no account system on this site.

What this site collects

Roblox Guides itself does not ask you to create an account, does not run a comment form, and does not include a contact or newsletter form that collects your name or email. We do not sell products through a checkout on this site.

Server logs

Like most websites, the hosting server may automatically record technical data when your browser requests a page. That typically includes your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page or file requested, the referring URL if your browser sends one, and your browser or device user-agent. These logs are used to operate and secure the site, diagnose errors, and understand aggregate traffic. We do not use server logs to build a marketing profile of you. Retention is controlled by the hosting environment and is limited to what is needed for those purposes.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics through a Google tag (container ID GTM-54BMLPB9). The tag collects usage data such as pages viewed, device and browser information, approximate location, and the referring site, using cookies or similar identifiers. Google may also process this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can opt out of Google Analytics in some browsers with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. This site does not include a Facebook, TikTok or similar tracking pixel.

YouTube embeds

Many guides can include Fridge videos or YouTube Shorts. Full-length videos on this site use a click-to-play thumbnail and, when you choose to play them, load from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com). Shorts, when a matching video exists, also use youtube-nocookie.com iframes. We do not load a YouTube player until you click a full video, except where a Short is actually published on the page.

Even in privacy-enhanced mode, Google or YouTube may still set or read cookies or similar identifiers, collect your IP address, and receive the page URL, especially if you are signed in to a Google account. That processing is governed by Google’s policies, including How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. If you do not want that to happen, do not play the embeds and consider using browser controls described below.

Advertising, Google AdSense and cookies

This site may display advertisements, including ads served by Google AdSense and its advertising partners. We do not currently include AdSense ad code in pages until an official publisher account is approved, but the following disclosures apply whenever Google or other third-party vendors serve ads on Roblox Guides.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to this and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info (including the DAA consumer opt-out) and, for many companies in Europe, at www.youronlinechoices.eu.

To understand how Google uses data when you use sites or apps that use Google services, please read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Google may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses and other identifiers to serve and measure ads, limit how often you see an ad, detect fraud and abuse, and — depending on your settings and, where required, your consent — personalize ads.

We do not control the cookies that Google or other ad vendors set once ads are enabled. Those companies act as independent controllers or processors of the information they collect, according to their own policies.

Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device. Similar technologies include pixels, local storage and tags. This site may involve:

  • Essential or operational data such as server logs needed to deliver pages and keep the site secure.
  • Google Analytics / Google tag cookies or identifiers used to measure visits, as described in the Analytics section.
  • YouTube / Google cookies or identifiers if you play an embedded video, as described above.
  • Advertising cookies if and when Google AdSense or other ad vendors run on the site, as described in the advertising section.

You can usually delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect embeds or, later, how ads are shown (for example, you may still see ads that are less relevant). Browser “Do Not Track” signals are not consistently honored across the industry; this site does not currently respond to DNT as a separate control.

EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland: Google’s EU User Consent Policy requires publishers to obtain legally valid consent, where required, for cookies or local storage and for the collection, sharing and use of personal data for ads personalization, and to use a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) that works with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework when serving personalized ads to users in those regions. We have not built an unofficial or “fake” cookie banner, because that would not meet Google’s requirements. After AdSense approval, the operator intends to enable Google’s own Privacy & messaging tools (or another Google-certified CMP) for GDPR and, as applicable, US state messages. Until that CMP is live and ads are served, we are not asking you to consent to advertising cookies through a banner on this site.

Children’s privacy (COPPA)

Roblox Guides publishes general-audience information about a popular game platform. The site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. We do not ask visitors for a date of birth, and we do not operate accounts, comments or profiles on this website.

We know that many Roblox players are young, and that a parent or guardian may visit these pages with a child. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us through this site, please contact us as described on the Contact page and we will delete it if we have it.

Advertising products have special rules for child-directed content. The operator will review Google’s child-directed treatment settings in AdSense and apply the tag that honestly matches this site. Visitors and parents should not treat this website as a service designed for young children.

Rights in the European Economic Area and United Kingdom

If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, you may have rights to request access to personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to data portability, subject to legal limits. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (for example, a European supervisory authority or the UK Information Commissioner’s Office).

Our legal bases, when those laws apply, include: operating the site and keeping it secure (legitimate interests, and where applicable our need to provide the website); complying with law; and, once a certified CMP and ads are in use, consent for advertising cookies and ads personalization where consent is required. Because this site does not run its own user accounts, we may only be able to identify you from information you include in a request (for example, an IP address and a date of a visit). To make a GDPR or UK GDPR request, email blazingblox49@gmail.com or use the Contact page.

California, other US state privacy rights, and “Do Not Sell or Share”

If you are a resident of California or of another US state that has a consumer privacy law (including, where applicable, the CCPA/CPRA and similar state statutes), you may have the right to request that we disclose the personal information we have collected about you, to request deletion or correction, to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information (including sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising), and to not be discriminated against for exercising those rights. You may also have the right to use an authorized agent, subject to verification.

We do not sell personal information for money. If Google AdSense or similar advertising is enabled, some uses of cookies or identifiers for personalized ads may be treated as “sharing” or targeted advertising under those laws. When ads are live, we intend to offer required opt-out tools, including through Google’s Privacy & messaging / US state messages where available, and through the opt-out links in the advertising section above. To make a request, email blazingblox49@gmail.com or use the Contact page. We will take reasonable steps to verify a request and will describe if we cannot complete it.

This site does not currently have a first-party cookie banner or a “Do Not Sell or Share” link of its own, because we are not yet running a certified advertising consent or opt-out interface and we do not collect account data. That will be updated when ads and a compliant messaging tool are enabled.

Guides may link to official Roblox help pages, YouTube, game-related resources, and other independent sites (including third-party tool sites such as ExoPanda). Those links are not sponsored unless a page clearly says so. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of websites we do not operate. Their policies apply when you leave Roblox Guides.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the site, our hosting, advertising, or the law changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. The revised policy applies from that date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
