About Roblox Guides and Fridge

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Roblox Guides is an independent site of Roblox codes, walkthroughs, event coverage and practical tips. It is written as the companion to the Fridge YouTube channel (@Fridgetherealone) and is operated by Daniel Motter. It is not affiliated with Roblox Corporation or with any game developer.

Who runs this site

Fridge is a Roblox creator who makes concise videos on working codes, leveling, collectibles and events. Daniel Motter operates this website so the same research can be read, copied and checked without scrubbing through a video. The header brand is Roblox Guides; Fridge remains the name of the channel and the byline on guides. You can watch new uploads on @Fridgetherealone or use the Contact page to reach the channel.

What the site covers

  • Codes: working lists for popular games, plus honest pages when a game has no code system — for example Anime Dice codes, Huzz Valley codes and Steal an Egg codes.
  • Guides: walkthroughs such as how to play Scrooms, location guides, and Roblox help topics like voice chat, parental controls and gift cards.
  • Events: confirmed event recaps and carefully labeled leak coverage, such as the Roblox Hollow event guide.
  • Tips: practical pages on speed, lag, official FPS settings and similar topics.

Some pages also discuss unofficial tools that people search for, such as macros or launchers. Those articles explain risks and official alternatives. They do not host downloads of exploits or executors, and they are not permission to break Roblox’s rules.

Editorial and verification policy

Accuracy matters more than being first. Our standing rules are:

  • Two-source rule for working codes. A code is marked working only when at least two independent sources agree or when it appears on an official channel (for example, a developer’s official social account or an in-game inbox). One unverified screenshot is not enough.
  • No invented codes, leaks, items or dates. If we cannot confirm a detail, we say so. Rumors are labeled as rumors.
  • Expired and disputed codes are flagged. When a code stops working, the page should say it is expired rather than leave it in a working list.
  • Games with no codes. If an experience has no redeem system, the page states that clearly instead of padding a fake list.
  • Visible update dates. Guides show a published date and a “Last updated” date so you can see when the page was last checked.

Code pages are re-checked when a new Fridge video goes up and when official sources change. Event and systems pages are updated when a new, checkable fact appears. We still recommend confirming a code in-game, because publishers can disable codes without notice.

Ads, affiliates and independence

The Site may display advertisements, including Google ads, to help support the work. Ads will be identifiable as ads. Links to third-party sites or tools (including ExoPanda) are for context and are not sponsored unless a page says otherwise. We do not accept payment to invent a “working” code or to hide a ban risk.

Watch and subscribe

New videos are posted on the Fridge channel. The written guides often include a click-to-play player when a matching video exists. If a page does not have a video yet, you can still follow the written steps and subscribe for future uploads.
