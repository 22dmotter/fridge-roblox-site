About Fridge: Roblox Guides on YouTube

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Fridge (@Fridgetherealone) is a Roblox YouTube channel that makes quick, useful guides: working codes, the fastest ways to level up and get stronger, collectible locations, and full coverage of big Roblox events.

What Fridge covers

  • Codes: working code lists for games like Anime Dice and Huzz Valley, plus honest answers when a game like Steal an Egg has no codes at all.
  • Guides: beginner walkthroughs such as how to play Scrooms and location guides like the Monkey Escape shards.
  • Events: leaks, confirmed games and badge guides for events like the Roblox Hollow event.
  • Tips: speed, XP and racing tricks that save you hours of grinding.

Past videos have also covered The Forge (pickaxe upgrades, money-making methods and Crimson Depths), Steal an Egg rare eggs and more.

How this site works

Each guide on this site goes with a Fridge video. The written version lets you copy codes, scan tables and follow numbered steps at your own pace. Guides show a visible "Last updated" date, and code pages are rechecked against current sources whenever a new video goes up. If something has expired or changed, the page says so. We never list made-up codes or present rumors as facts.
