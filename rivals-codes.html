How to Redeem RIVALS Codes

  1. Follow a developer first. On Roblox, follow @Nosniy or @SenseiWarrior. The RIVALS Wiki says codes only redeem if you follow at least one of them.
  2. Launch RIVALS and stay in the lobby.
  3. Open the Shop and press the Rewards button.
  4. Complete the one-time verify step if the game asks for it.
  5. Type a code exactly and press Redeem. Repeat for each code.
"Code invalid"? Double-check that you followed a developer on the same account, then retype the code without spaces. Event codes like the Spooky Prize Wheel ones can stop working without warning, so redeem them first.

How the RIVALS Code Board Works

RIVALS has a code board in the lobby. According to the RIVALS Wiki, it refreshes every 50,000 likes, and the codes follow a FREE[number] pattern where the number goes up by one at each milestone. That means the next board code is expected to be FREE207, but don't type it until it appears on the board. Older numbers stop working once the board moves on.

Bigger updates usually bring codes from Nosniy Games' official X account. Season 4 starts November 13 at 9:00 PM ET (November 14, 02:00 UTC), so check back then.

Tips for Spending Your Free Keys

  • Keys open weapon cases, so save a few up rather than opening one at a time.

Quick Checklist Before You Redeem

  1. Follow @Nosniy or @SenseiWarrior on Roblox first. This is the step most players miss.
  2. Copy each code exactly. Board codes use capital letters and numbers, like FREE206.
  3. Redeem one at a time in Shop → Rewards and wait for the confirmation.

RIVALS FAQ

Why are my RIVALS promo codes not working?

You must follow @Nosniy or @SenseiWarrior on Roblox before redeeming. Also check the spelling and that the code hasn't expired; FREE board codes stop working when the board moves to the next number.

Where do you redeem RIVALS promo codes?

In the lobby, open the Shop and press the Rewards button, then enter the code and press Redeem.

How many free Keys can I get from RIVALS promo codes?

At least 10 Keys right now: 3 from FREE206, 5 from ROBLOX_RTC, and 1 each from BONUS and BOOST.

When is the next RIVALS code?

A new FREE code appears on the lobby board every 50,000 likes, and update codes usually arrive with big updates like Season 4 on November 13 at 9:00 PM ET.

Do RIVALS promo codes expire?

Yes. Board codes like FREE206 stop working when the board moves to the next number, and event codes end without warning, so redeem them soon.
