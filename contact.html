Contact Roblox Guides

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Roblox Guides is the written site for the Fridge YouTube channel. The public way to reach the operator is through that channel. We do not publish a personal email address on this website.

YouTube is the current contact method

Please use the Fridge channel to get in touch:

  • Channel: youtube.com/@Fridgetherealone
  • Subscribe or open the channel: Subscribe on YouTube

The most useful public options are a comment on a relevant video or the channel’s YouTube contact features (including the About / business inquiry options YouTube provides). Tell us which page you are writing about and what you need. Please do not post your Roblox password, cookie, backup codes or other login secrets in a comment or message.

What to contact us about

This address is appropriate for questions about a guide, a correction (for example, a code that no longer works), a privacy or advertising request described in the Privacy Policy, or a question about these pages. We also welcome feedback on missing games or unclear steps.

We cannot reset a Roblox account, restore Robux, or override a game ban. For those issues, use official Roblox Support. We also cannot provide private “undetected” tools or downloads.

Email

There is no public email address listed on Roblox Guides at this time, and we have not added a mailto: link. A dedicated site email is recommended for privacy requests and for publisher programs, and it will be added here if and when the operator publishes one.
