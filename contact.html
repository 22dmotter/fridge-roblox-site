Contact Roblox Guides

Last updated: November 12, 2026

The primary way to reach Roblox Guides is by email at blazingblox49@gmail.com. The Fridge YouTube channel is a secondary public contact method.

Email (primary contact)

Write to blazingblox49@gmail.com for questions about a guide, a correction (for example, a code that no longer works), a privacy or advertising request described in the Privacy Policy, or other feedback about this site. Tell us which page you are writing about and what you need. Please do not send your Roblox password, cookie, backup codes or other login secrets.

What to contact us about

Email is the best path for corrections, privacy or advertising requests, and questions about these pages. We also welcome feedback on missing games or unclear steps.

We cannot reset a Roblox account, restore Robux, or override a game ban. For those issues, use official Roblox Support. We also cannot provide private “undetected” tools or downloads.
