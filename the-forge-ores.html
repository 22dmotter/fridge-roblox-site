The Forge Ores: Stats and Locations

Last checked: November 15, 2026

This list covers every ore we could confirm with two or more sources across World 1 (Stonewake's Cross), World 2 (Forgotten Kingdom), Goblin Cave, World 3 (Frostspire Expanse), The Peak, Raven Cave, Bamboo Forest, and the Holy Tree, in official Forge Wiki list order. Every stat on these pages was checked against at least two sources on November 15, 2026. A dash (—) means we did not find two sources that agree.

World 1World 2Goblin CaveWorld 3The PeakRaven CaveBamboo ForestHoly Tree
114 confirmed ores: This list has the full Stonewake's Cross set (14), the Forgotten Kingdom set from Cobalt through Darkryte (25), Goblin Cave (7), the Frostspire Expanse set from Grass through Prismatic Heart (16), The Peak (17), Raven Cave (8), Bamboo Forest (12), and the Holy Tree (15). Royal Boneite and Shikanite appear on the official wiki list but could not be confirmed with a second independent source, so they are not on this site yet. The same is true of later official areas (Jade Spirit Cave, Aurelia Hall, Void Cave, Tower of Hell) and the Special event ores Pumpkinite, Candy Ore, and Bismuth. For most Peak and Raven Cave ores, and for many World 3 ores, sources disagree on drop chance and sometimes multiplier, so those fields are dashes.

Sources

Item pages cite the sources used for each ore. The list was checked against:

  • Forge Wiki: Ores list and the individual pages for the confirmed ores in this list
  • Game8: List of All Ores and Traits (updated February 24, 2026)
  • Destructoid: All Ores in The Forge (updated January 8, 2026)
  • BloxInformer: The Forge ores (updated September 7, 2026)
  • Pocket Tactics: All The Forge ores (updated March 3, 2026)

Destructoid's tables (updated January 8, 2026) go through Raven Cave and are not used as a confirming source for Bamboo Forest or Holy Tree ores, or for World 1–2 ores they omit (Fichillium, Boneite, Dark Boneite, Slimite, Volcanic Rock Ore, and Grass). Game8 (February 24, 2026), BloxInformer (September 7, 2026), and Pocket Tactics (March 3, 2026) cover Goblin Cave through the Holy Tree, but not later official areas. For most World 3, Peak, and Raven Cave ores, the official wiki and BloxInformer list lower drop-chance denominators (and often lower multipliers) than Game8, Destructoid, and Pocket Tactics; those fields are dashes where two source camps disagree. Holy Tree sell prices are dashes because BloxInformer and Pocket Tactics list them as TBA. Pro Game Guides published an ores guide dated January 13, 2026, but the live page returned HTTP 403 when we tried to re-fetch it on November 15, 2026, so it is not used as a confirming source here.
