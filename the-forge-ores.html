The Forge Ores: Stats and Locations

Last checked: November 14, 2026

This list covers every ore in World 1 (Stonewake's Cross), in the official Forge Wiki list order from Stone through Fichillium. Every stat on these pages was checked against at least two sources on November 14, 2026. A dash (—) means we did not find two sources that agree. Later worlds are not included yet.

World 1 complete: These 14 ores are the full Stonewake's Cross set from the official Forge Wiki list, including Fichillium, a Relic Lucky Block ore. Forgotten Kingdom and later areas are not on this list yet.

Sources

Item pages cite the sources used for each ore. The list was checked against:

  • Forge Wiki: Ores list and the individual pages for all 14 Stonewake's Cross ores
  • Game8: List of All Ores and Traits and the Game8 Gold page (updated February 22, 2026)

Destructoid's World 1 table (updated January 8, 2026) covers Stone through Poopite but does not include Fichillium, so it is not used as a confirming source for that ore. Pro Game Guides published an ores guide dated January 13, 2026 whose visible snippets matched Stone, Sand Stone, Copper and Iron, but the live page returned HTTP 403 when we tried to re-fetch it on November 14, 2026, so it is not used as a confirming source here.
