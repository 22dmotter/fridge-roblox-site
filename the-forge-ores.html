The Forge Ores: Stats and Locations

Last checked: November 14, 2026

This list covers every ore in World 1 (Stonewake's Cross), World 2 (Forgotten Kingdom), and World 3 (Frostspire Expanse), in the official Forge Wiki list order from Stone through Fichillium, then Cobalt through Darkryte, then Grass through Prismatic Heart. Every stat on these pages was checked against at least two sources on November 14, 2026. A dash (—) means we did not find two sources that agree. Later official areas such as The Peak are not included yet.

Worlds 1–3: These 55 ores are the full Stonewake's Cross set (14), the Forgotten Kingdom set from Cobalt through Darkryte (25), and the Frostspire Expanse set from Grass through Prismatic Heart (16) on the official Forge Wiki list. Royal Boneite appears on the World 2 list but could not be confirmed with a second independent source, so it is not on this site yet. For most World 3 ores, the official wiki drop chance does not match Game8, Destructoid, and BloxInformer, so those chance fields are dashes. Later official areas such as The Peak are not included yet.

Sources

Item pages cite the sources used for each ore. The list was checked against:

  • Forge Wiki: Ores list and the individual pages for all 14 Stonewake's Cross ores, the 25 Forgotten Kingdom ores from Cobalt through Darkryte, and the 16 Frostspire Expanse ores from Grass through Prismatic Heart
  • Game8: List of All Ores and Traits and the matching Game8 item pages (updated February 22, 2026; the list page itself shows a February 24, 2026 update)
  • Destructoid: All Ores in The Forge (updated January 8, 2026)
  • BloxInformer: The Forge ores

Destructoid's tables (updated January 8, 2026) cover Stone through Poopite in World 1, most Forgotten Kingdom rock ores, and most Frostspire Expanse ores, but do not include Fichillium, Boneite, Dark Boneite, Slimite, Volcanic Rock Ore, or Grass, so Destructoid is not used as a confirming source for those ores. BloxInformer's Frostspire table also omits Grass. For most World 3 ores, the official wiki drop chance is a lower denominator than the chance listed by Game8, Destructoid, and BloxInformer; those chance fields are dashes on the World 3 pages. Destructoid also lists higher World 3 multipliers than the official wiki, Game8, and BloxInformer, which agree with each other. Pro Game Guides published an ores guide dated January 13, 2026 whose visible snippets matched Stone, Sand Stone, Copper and Iron, but the live page returned HTTP 403 when we tried to re-fetch it on November 14, 2026, so it is not used as a confirming source here.
