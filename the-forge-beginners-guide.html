The Forge Beginner Guide: How to Mine, Forge and Progress Fast

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

New to The Forge on Roblox? This The Forge beginner guide covers the whole early game: how the mine, forge and sell loop works, which quests to do first, which pickaxes to buy, and when you're ready for the next island. It pairs Fridge's full tutorial with his beginner money guide.

How The Forge Works

The Forge is an open-world mining and forging RPG. The core loop never changes:

  1. Mine rocks with your pickaxe to collect ores.
  2. Forge those ores into weapons and armor at the Forge in the island hub.
  3. Fight enemies in the caves for essences, runes and quest progress.
  4. Sell forged gear for Gold, then buy a better pickaxe so you can mine rarer rocks.

Every pickaxe has four stats, according to the Forge Wiki: Mine Power (damage per swing), Mine Speed, Luck Boost (better odds of rare ores) and Rune Slots. Some rocks need a minimum Mine Power before you can break them at all.

Your First Hour in Stonewake's Cross

You start on Stonewake's Cross and can't leave until you've finished the tutorial questline from Sensei Moro. His quests teach each system one at a time. For example, Learning to Forge! asks you to forge two weapons. Everything you need is in the hub:

  • The Forge: where you turn ores into weapons and armor.
  • Miner Fred: sells pickaxes.
  • Enhancer: upgrades gear with essences and Gold.
  • Runemaker: sockets runes into gear and pickaxes.
  • Maria's Potion Shop: temporary buffs like Luck and Miner potions.
  • Greedy Cey: buys ores, runes and essences.
  • Marbles' Weapon Shop: buys the weapons and armor you forge.
  • Wizard's Tower: race rerolls, up the hill to the left.

Open the Mailbox from the letter icon at the top right and claim everything in it. The wiki recommends this for every new player, and it's also where you redeem The Forge codes.

Which Pickaxes to Buy First

The Forge Wiki's pickaxe order starts with the Iron Pickaxe from the World 1 shop, then the Stonewake's Pickaxe. That one is stuck in stone in the Inner Cave and costs 3,333 Gold. It has 33 Mine Power, -30% speed, +10% luck and 3 rune slots, and Pro Game Guides calls it the best pickaxe on Island 1. They also suggest skipping the Platinum Pickaxe. After that, the wiki route goes Cobalt, Mythril and Lightite on Island 2. Our pickaxe progression guide lists the full route.

Early Money Tips

  • Sell forged gear, not raw ores. Ores are worth more once they're forged into an item. Pro Game Guides advises against selling ores to Greedy Cey.
  • Do repeatable quests. Umut the Brave and Nord in the Stonewake cave give repeatable money and XP, and the wiki lists them as high priority on World 1.
  • Skip potions early. Save Gold for pickaxes first.

Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

Every The Forge beginner guide warns about the same traps. These are the ones that cost the most time:

  • Putting runes on gear you still plan to enhance. A failed enhancement can cost a rune slot and the rune in it, Pro Game Guides warns.
  • Spending Demonite before Island 3. The Ice Man's quest there asks for it.
  • Rerolling your race with a Luck Potion active. Luck buffs don't affect race rerolls.
  • Rushing the Goblin Cave. The wiki rates it low priority compared with buying better pickaxes.

The Forge FAQ

What should I do first in The Forge?

Follow Sensei Moro's tutorial quests, claim your Mailbox rewards, and buy the Stonewake's Pickaxe as soon as you have 3,333 Gold.

What level do you need for the Forgotten Kingdom?

Level 10, after finishing Sensei Moro's Stonewake's Cross questline. Then use the Wizard's portal.

Is The Forge free to play?

Yes. Gamepasses like Fast Forge and Double Storage are optional, and codes give free race rerolls.
