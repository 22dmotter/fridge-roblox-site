How to Unlock the Goblin Cave in The Forge: Every Goblin King Quest

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

The Goblin Cave is a crystal cave on the Forgotten Kingdom, locked behind the Goblin King's questline. Fridge's full tutorial shows the route. Here's how to unlock the Goblin Cave: every quest step, what it costs, and what's inside, as listed on the Forge Wiki on November 11, 2026.

Watch Fridge's Goblin Cave Tutorial

Fridge covered this topic in 1 full video uploaded on December 17, 2025. They are all embedded here, so you can watch and follow the written steps side by side.

Video date check: The Forge gets updates often. The newest is version 1.09, Halloween & Guilds, from October 20, 2026. Fridge's videos show the game as it was when he filmed them. The written steps on this page match the game as of November 11, 2026, and were checked against the Forge Wiki.

Where the Goblin King Is

From the Forgotten Kingdom spawn, don't take the path up to the Ruined Cave. Keep going down the path until you see two goblins guarding the Goblin Village. The Goblin King lives there. Pro Game Guides notes that the guards won't let Zombie-race players in, but any other race can enter.

Every Goblin King Quest

Goblin King questline (Forge Wiki)
QuestBringReward (XP)
Goblin King 15,000 Gold1,000
Goblin King 23 Topaz + 10,000 Gold1,500
Goblin King 35 Diamond + 15,000 Gold2,500
Goblin King 45 Emerald + 20,000 Gold4,500
Let's get over it!Final payment7,500

That's 50,000 Gold across the first four quests. Allthings.how puts the total for the whole questline at 85,000 Gold, including the final step. When you finish, the Goblin King gives you the key to the Goblin Cave.

How to Unlock the Cave

To unlock the Goblin Cave once you've saved up:

  1. Mine Topaz, Diamond and Emerald in the Ruined Cave while you save Gold.
  2. Hand in each Goblin King quest in order.
  3. Collect the Goblin Cave key after the last payment.
  4. Use it on the cave entrance to the right of the Goblin King.

What's Inside the Goblin Cave

The Forge Wiki describes it as a dangerous cavern full of slimes and rare crystals. Its crystal ores include Blue, Crimson, Green, Magenta and Orange Crystal Ore (Epic), Rainbow Crystal (Legendary) and Arcane Crystal (Mythic). Arcane Crystal is a key crafting ingredient for Heavenite and Stolen Heart. Bring a strong pickaxe: Pro Game Guides suggests Mythril, Lightite, Magma, Arcane or Demonic.

When Fridge made his video in December 2025, Galaxite could also drop here at 1 in 1,000,000. It's now crafted in the Void Cave instead. See our Galaxite guide.

Is the Goblin Cave Worth It?

Not early on. The Forge Wiki's quest priority list rates the Goblin King quest low priority, and Pro Game Guides says to buy better pickaxes first. Do it once you have spare Gold and want crystal ores for crafting. Island 3 has a sequel questline from the Goblin Lord. See the Goblin Lord quest guide.

The Forge FAQ

How do you unlock the Goblin Cave in The Forge?

Complete the Goblin King's questline in the Forgotten Kingdom's Goblin Village, then use the key he gives you on the cave entrance.

How much does the Goblin Cave cost?

At least 50,000 Gold plus 3 Topaz, 5 Diamond and 5 Emerald across the first four quests. Allthings.how puts the full questline at 85,000 Gold.

Can Zombies enter the Goblin Village?

No. Pro Game Guides reports that the goblin guards turn away Zombie-race players.
