How to Get to the Volcanic Depths in The Forge and Farm Darkryte and Demonite

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

The Volcanic Depths is the high-level lava zone under the Forgotten Kingdom, and it's home to Darkryte and Demonite. Fridge has a video on getting there and another on farming Volcanic Rocks. Here's the route and the current drop rates.

Watch Fridge's Volcanic Depths Videos

Fridge covered this topic in 2 full videos uploaded between December 16, 2025 and December 18, 2025. They are all embedded here, so you can watch and follow the written steps side by side.

Video date check: The Forge gets updates often. The newest is version 1.09, Halloween & Guilds, from October 20, 2026. Fridge's videos show the game as it was when he filmed them. The written steps on this page match the game as of November 11, 2026, and were checked against the Forge Wiki.

How to Get to the Volcanic Depths

  1. Teleport to the Forgotten Kingdom (Island 2, level 10).
  2. Head into the Ruined Cave. The Cannon near spawn launches you to its entrance.
  3. Inside, take the right-hand path marked by a wooden DANGER sign, into the Ashen Passage.
  4. Keep to the right wall until you reach the orange, spiky rocks near Amber, who stands by the Magma Pickaxe.
  5. Break the two Basalt Veins blocking a hole in the floor. Gamezebo puts them at about 2,750 HP.
  6. Drop down the hole into the Volcanic Depths.

This route was checked against Gamezebo and Pro Game Guides, and the area names match the Forge Wiki.

What's Down There

The Forge Wiki calls it a high-level zone full of lava pools, magma walls and volcanic versions of enemies. Watch out for the Reaper, which drops the Drain Edge rune, the Elite Deathaxe Skeleton and the Blight Pyromancer. Use block and parry, covered in our combat guide. At the far back is Skal, the demon in Demon's Hideout. Bring him 3 Demonite for the Demonite Key, which opens the gate to the Demonic Pickaxe (200 Mine Power, +53% Luck).

Farming Volcanic Rocks

Volcanic Rock (Forge Wiki)
StatValue
Health3,680 (the highest on the island)
Required damage100 Mine Power
Ores per rock3–4
Luck multiplier9x
XP45

Drops include Topaz, Cuprite, Rivalite, Obsidian, Eye Ore, Fireite, Magmaite, Demonite and Darkryte. For speed, use a pickaxe well above 100 Mine Power, rune it for Swift Mining and Mine Power, and drink a Luck Potion during a Fortune event.

Darkryte and Demonite

  • Demonite (Mythic): about a 1 in 3,666 chance, 5.5x multiplier. On armor, it adds Burn and Backfire. You can also craft it from 1 Magmaite, 1 Uranium and 1 Fireite for 500 Gold.
  • Darkryte (Mythic): about a 1 in 6,655 chance, 6.3x multiplier. On armor, it gives a 15% Phantom Step dodge. You can craft it from 1 Demonite and 1 Magmaite for 500 Gold.

The crafting recipes mean you don't have to rely only on lucky drops. Turn them into armor with our armor guide. Keep at least 1 Demonite for the Ice Man's quest on Island 3.

The Forge FAQ

How do you get to the Volcanic Depths in The Forge?

In the Forgotten Kingdom's Ruined Cave, follow the DANGER sign to the right, break the two Basalt Veins near Amber, and drop down the hole.

What pickaxe do you need for Volcanic Rocks?

At least 100 Mine Power, according to the Forge Wiki.

What is the Darkryte drop chance in The Forge?

About 1 in 6,655 from Volcanic Rocks. You can also craft it from Demonite and Magmaite.
