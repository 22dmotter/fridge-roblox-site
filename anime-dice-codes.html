How to Redeem Codes in Anime Dice

  1. Open Anime Dice on Roblox. If you're brand new, finish the short tutorial first.
  2. Click the Shop button on the left side of the screen.
  3. Scroll to the Codes section at the bottom of the Shop menu.
  4. Paste a code into the "Enter Code" box. Copy it exactly from the table above.
  5. Press Redeem and your rewards drop straight into your inventory.

Each code works once per account, and there's no level requirement listed for any of them. Fridge shows the full redeem process in the video below.

What Anime Dice Code Rewards Do

Trait Rerolls

Trait Rerolls are the most valuable reward on this list. Traits change how strong a character performs, and a good trait on your best unit can matter more than rolling a brand-new character. Save your rerolls for the units you actually plan to keep, not your starter characters.

Lucky Spins

Lucky Spins boost your odds on a roll. Here's the trick: spend them after your next dice upgrade, not right away. Lucky Spins stack with your existing Luck, so using them when your Luck is higher gives you a better shot at rare characters.

Gems and Tickets

Gems are a premium currency used across the shop, while Tickets show up on some older update codes. Hold onto Gems for limited-time offers rather than spending them the moment they land.

Leaves, Jackpots and Potions

The autumn codes hand out Leaves (event currency), a Harvest Jackpot and Luck-style potions. Spend the Leaves during the event, because event currencies usually lose their value once the season ends.

Tips to Get More Out of Your Free Rerolls

  • Redeem every code on day one. Codes tied to like and player-count goals (like 400KLIKES or 100KCCU) can expire without warning.
  • Pick one main team. Spreading Trait Rerolls across a dozen units leaves all of them weak.
  • Stack Luck before rolling. Upgrade your dice and use potions, then use Lucky Spins.
  • Like and favorite the game. The KLIKES codes exist because of like milestones, so more likes usually means more codes.

How to Get New Codes First

The developers release codes when the game hits like and concurrent-player milestones and with each numbered update, which is why so many codes are named UPDATE or KCCU.
