How to Delete a Roblox Account Permanently (2026 Official Steps)

By Fridge · Published November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 10, 2026

Before you delete: Deletion is permanent. Your username, Robux, items, purchases, friends and creations are gone and can't be restored. Roblox Support can't undo it. If you just want a break, deactivate instead. Also, never pay or give your login to a site or person offering to delete an account for you; deletion only happens through Roblox.

This guide explains how to delete a Roblox account using Roblox's official tools, plus the reversible option (deactivation), what happens to your Robux and items, and what to do if you can't sign in. Steps are based on Roblox Support's own deletion article.

How to Delete a Roblox Account (Official Steps)

Here is the official way to do it from the settings menu. It works on the website and in the Roblox app.

  1. Log in to Roblox on the website or app.
  2. Open Settings (the gear icon).
  3. Go to Privacy & content restrictions.
  4. Find Account data, deactivation & deletion (menu names can change slightly over time).
  5. Choose Delete account and read the warnings.
  6. Complete the verification Roblox asks for. Roblox Support says you need a verified email, and you may need to complete identity verification.
  7. Confirm. Roblox then processes the deletion.
Tip: If you don't see the delete option, make sure your email is verified first. Accounts with parental controls may need a linked parent to manage this.

How to Deactivate Instead

  1. Follow steps 1–4 above.
  2. Choose Deactivate account.
  3. Confirm. To come back later, just log in again.

Delete Your Roblox Account Without Logging In

If you can't access the account, first try to get back in with our Roblox account recovery guide. If that's not possible, Roblox Support lets you submit a data privacy request through its official contact form at roblox.com/support (choose the data privacy option and the "right to be forgotten" / deletion request). Use the email tied to the account so Roblox can confirm you own it.

Request a Copy of Your Data First

Want to keep a record of your account? Roblox lets you request a copy of your account data (a "right to access" request) through the same Account data area or the support form. Do this before deleting.

Pre-Deletion Checklist

  • Cancel Roblox Premium and any subscriptions so you aren't billed again. Subscriptions bought through the App Store or Google Play must be cancelled there.
  • Spend or accept losing your Robux. Robux aren't refunded when you delete.
  • Save your creations. Download any places or models you built in Studio.
  • Tell your friends or groups if you manage any, and hand over group ownership if needed.
  • Close alt accounts separately; each account must be deleted on its own (see our multiple Roblox accounts guide for the Account Switcher, which makes switching between them easy).

For Parents: Deleting a Child's Account

If you're a parent, you can manage much of your child's account through Roblox's parental controls, including spending limits, chat and who can contact them. Linking a parent account first often solves the problem without deleting anything. If you still want the account removed, sign in to it and follow the steps above, or contact Roblox Support with proof that you're the parent or guardian. Make sure you know the account's email and username before you start, since Roblox needs to confirm ownership.

Starting Fresh Instead?

Some players delete accounts just to get a new username. Roblox lets you change your username for Robux in account settings, which keeps your items and friends. If you want a second account alongside your main, you don't need to delete anything either.

Just Want More Privacy?

If the real problem is unwanted contact, you might not need to delete anything. Roblox's Privacy settings let you limit who can message you, chat with you and join you. Our join someone on Roblox guide explains the "who can see and join you" setting, and you can block or report any user. Taking a break? Deactivation, or simply logging out, works too; there's no need to AFK in Roblox to keep your account active.

Roblox FAQ

How do I permanently delete my Roblox account?

Go to Settings, Privacy & content restrictions, then Account data, deactivation & deletion, and choose Delete account. You need a verified email and may need to verify your identity.

Can I get my Roblox account back after deleting it?

No. Deletion is permanent. If you might come back, deactivate the account instead.

How do I delete a Roblox account without logging in?

Try official account recovery first. If you still can't access it, submit a data privacy deletion request through Roblox's support form using the account's email.

Do I get my Robux back if I delete my Roblox account?

No. Robux, items and purchases are lost when an account is deleted. Cancel Premium and subscriptions first.
