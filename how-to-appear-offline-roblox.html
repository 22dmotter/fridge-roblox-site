How to Appear Offline on Roblox in 2026 (PC, Mobile and Console)

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Here's how to appear offline on Roblox in 2026. Roblox doesn't have a one-tap "invisible" button, but its privacy settings do the same job. When you set Online Status to No one, anyone outside that setting sees you as offline on your profile and in their Friends list. Pair it with hiding your current game, and you can play in peace.

How to Appear Offline on Roblox (Step by Step)

These steps come from Roblox Support's Online Status and Visibility article.

  1. Open Settings. On desktop, click the gear icon at the top right. On mobile, tap More (bottom right) and then the gear icon.
  2. Go to Privacy & content restrictions.
  3. Open Visibility & private servers.
  4. Open Visibility.

Set Online Status to "No one"

Under Online Status, choose who can see that you're online. Depending on your age and region, options include Friends, Trusted friends, Followers, People you follow, Everyone and No one.

Online Status options
SettingWho sees you online
No oneNobody; you appear offline to everyone
Trusted friendsOnly people you've added as Trusted Friends
FriendsAll your friends
EveryoneAnyone on Roblox (where available)

A good middle ground is Trusted friends: your closest friends still see you, everyone else doesn't. Learn how to set them up in our how to add Trusted Friends on Roblox guide.

Hide the Game You're Playing

In the same Visibility menu, change Show current experience. Anyone outside your choice won't see which game you're in, and this setting also controls who can join you from your profile. Our join someone on Roblox guide explains how this affects joining.

Note: Roblox says restricting some visibility settings can also restrict related ones. If you set Online Status to No one, check what happened to Show current experience too.

Turn Off Activity Updates

Roblox can tell your friends about your recent in-game activity. Turn off the Share activity updates toggle in the same area so friends don't get updates about what you've been playing.

How to Hide Your Online Status on Roblox Mobile, Xbox and PlayStation

  • Mobile: tap More → Settings → Privacy & content restrictions → Visibility & private servers → Visibility.
  • Xbox and PlayStation: privacy settings are tied to your Roblox account. If the console menu doesn't show the option, change it once on the Roblox website or mobile app and it applies everywhere.
  • Console platform status: your Xbox or PlayStation network may still show you online to platform friends; that's controlled in the console's own privacy settings.

What Appearing Offline Doesn't Hide

  • Players in the same server can still see you in the player list.
  • Messages and friend requests still arrive based on your other privacy settings.
  • Your avatar and profile stay visible.

For Parents

For children, a linked parent can set Online status and Show current game to Friends, Trusted friends or No one from parental controls. See our Roblox parental controls guide. If you want to talk with friends while staying hidden from everyone else, voice works normally; here's how to turn on voice chat on Roblox.

How to Show as Online Again

Changed your mind? Go back to Settings → Privacy & content restrictions → Visibility & private servers → Visibility and set Online Status back to Friends (or whichever group you prefer). Do the same for Show current experience if you want friends to be able to join you from your profile again. Changes apply to your account, so they carry over to every device you sign in on.

Quick Privacy Tips

  • Use Trusted friends instead of No one if you still want a couple of close friends to find you.
  • Remember that joining a friend's game still shows you in that server.
  • Recheck your visibility settings after big Roblox updates, since menus sometimes move.

Roblox FAQ

Can you appear offline on Roblox?

Yes. Go to Settings > Privacy & content restrictions > Visibility & private servers > Visibility and set Online Status to No one. Others will see you as offline.

How do I hide what game I'm playing on Roblox?

In the same Visibility menu, change Show current experience to No one or a smaller group like Trusted friends.

Can people in my server see me if I appear offline?

Yes. Appearing offline only hides your status on profiles and Friends lists; players in the same server still see you.

How do I appear offline on Roblox mobile?

Tap More, then Settings, then Privacy & content restrictions > Visibility & private servers > Visibility, and set Online Status to No one.

Does appearing offline on Roblox stop friends from joining me?

Yes, if you also restrict Show current experience. That setting controls who can see your game and join you from your profile.
