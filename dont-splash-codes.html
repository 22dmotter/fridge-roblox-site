DONT SPLASH Codes (November 2026): What Works and What Doesn't

By Fridge · Published November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Looking for DONT SPLASH codes? As of last checked November 11, 2026, no code is confirmed by two sources. One tracker reports a single code, Bathhouse, from the November 8 Bathhouse update, but another site checking the official description daily still found none on November 10. The popular ILOVEWATER code belongs to a different game. DONT SPLASH by Bacon Theory launched on October 17, 2026, and it was the #17 most-played game on Roblox on November 11, with about 61,000 players online.

The One Reported Code: Bathhouse

Unconfirmed code
CodeReported rewardWhat sources say
Bathhouse100 CoinsBloxron (Nov 8) lists it as active with an in-game screenshot of a Shop Codes panel. BloxStatus (Nov 10) found no working codes in the official description.

It's free to try. Bloxron says to open SHOP on the left side of the screen, stay on the Codes panel, paste the code into ENTER CODE and tap REDEEM. Bloxron also says to match the capitalization exactly. If it works for you, it's 100 Coins; if not, you haven't lost anything.

Why ILOVEWATER Doesn't Work

Many DONT SPLASH code lists show ILOVEWATER. According to dontsplash.com and Bloxron, that code belongs to Dont Spill the Water!, a separate party game whose description says "USE CODE: ILOVEWATER." It gives that game's Bux currency, and DONT SPLASH uses Coins. Codes from other games never redeem in DONT SPLASH.

Expired codes

No expired DONT SPLASH codes have been recorded. ILOVEWATER isn't expired, it's just from the wrong game.

How to Earn Free Coins Without Codes

  • Claim the welcome reward. Bloxron says liking the game and joining the developer's group lets you claim +200 Coins and a free Self-Revive.
  • Finish Easy runs for steady Coins while you learn the rooms.
  • Buy useful gear first. Bloxron recommends Throwing Range or Pebble Skipping early, and Rescue Rope (250 Coins) if you play co-op, before cosmetics.
  • Keep your frame rate up. Runs punish stutters, so see our fix Roblox lag guide.

When to Check Again

BloxStatus counted 9 updates between October 26 and November 7, so DONT SPLASH updates almost daily, and codes in new games usually arrive with updates. We'll move Bathhouse or any new code into the confirmed table as soon as a second source or the official description confirms it.

Want confirmed freebies today? Survive Your Ideas codes give 45 free spins, and our RIVALS codes give free Keys. Like Throw Food For Animals, DONT SPLASH is a new hit that hasn't settled on a code system yet.

What Is DONT SPLASH?

DONT SPLASH is a runner and obby game inspired by the eerie Poolrooms and House of the Locust, according to its description quoted by BloxStatus. Servers hold up to 10 players. It exploded after launch, peaking at nearly 140,000 players on October 31, 2026, and it still holds tens of thousands of players every day.

Quick Checklist

  1. Claim the welcome reward first.
  2. Try Bathhouse in the Shop's Codes panel, typed exactly.
  3. Ignore ILOVEWATER and codes from other DON'T SPLASH copies.

DONT SPLASH FAQ

Are there any DONT SPLASH codes?

None are confirmed by two sources as of November 11, 2026. One tracker reports Bathhouse for 100 Coins.

Does ILOVEWATER work in DONT SPLASH?

No. ILOVEWATER belongs to Dont Spill the Water!, a different Roblox game.

Where do you redeem DONT SPLASH promo codes?

Bloxron reports a Codes panel inside the SHOP on the left side of the screen: paste the code into ENTER CODE and tap REDEEM.

How do I get free Coins in DONT SPLASH?

Claim the welcome reward by liking the game and joining the developer's group, then finish runs for more Coins.

Who made DONT SPLASH?

The Roblox game page lists Bacon Theory as the creator. It launched on October 17, 2026.

Does DONT SPLASH have a codes menu?

Bloxron reports a Codes panel in the Shop, but BloxStatus hadn't confirmed any code there as of November 10, 2026.

Is DONT SPLASH the same as Dont Spill the Water?

No. They're different Roblox games by different creators, and their codes don't carry over.

How many players can join a DONT SPLASH server?

Up to 10 players per server, according to BloxStatus.
